# Brand Context

Always-loaded brand facts. If this conflicts with current repo evidence, fix this
file; do not build a second brand system beside it.

## Brand

- **Corporate/category frame:** Agentic Economy / AE.
- **Consumer name:** pending Phase 0. R2 recommends **Cinch**; **Handled** is fallback.
- **Dead prior direction:** Aeco. Do not treat it as locked, public, or strategic.
- **Category:** the action layer of the agentic economy for households.
- **Promise:** **Ask once. It gets sorted.**
- **Today’s shipped rung:** qualified inquiry in writing + one record the household keeps.
- **Destination:** book, charge, confirm — stated as trajectory/internal founder ambition until those paths ship.

## Audience

- **Primary:** AU households stuck after AI search or Google gives them a list.
- **Mirror:** local business owners who want clear inquiries direct, in writing, with no lead fees and no resold leads.
- **Enemy:** the halfway — ten tabs, phone tag, quote chaos, “knows a guy,” chasing replies, nobody accountable.
- **Language:** “sorted,” “phone tag,” “one ask,” “in writing,” “own your page,” “no lead fees.”

## Positioning

AE is not a smarter list, a quote marketplace, or a trust badge. It is where the
ask starts moving: one written approach to a business, one durable record, then
the loop expands rung by rung.

The moat is the named enemy, the owner economics, the record, and the receipt /
evidence plumbing underneath — not a claim to have invented acting AI.

## Voice

- **Personality:** visionary, kinetic, unflinching, exact, first-mover.
- **Register:** swagger with receipts.
- **Rule:** boundaries are product facts, not brand posture. State them once where needed; never chant them.
- **Banned feel:** warm marketplace mush, hedging, AI-slop futurism, economist language.

## Values

Momentum over hedging. Written record over hearsay. Owner economics in the open.
Household-real language. Ambition without fabricated capability.

## Goals

1. Own the action layer before Google, OpenAI, Perplexity, or legacy quote marketplaces attach a brand to it.
2. Clear the final consumer name and Slipstream mark.
3. Prove hero → registry → listing → inquiry with enough real supply to make the promise feel alive.
4. Measure sent asks, retained records, owner claims, reply readiness, and name recall.
