## Position (locked)

**The decomposition thesis.** The hardest moment of any big undertaking is not doing it — it is not
knowing where to start. Big things decompose into facets; facets branch into decisions, comparisons,
options, pricing, timing. People stall at that wall. **Agentic Economy cuts through it: say the big
thing, and your agent breaks it into the decisions that matter — in the order they matter — finds the
businesses, compares real quotes, and gets it done, receipts and all.**

**Provenance (internal, never in copy):** this commoditizes the founder's own craft — project
management and consulting. See a big problem, decompose it into small manageable tasks, rank by
stakeholder, critical path, cost, scope, then drive it. People go to AI to help them plan; AI gives
them a list. AE gives them a managed, priced, sequenced project that runs. People know they need a
project manager and do not want to be one — and AE is more than a PM or consultant: it does not
advise, it takes the thing on. **The words "project manager", "PM", "critical path", "scope",
"stakeholder" are PROHIBITED in public copy.** The judgment shows through effect-words only ("the
decisions that matter, in the order they matter").

- **Soul:** the decided path. Overwhelm in; a structured, decided, moving plan out. Commerce is the
  machinery underneath.
- **The leap we never ask:** trusting an agent to act on your behalf as the entry point. The on-ramp is
  the decided path (planning and comparing — things people already do); acting graduates opt-in from
  inside the plan, one approval at a time.
- **One product, two truths:** for people, a new way to take on big things and get them done; for
  agents, the infrastructure that makes those plans executable (published businesses, priced
  capabilities, callable actions).
- **One brand:** Agentic Economy, on all three doors. Person-side warmth comes from the copy, not a
  sub-brand.
- **Category:** agentic commerce for services and outcomes (not goods checkout, not tool/data supply,
  not a directory).
- **Audience order:** person → agent → business.
- **Enemy:** the moment itself — "where do we even start?" The hero names it; no extra villain lines.
- **Promise register:** the full destination (PROJECT.md), stated without hedging. Honesty lives at
  decision points (approvals, evidence, refusals), never as standing public qualification.

## Voice rules (binding)

1. The demonstration is the headline; no slogan-only posting. Mechanism and named supply over
   adjectives; category thesis demoted to secondary bands.
2. Instances are furniture, never the anchor. The hero names the universal moment; example asks carry
   the instances and are swappable in one file without a rebrand.
3. Example asks are familiar and problem-phrased — what a person actually says at the wall. No invented
   novelty niches; no vendor-search phrasing ("find a Y near me" — AE is not local hire, not a
   directory).
4. No "work" and no PM vocabulary on person-facing surfaces. Say: the big thing, decisions, done,
   sorted, moving, off your plate.
5. Invite the problem, not the specification. People arrive with a situation; the agent does the
   scoping. Never demand the person name a service category.
6. Destination-promise verbs on the doors ("gets it done, receipts and all"); capability limitations
   surface at the decision that needs them, not as hedges.
7. Live counters and named supply only from source-owned truth; no invented numbers.
8. Machine descriptors (`defineAction`, llms.txt, SKILL.md, UCP) are exactness-governed and are never
   restyled in a rebrand — semantic alignment only.
