# AE VOICE — 2026-07-05

The full verbal identity. Extends — does not replace — the ten-rule voice
contract in `MANIFESTO-MESSAGING-2026-07-04.md` §5. Those ten rules are load-
bearing; read them first. This document adds the next tier: voice qualities, the
words we own and ban, sentence rhythm, and a register matrix that shows how the
one voice flexes from manifesto to error state — with rewrites of real AE surface
copy at each register.

Posture (carried, re-scoped per `COUNCIL-CONVERGENCE-2026-07-05.md`): AE is an
**action brand**, and its internal conviction is the destination said in the
future tense — "we will book, we will charge, we will confirm" — the BONES §2
north star that fuels the build. That declaration is the **internal and founder
register**: it lives in BONES and in the founder's own voice, and it never lands
on a public or campaign surface (households read it cold as a threat to the bank
account, the calendar, and the promise the product can't yet back). The **public**
default register is the master-promise register: plain confidence in household
language, "show, don't announce" — the register of "Ask once. It gets sorted." On
product surfaces the shipped rung is stated as plain fact — a qualified inquiry, in
writing, and a record you keep — and the confidence becomes exactness and momentum,
not adjectives. Where a contract line is required today ("AE has not booked,
charged, or confirmed"), it is a fact stated once, and the line we exist to erase —
never personality, never apology.

---

## 01 — VOICE OVERVIEW

AE talks like someone useful already moving the work forward — certain, concrete,
and allergic to making the household carry the chase alone. Publicly, that
certainty is the master-promise register: the lived afternoon, the written ask,
the record you keep, and the exhale of *sorted*. Internally, the founder register
can hold the full future-tense destination; publicly, the voice earns belief by
showing the household's problem plainly and naming the next real step.

**Voice Essence:**
> *AE sounds like the competent person who knows where the chase usually breaks —
> and has started closing that gap in writing.*

---

## 02 — TONE DIMENSIONS

**Formality — Casual ←●———→ Formal.**
Plainspoken and human, never sloppy, never corporate. We write the way a sharp
friend talks, not the way a platform's legal team writes.
- *Do:* "Ten tabs, three quotes you can't line up, and voicemail."
- *Not:* "Users may experience friction across multiple discovery touchpoints."

**Energy — Calm ————●→ Energetic.**
Kinetic. Motion lives in the verbs. Momentum over throat-clearing.
- *Do:* "Ask once. It's sorted."
- *Not:* "To get started, you might consider submitting a request."

**Certainty — Tentative ————————● Certain.**
The far end — as plain confidence, not swagger. Publicly it reads as certainty
without a declaration: *"Ask once. It gets sorted,"* not *"we aim to."* The
future-tense *we will* declaration is the internal / founder register (posture note).
- *Do (public):* "Ask once. It gets sorted."
- *Not:* "We hope to eventually help facilitate some of these steps."

**Expertise — Accessible ●———→ Authoritative.**
Authoritative about the destination, accessible about the how. We know where this
goes; we explain the next step in words a household uses.
- *Do:* "We start where the list stops."
- *Not:* "Leveraging our proprietary agentic orchestration layer…"

**Warmth — Distant ———●——→ Intimate.**
Warm through recognition, not softness. We earn warmth by describing the
afternoon so exactly the reader feels seen — then we point forward.
- *Do:* "We've all lived that afternoon."
- *Not:* "We deeply care about your customer journey."

## 03 — VOICE QUALITIES

**1. Convinced.**
Public confidence comes through the promise and the next concrete step, not a
declaration of future capability. The transaction destination stays in the
internal / founder register until each rung ships.
- *Do:* "Ask once. It gets sorted."
- *Don't:* "We're on a mission to try to make things a little easier."

**2. Concrete.**
Nouns and verbs carry the weight. If an adjective is doing a verb's job, cut it.
- *Do:* "One ask out. One written record back."
- *Don't:* "A truly effortless, best-in-class inquiry experience."

**3. Adversarial (toward abandonment, never the household).**
We show where the help stops and refuse to leave the reader there. The jab is at
the list, the blinking cursor, the lead-fee racket — never at the person.
- *Do:* "You got the list. We help turn the ask into writing."
- *Don't:* "Other tools in this space also have certain limitations."

**4. Honest without hedging.**
We state exactly what has shipped, and we carry the destination without pretending
it has arrived. Truth and confidence are not in tension when the register is
clean: public copy promises the outcome; product copy names the shipped rung.
- *Do:* "Today it's one qualified inquiry, in writing, and a record you keep."
- *Don't:* "AE books your plumber for you." (not shipped — never claim it as live)
- *Don't:* "AE is just a simple way to send an enquiry." (shrinks the destination)

**5. Household-real.**
We speak the afternoon, not the market. Suburbs, kettles, group chats, tax time —
not "the domestic services sector."
- *Do:* "The hot water's gone and standup's at nine."
- *Don't:* "AE optimises matching efficiency across service verticals."

**6. Forward-pointing, even in a record.**
Receipts and confirmations read as momentum: state what happened, then point at
what's next. The swagger of a receipt is exactness.
- *Do:* "Your inquiry is written and sent. The business can reply with timing and
  quote."
- *Cut:* any version that turns the reply state into guarantee or warranty language.

## 04 — VOCABULARY

**Words we own publicly (use freely, on purpose):**
sorted · ask once · in writing · get found · no phone tag · no lead fees · own
your page · one ask · the record · a receipt you keep · close the distance ·
momentum · reach them · done.

**Brand-specific language:**
- **The halfway** — our name for the enemy: the list you get, then the
  abandonment. Use it as a proper noun.
- **Sorted** — the hero outcome; the household's exhale. The whole product in one
  word.
- **The record / a receipt you keep** — how we refer to the written trail. Trust
  is texture, so we name the artifact plainly, never with jargon.
- **The household** — who we serve, said warmly and specifically. Not "users,"
  not "consumers."
- **We will book, charge, and confirm** — the destination line, reserved for the
  internal / founder register (BONES §2 north star, founder posts). Never a public,
  campaign, or product surface, and never a live claim.

**Words we ban:**
- *Hollow hype:* unique, revolutionary, world-class, seamless, empower,
  game-changing, cutting-edge, next-gen, solutions (as a noun).
- *Corporate throat-clearing:* leverage, facilitate, aim to, hope to, strive to,
  "in order to," "in conclusion," "at the end of the day."
- *Banned by prior authorities:* "domestic trade" (economist framing);
  proof-ledger, business of record, "the honest way," proof desk, "published
  facts" (as a heading), source-owned, manifest/handoff (as public jargon),
  operator, capability (as public jargon).
- *Provider-quality claims we can't make:* best, top, trusted, vetted, good,
  five-star, guaranteed — no quality certification of a business.
- *Defensive drumbeat:* stacked "does not / cannot / will not" caveats. One
  contract line where required, never a chorus.
- *Fake-live capability:* any present-tense claim that AE books, charges, pays,
  dispatches, or confirms *today* on a product surface.

## 05 — SENTENCE RHYTHM & STYLE RULES

**Sentence length:** Short by default; kinetic. Vary a longer setup against a
short punch to land the turn. The two-beat command is the signature move: *"Ask
once. It's sorted."*

**Fragments:** Allowed, on purpose, for momentum. "Booked. In writing. Done." A
fragment is a drumbeat, not a mistake.

**Punctuation:** The em-dash is for the turn and the reveal — not for hedging.
Full stops do the heavy lifting; a period between two words is a beat. Exclamation
marks: essentially never (the conviction carries the energy; punctuation doesn't
need to shout). Ellipses only in a real prompt placeholder ("Need a plumber in
Marrickville…"), never to trail off timidly.

**Capitalization:** Sentence case everywhere, including headlines and the public
manifesto. The all-caps north-star / destination line is an **internal and
founder-register** device only (BONES §2, founder posts) — it never appears on a
public, campaign, or product surface. Even there, reserve it for that one line;
overuse kills it.

**Numbers:** Numerals for anything a household would read fast ("3 quotes," "10
tabs"). Spell out only when a number opens a sentence.

**Contractions:** Always. "It's sorted," "you'll have a receipt," "we're
building." Contractions are how humans talk.

**Voice (grammar):** Active, first-person plural. *We* do things. Passive voice is
a smell — it's usually a caveat or a dodge in disguise.

**Humor:** Dry and confident, aimed at the halfway, never the household. A wry jab
at the blinking cursor is on-brand; a joke at the reader's expense is not.

## 06 — REGISTER MATRIX

One voice, five registers. Conviction is constant; concreteness rises as you move
toward the product surface. Each register below shows an off-register version and
the on-register rewrite, anchored to real AE copy.

### Register A — INTERNAL / FOUNDER (conviction at full height)
*Where:* BONES §2, founder posts, internal rallying — the future-tense north star
that fuels the build. **Not** a public or campaign surface. The public manifesto
now lives in the promise register (below, and in the rewritten
`MANIFESTO-MESSAGING-2026-07-04.md` §1).

- **Off-register:** "Our mission is to help make finding local services a little
  easier and more efficient for everyone."
- **On-register (internal / founder):** "We are building the domestic agentic
  economy. We will book. We will charge. We will confirm." — the conviction, said
  to ourselves, in the founder's voice.
- **Public counterpart (promise register):** "Everyone knows the afternoon: ten
  tabs, 'the best businesses in your area,' then silence. An answer is not an
  outcome. That's the part we're building. Ask once. It gets sorted."
- **Why:** the internal conviction fuels the build; the public surface earns belief
  by showing the household's afternoon and making the promise — not by declaring war.

### Register B — CAMPAIGN (promise, out loud)
*Where:* homepage hero, taglines, ads, launch copy. Conviction meets the
household's afternoon; the line is short, verb-led, and plain enough to say to a
partner or a mate.

- **Real shipped line:** "Find a local business. Ask once."
- **Off-register alternative:** "The smart, easy way to connect with trusted local
  professionals near you."
- **On-register range (same energy, campaign flex):** "Ask once. It gets sorted."
  / "Say it once." / "From ask to done." — kept short, verb-led, plain promise
  register. The coined-jargon line was cut after households read it as a fun run,
  not a plumber app.
- **Why:** lands the exhale and the next motion in a breath; nothing depends on
  caps, coined jargon, or a capability we have not shipped.

### Register C — PRODUCT SURFACE (concrete, in motion)
*Where:* subheads, buttons, in-product copy, empty states. Conviction stays;
adjectives become concrete nouns; state exactly what ships. No destination claim
as if it's live.

- **Real shipped sub:** "Say what you need once. It reaches the business in
  writing, no phone tag."
- **Off-register:** "Effortlessly connect with the best local businesses through
  our seamless platform."
- **On-register (empty state, real):** "No businesses here yet." / "Try a service,
  business name, or suburb." — plain, useful, no apology, points to the next
  move.
- **Why:** it says what actually happens in the household's words; the swagger is
  the exactness ("one," "in writing," "no phone tag").

### Register D — RECEIPT / RECORD (exact, forward-pointing)
*Where:* the inquiry receipt, confirmations, the written thread. Momentum through
precision. State what happened, point at what's next. The required contract line
appears once — as a fact and the line we're here to erase.

- **Real shipped copy:** "What AE sent — One clear inquiry for {service} with the
  message and reply details you supplied." / "What happens next — The business can
  reply with timing, quote, or whether they can take the work." / "AE has not
  booked, charged, or confirmed."
- **Off-register:** a legal paragraph that leads with guarantees, liability, and
  every power AE lacks.
- **On-register:** keep the shipped copy — it already reads as momentum. The
  contract fact stays exactly once, stated flat, with the forward-pointing lines
  doing the work around it. As booking and charging ship, that line is what
  changes first.
- **Why:** exactness *is* the swagger; the record is trust as texture, not a
  disclaimer chorus.

### Register E — ERROR STATES (calm, owned, no grovelling)
*Where:* failures, toasts, edge cases. Take responsibility in one plain line,
point at the fix, never grovel and never blame the household.

- **Real shipped toasts:** "Inquiry could not be sent." / "Receipt could not be
  copied."
- **Off-register:** "Oops! Something went wrong 😢 We're so sorry for the
  inconvenience, please try again later."
- **On-register:** "Inquiry didn't send. Try again — nothing was lost." /
  "Couldn't copy the receipt. It's still saved to your record." — one line, own
  it, reassure the record survived, point forward.
- **Why:** even the failure keeps the record's promise ("nothing was lost") and
  stays exact; no emoji, no grovel, no false urgency.

## 07 — BEFORE & AFTER

**Before (off-brand):**
> Welcome to Agentic Economy, a revolutionary platform empowering you to
> seamlessly connect with world-class local professionals.

**After (on-brand):**
> Ask once. It gets sorted — in writing, without phone tag.

*Why it works:* kills every hollow word, leads with the exhale, states the real
mechanism.

---

**Before (off-brand):**
> Our platform aims to eventually facilitate connections and may, in the future,
> support additional transactional capabilities.

**After (on-brand):**
> Today: one qualified inquiry, in writing, and a record you keep. Where it's
> going: the same ask, all the way to sorted.

*Why it works:* honest about the shipped rung, plain confidence about where it's
going — the promise register, not a declaration.

---

**Before (off-brand):**
> A disclaimer block listing every action AE has not taken and every warranty it
> refuses.

**After (on-brand):**
> The business confirms timing, quote, and availability. AE has not booked,
> charged, or confirmed.

*Why it works:* one clean fact; no inventory of missing powers. The trajectory
belongs in the surrounding promise, not in the contract line.

---

**Before (off-brand):**
> Sign up now to unlock our unique, best-in-class lead generation solution for
> tradespeople!

**After (on-brand):**
> Work arrives in writing, direct. No lead fees, no resold leads. Claim your page
> free.

*Why it works:* the ROI mirror in concrete nouns; the structural wedge, not hype.

## 08 — THINGS WE NEVER SAY

- **Hollow hype:** unique, revolutionary, world-class, seamless, empower,
  game-changing, cutting-edge.
- **Hedges:** "aim to," "hope to," "strive to," "we're on a mission to try to."
- **Defensive chorus:** stacked disclaimers, warranty language, or lists of powers
  AE lacks. One contract line where the contract needs it — never a drumbeat,
  never as personality.
- **Fake-live capability:** any present-tense claim that AE books, charges, pays,
  dispatches, or confirms *today*. The destination is future-tense conviction;
  never dressed up as a shipped feature.
- **Ambition-shrinking:** "just a simple directory," "only an inquiry tool,"
  anything that caps the destination below the transaction loop recorded in BONES.
- **Provider-quality claims:** best, top, trusted, vetted, good — no quality
  certification of a business.
- **Economist framing:** "domestic trade," "service verticals," "matching
  efficiency," "the domestic services sector."
- **Grovelling / false urgency:** "so sorry for the inconvenience," "oops,"
  "act now," "limited time," emoji in error copy.
- **Internal jargon in public:** proof-ledger, business of record, "the honest
  way," source-owned, manifest, handoff, operator, capability.
