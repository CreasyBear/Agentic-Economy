# A — The Proof Ledger

Emotional bet: trust made tangible as evidence — the receipt/docket/proof-spine is AE’s hero brand object.

## Palette

- `#17201F` Ink — primary text, logo mark, deep UI chrome, authority anchor.
- `#F4EFE6` Bone — main light surface, warm civic/institutional ground.
- `#D8CFC2` Ledger Line — hairlines, dividers, proof-spine rules, quiet borders.
- `#8A2D24` Oxblood — single confident accent for stamps, active proof nodes, CTAs, critical boundary notes.
- `#ECE6DC` Mist — secondary panels, inactive proof states, soft background contrast.

## Type direction

Refined grotesk for headlines and product UI hierarchy; disciplined monospace for receipt IDs, timestamps, source/freshness stamps, and docket metadata. Large type stays calm and exact, not loud. Small type is sparse and readable.

## Logo concept + construction

A record mark: an `A/E` monogram cut by a horizontal ledger/proof line, held inside a restrained seal geometry. The negative-space proof line is the key construction move: it makes the mark feel checked, filed, and handoff-ready without implying certification unless a named standard is present.

## Motif system

- Receipt/docket cards as flat paperless evidence objects.
- Source/freshness stamps: `business supplied · 12 Jun`, `last checked · 14 Jun`.
- Hairline proof-spine connectors linking published facts to inquiry receipts and owner replies.
- Registry/index typography, page labels, alignment marks, and monospace proof IDs.
- Oxblood nodes/stamps only at moments of action or boundary.

## Imagery treatment rules

- Build on Astryx-neutral light: flat clean surfaces, hairline borders, 4–6px radii, restrained shadows.
- Avoid skeuomorphic paper, rubber stamps, desks, amber Daylight identity, hand-drawn texture, purple/AI gradients, blobs, glass, generic icon grids, and fake marketplace proof.
- Business names must be fictional placeholders. Never show star reviews, completed bookings, payments, dispatch, or confirmed availability/price/timing.
- Use `checked`, `business supplied`, `published`, `last checked`, and `owner reply required`; use `Verified` only when paired with a named standard.
