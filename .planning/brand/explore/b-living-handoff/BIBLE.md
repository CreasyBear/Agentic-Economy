# Territory B — The Living Handoff

Emotional bet: make the handoff feel alive — a real need is read, routed through a safe proof conduit, sent to a business, and returned as a tangible receipt without pretending AE has completed the job.

## Palette

- `#F4F1EA` — warm neutral base / page atmosphere.
- `#FCFAF6` — clean surface / cards / phone screens.
- `#18201C` — ink / primary text / logo wordmark.
- `#D8D2C7` — hairline borders / construction rules / quiet dividers.
- `#2F6F4E` — signal green / the only living accent for routes, nodes, primary actions, and proof-state emphasis.

## Type direction

Refined grotesk product sans with restrained weight contrast, tight-but-readable display tracking, and tabular mono accents for timestamps and receipt metadata. No serif relapse, no Fraunces, no decorative handwriting.

## Logo concept + construction

Relay/path mark: two measured nodes bridged by a clean arc. The arc cuts a restrained negative-space fold that can be read as an abstract A/E handoff path at small sizes. Construction uses a simple node radius, one constant stroke width, and a single arc angle so the mark scales from app icon to docket stamp.

## Motif system

- Routed path lines: one signal-green hairline route, never multiple neon trails.
- Waypoint nodes: small filled or outlined dots marking request, provider, receipt.
- Handoff dossier: flat card object with mono timestamp, source/freshness line, and clear boundary copy.
- Response docket returning: a second small card or dotted return segment implying the business replies later.
- Proof spine: Published facts → Source/Freshness → Inquiry sent → Business reply, always boundary-honest.

## Imagery treatment rules

Build on Astryx neutral light: flat matte surfaces, hairline borders, small 4–6px radii, restrained soft shadows, generous gutters, and subtle grain only. Use fictional business names only. Never imply booking, payment, dispatch, confirmed timing, confirmed quote, confirmed availability, star reviews, or unnamed verification. Avoid purple/AI gradients, glassmorphism, blobs, centered-everything, 3-column icon grids, gradient CTAs, and the retired Daylight amber/paper/serif identity.
