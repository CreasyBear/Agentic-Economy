Public launch needs a path that feels alive:

- one named wedge, not “local services”;
- 25 real listings, or 10 credible listings per category if split;
- 8 inquiry-ready owner routes;
- top 10 launch searches return plausible options;
- one founder click-through: hero → registry → listing → inquiry.

If this misses, fix supply. Do not polish more brand copy.
