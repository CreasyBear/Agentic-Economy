## Founder launch post

### Short version

Today we are putting a sharper name and a sharper promise on the thing we are building.

People already ask assistants for help: a plumber, a practice, someone who can take the job off their hands. The assistant gives a list. Then the work comes back to the household — ten tabs, phone tag, quotes that do not line up, and a half-typed message to someone who “knows a guy.”

That gap is the halfway.

`{Final Consumer Name}` starts where the list stops. You say what you need once. It reaches the business in writing. You keep the record.

For owners, the mirror matters just as much: clear inquiries, direct, no lead fees, no resold leads, a page they own.

Ask once. It gets sorted.

### Longer version

The answer engines made finding easier. They did not make the afternoon go away.

Ask for a local business and you get a list. Good names, confident summaries, maybe the right direction. But the real work is still yours: compare, contact, chase, agree, remember what was said, and start again when nobody replies.

Agentic Economy exists to close that distance.

`{Final Consumer Name}` is the consumer door for that promise. Today it turns one household ask into a qualified inquiry in writing, sent to the business, with a record the household keeps. The business replies with timing, quote, or whether they can take the work. That is the shipped rung. It is also the ground the rest of the loop will stand on.

The owner side is not an afterthought. A business should not pay to talk to a customer who was already asking for them. Work should arrive in writing, direct. Pages should be owned by the businesses they describe.

This is one brand, two doors, one record.

Ask once. It gets sorted.
