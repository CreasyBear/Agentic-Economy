## 00 — THE ONE LINE

> **The product should feel like the distance from ask to done closing in front
> of you.**

Everything below is that sentence made buildable. If a design choice does not
either *move the household toward "sorted"* or *hold that motion calm enough to
trust*, it is off-brand.

## 01 — IDENTITY STRATEGY STATEMENT

AE is the action layer of the agentic economy for households — the place the
list stops being a list and starts moving. The visual identity has one job: make
momentum legible and inevitable, not loud. Where every competitor looks
warm-and-reassuring (Airtasker, hipages, Thumbtack, Angi) or cold-and-clever
(the AI answer engines), AE should look like a service that has already seen the
finished thing: confident, spare, in motion, and unbothered. The warmth and the
receipts are real, but they are texture and brakes, never the posture: a fast,
quiet car whose calm makes the speed believable. Design expresses the
destination as trajectory and conviction, and states what ships today as plain,
exact fact. The feeling to manufacture on every screen is the household's
exhale: *it's handled.*

## 02 — KINETIC CALM (internal visual personality)

**Kinetic Calm** is the internal shorthand for a plain public feel: **fast,
calm, one green accent, written record, forward motion.**

Two forces, deliberately held in tension, and neither is allowed to win alone:

- **KINETIC** — the *intent*. Every surface moves someone from ask toward done.
  Composition has a direction. The accent marks motion and arrival. The
  signature beat is a reach completing. Nothing sits still for its own sake.
- **CALM** — the *control*. Soft radii, warm canvas, one accent, generous
  space, flat records, restrained motion. Calm is what makes the momentum read
  as *inevitability* instead of hype — the difference between a sports car at
  speed and a sports car rattling.

Do not use "Kinetic Calm" as a public phrase, campaign line, or end-user
executor brief. Translate it for public work as: **fast, calm, one green accent,
written record, forward motion.**

**Protocol theatre** is the internal banned thing this shorthand exists to refuse:
performing the machinery for show — AI-sparkle glyphs, gateway/manifest jargon
made visual, epistemic ledgers (`KNOWN`/`UNKNOWN`), glowing "intelligence"
gradients, spinners as brand, a stamp that certifies stasis. We show the
*outcome moving*, never the *mechanism performing*.

**Why this resolves the known tension.** The prior anatomy language called the
surface "calm, warm, utility-first" (`.ui-craft/brief.md`, `DESIGN.md` §2a) — a
trust-era, restrained register — while `BONES` makes Aeco an **action brand**.
The two are only in conflict if "calm" means *static* and "utility-first" means
*featureless*. Kinetic Calm resolves it forward: calm is retained as the
**quality of the momentum's control**, and utility becomes **outcome-first** —
the utility *is* that it moves you to done. We keep the warmth and the
composure; we give them a direction. The category we own is not "a calmer
directory" and not "a louder AI" — it is *the calm inevitability of the ask
getting handled.* Restraint is never the goal; **controlled momentum** is.

- **Do:** make calm the reason the speed is trustworthy — spare surfaces, one
  accent, records that settle flat, motion only on state change.
- **Do:** make kinetic the reason the calm isn't boring — directional layout, an
  accent that only ever marks motion or arrival, one honest completion beat.
- **Don't:** read "calm" as "quiet, safe, understated" and drain the momentum —
  that relapses into the defensive trust-brand `BONES` retired.
- **Don't:** read "kinetic" as "busy, animated, spectacle" and add protocol
  theatre — that is the AI-slop the brand refuses.

## 03 — LOGO SYSTEM

### 03.1 The mark — Slipstream (Direction A)

Full geometry, animation, and favicon spec live in `MARK-BRIEF-2026-07-04.md`
§2 Direction A + §4. Summary for builders:

- **Concept:** two unequal converging strokes form a forward-leaning open apex;
  the negative gap at the apex **is "the halfway,"** and a single eucalyptus
  node sits just past it on the trajectory line — the destination, **"done."**
  The eye completes apex → node: ask collapsing into handled. The mark *is* the
  brand thesis; nothing needs explaining.
- **Geometry (64×64 viewBox, optical center ~32,32, ~12° forward lean):** upper
  stroke `M16 16 L44 30`; lower stroke `M16 48 L44 34`; strokes stop ~3px short
  of meeting (the open apex near `(44,32)`); destination node = filled circle
  center `(52,32)`, `r 4`, on the extended trajectory. Stroke weight 5.2 at
  64px; leading (left) caps round, converging (right) caps squared; optional
  acceleration taper 5.2→3.0 toward the apex.
- **Color logic:** two strokes in **Ink `#20261F`**; the node is the **only
  eucalyptus `#40614F`** element — the single "done" spark. This is the
  one-accent rule expressed in the mark itself.
- **16px favicon:** drop the taper, close the 3px apex (strokes meet), keep 3
  solid shapes (two-stroke chevron + node) at ~2px stroke, node `r ~1.5`. Reads
  as a solid arrowhead hitting a target.

### 03.2 The wordmark — consumer-name family

- Set the 4–5 letter consumer name in the **theme grotesk** at weight **~650**,
  tracking **−1.2 to −1.8**, sentence-case as default; all-caps is reserved for
  internal/founder or brand-system artifacts, not public campaign copy.
- Cap-height of the wordmark **= mark height**.
- The wordmark is **name-agnostic but the lockup is not** — do not commission the
  final lockup until the naming re-run clears; build the mark and a placeholder
  wordmark that swaps cleanly.

### 03.3 Lockups

1. **Primary (horizontal):** mark left of wordmark; the apex → node trajectory
   **aims into the first letter** — the mark literally points at the name. Gap
   between node and first glyph ≈ one node-diameter. Use in nav, footer,
   email signature, OG cards.
2. **Stacked (vertical):** mark centered above the wordmark for square avatars
   (social profile, app store). The forward lean is preserved; never rotate the
   mark upright to "balance" the stack.
3. **Mark-only:** favicon, app icon, small-space watermark, motion sting.
4. **Wordmark-only:** running headers/body references where a mark would be
   clutter, and inline prose.

- **Do:** let the trajectory point into the wordmark; keep node-eucalyptus as the
  lone accent in the lockup.
- **Don't:** enclose any lockup in a box, ring, badge, or plate — that resurrects
  the dead seal by another silhouette.

### 03.4 Clearspace & minimum size

- **Clearspace:** a margin equal to the **node diameter** (the `r 4` circle → 8px
  at 64px) on all four sides of the mark, and equal to the **cap-height** around
  a full lockup. Nothing — text, rule, image edge, another logo — enters it.
- **Minimum size:** mark 16px (favicon floor, use the degraded geometry);
  primary horizontal lockup ~96px wide; stacked lockup ~48px wide. Below the
  floor, use the mark-only, never a squeezed lockup.

### 03.5 Application color

- On **Canvas / white:** strokes Ink `#20261F`, node eucalyptus `#40614F`.
- On a **eucalyptus drench field:** strokes `#FDFDFB`; node `#FDFDFB` with a
  knocked-out ink center (or a lighter eucalyptus tint) so the node still reads
  as "the spark."
- **App icon:** `#40614F` rounded tile (radius = `--radius-container` scaled),
  mark in `#FDFDFB`, node knocked out to a lighter eucalyptus.

### 03.6 Misuse — refuse on sight

- **Don't** recolor the node to anything but eucalyptus (or on-accent on a
  drench) — the node's color is the "done" signal and the only accent.
- **Don't** close the apex except at favicon size — the open apex *is* the
  halfway; closing it kills the concept.
- **Don't** add a box, badge, roundel, ring, or shield around it; **don't**
  outline or add a checkmark, handshake, house, wrench, chat bubble, or
  AI-sparkle; **don't** apply gradients, glow, bevel, or drop shadow to the
  mark (depth comes from the token shadow scale on the *card*, never the mark).
- **Don't** stand the mark upright, mirror it, or reduce the forward lean —
  directionality is the point.
- **Don't** place the mark on a busy photo without a solid or scrimmed field
  behind it, and **never** ship the retired `ae-seal.svg` octagon.

**Where the drench moment lives.** Exactly **one** drenched deep-eucalyptus

**Accent behaves as a momentum cue.** Eucalyptus is the visual verb. It appears
on the forward vector (primary CTA), the reached step (active proof node), the
link about to move you, and the landed outcome (the "done" node, the sent-stamp)
— roughly **five to eight percent** of any surface (`DESIGN.md` §2a).

**★ The one new energy rule — *eucalyptus is spent, not sprinkled.*** The accent
may only mark **motion or arrival**: the thing you're about to do, the thing
that's happening, or the thing that just landed. It never tints a resting
surface, never decorates a static block, never appears twice competing for "the
next move." If a eucalyptus element is not moving the household forward or
marking something that already arrived, recolor it Ink, Slate, or a muted fill.
This is what keeps a single accent at 5–8% *and* gives the color a job — the
calm that makes the momentum trustworthy.

**Contrast (from `DESIGN.md`/token map, all AA-safe):** Ink on Canvas ≈ 14.5:1;
On-accent `#FDFDFB` on Eucalyptus ≈ 6:1; Eucalyptus on Canvas ≈ 6:1; Slate on
Canvas ≈ 5.4:1. Sage `#8AA396` on Canvas ≈ 2.4:1 and Border on Canvas ≈ 1.35:1
**fail** for text — use them for borders/dividers/fills only.

- **Do:** spend eucalyptus on exactly one forward move per viewport; keep records
  and resting surfaces Ink-on-white/canvas.
- **Do:** let warmth come from the canvas, imagery, and soft rounding — not from
  a cream wash or a tinted panel.
- **Don't:** add amber, purple, teal, a second green, gradient CTAs, glowing AI
  maps, glass, blobs, or colored proof side-stripes (`DESIGN.md` §2a).
- **Don't:** use eucalyptus as a background tint for decoration or use Sage/Border
  as text — never rely on color alone for status (`DESIGN.md`; WCAG AA floor).

## 06 — MOTION GRAMMAR

**The ONE signature beat: *ask → moving.*** One idea, one motion primitive, two
scales. It renders the brand thesis — the distance from ask to done, closing —
as a *reach completing*.

- **Brand scale (the mark):** Slipstream draws tail → apex (`stroke-dashoffset`,
  **~220ms ease-out**), then the destination node **pops** (scale 0→1, **~120ms**,
  starting **~60ms** after the strokes land). Reads: *the reach completes.* Use
  as the nav-load sting, the OG/social sting, and the manifesto accent.
- **Product scale (the surface):** the generated answer and the receipt
  **assemble in meaningful steps** — answer line → cards/table/map (when the data
  allows) → prose → *what to do now*; the proof spine **fills** step by step
  toward the reached node; the sent-stamp **settles** onto the receipt. Each step
  **120–240ms**, staggered, same ease-out. Reads: *it's moving, and it lands.*

**Durations & easing.** All motion **80–400ms**, GPU-only (transform/opacity),
**ease-out** as the house curve (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`; a slightly
springier `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for the node pop / stamp settle).
Hover/press affordances 80–160ms. **No scroll-jacking.** Honor
`prefers-reduced-motion` by rendering the **final state instantly** — every
animation must be pure enhancement of a state that is already correct on frame
one.

**Where motion is banned:**

- On the **boundary/contract line** ("AE has not booked, charged, or confirmed")
  — it never animates, pulses, or draws attention; it sits flat as a fact.
- On **records once settled** — a receipt does not bounce, breathe, or shimmer
  after it lands; the settle is a one-time arrival, then stillness.
- **Ambient / decorative loops** on content, autoplaying carousels, marquees,
  parallax spectacle, background drift on text, floating blobs.
- **AI-slop motion:** gradient shimmer, glow pulses, "thinking" sparkles, a
  spinner used as a brand device, particles.
- **Anything that moves without a state change.** If nothing changed, nothing
  moves. Motion is a signal, never wallpaper.

- **Do:** use the one beat to show an outcome arriving; stagger answer/receipt
  assembly; settle the stamp once.
- **Don't:** animate to decorate, loop for ambience, or move the boundary line —
  that is the protocol theatre Kinetic Calm forbids.

## 07 — IMAGERY & PHOTOGRAPHY

**Aesthetic:** real and household-real — the actual afternoon (a suburban
kitchen, a real workshop, a street the household knows), warm natural grading,
daylight, un-staged. It should feel like a place you could stand in, not a stock
library.

- **Source priority:** owner-supplied listing photos first (`DESIGN.md` §5).
  Otherwise **clearly-generic** category or neighbourhood mood imagery that can
  never be mistaken for a specific provider's own work.
- **Color treatment:** natural, warm, slightly desaturated to sit under the
  eucalyptus accent; never HDR-punchy, never cool-blue "tech" grading.
- **Honesty (non-negotiable):** generic imagery is **never** labelled as a named
  business's own photo, and no image may imply booking, payment, dispatch,
  availability, ratings, or endorsement (`DESIGN.md` §5, `GUIDELINES` §8).
- **North-star references:** Airbnb / Google Maps for inviting, place-forward
  warmth. Never the lead-selling urgency or star-rating theatre of the legacy
  directories.

- **Do:** use real, warm, place-forward imagery that makes the household feel a
  real business is real and honestly represented.
- **Do:** prefer the four hero objects (receipt, proof spine, stamps, comparison
  ledger) as the "product visual" — records, not fake dashboards or screenshots.
- **Don't:** use stock-smile slop (grinning handshake, headset "support" hero,
  suited-team-around-a-laptop), robot/AI imagery, purple/mesh gradients, glossy
  blobs, neon glow, glassmorphism, or circuit-board/brain/chat-bubble tropes.
- **Don't:** dress a generic photo as a specific provider's work or let any image
  imply a booked/paid/dispatched outcome.

## 08 — ICONOGRAPHY & ILLUSTRATION

- **Icons** come from the **Astryx theme** set: **line** style, functional
  weight (~1.5–2px optical), rounded to rhyme with the 8–16px radii. Icons are
  **utilitarian, not expressive** — wayfinding and affordance, never brand
  spectacle. One accent rule applies: an icon is eucalyptus only when it marks a
  forward action or a reached state; otherwise Ink or Slate.
- **Brand glyph language** (sparingly, for record objects only): the Slipstream
  vocabulary — a directional stroke resolving into a filled node — informs the
  **proof-spine dots/connectors** and the **source/sent stamps**. These are the
  one place a touch of drawn brand character is welcome; keep them thin,
  restrained, mono-adjacent.
- **Illustration:** none as a dependency. No custom illustration *system*, no
  mascot, no AI-sparkle set. If a divider or end-mark is genuinely needed, a
  single thin hairline (optionally with a tiny node) is the ceiling.

- **Do:** use theme line icons for UI; let the Slipstream node motif live only in
  proof spines and stamps.
- **Don't:** introduce a filled/expressive/branded icon set, AI-sparkle glyphs, a
  mascot, or an illustration dependency; **don't** put a checkmark on "done" — the
  eucalyptus node is "done," a tick is a certified verdict (stasis) the brand
  retired.

## 09 — LAYOUT ENERGY (momentum in composition)

Momentum is not just motion — it is **direction built into the still frame.** A
Kinetic Calm layout has a reading gravity that mirrors the Slipstream trajectory:
the eye is pulled **ask → answer → record → next**, generally left-to-right and
up-to-down, with a single clear forward vector per viewport.

- **Asymmetric, never centered-everything.** Heroes are off-balance and
  directional (image/product-object led on one side, ask/headline on the other),
  echoing the mark's ~12° lean (`DESIGN.md` §3). Centered-hero + three-column
  icon grid is the AI-slop default — banned.
- **One primary action per viewport = one forward vector.** The single eucalyptus
  CTA is the composition's arrowhead; secondary paths are quieter text links.
  Competing primary actions kill the sense of direction (`DESIGN.md` §5,
  `.ui-craft/brief.md` principle 7).
- **Hero objects carry the motion, not decoration.** Prefer the receipt / proof
  spine / source stamps / comparison ledger (`DESIGN.md` §2b) over generic
  marketing panels; each is momentum made visible — the ask turning into
  something that moves and lands.
- **Space is the calm.** Generous, uneven whitespace paces the eye and makes the
  one accent land. Density rises on owner/admin (VISUAL_DENSITY ~5) and stays low
  on public surfaces (~3) — calm surfaces, substantive records.
- **The grid is quiet.** Hairline borders (`#E5E4DD`) and soft warm elevation do
  the structural work; no heavy rules, no boxed-in everything, no card-in-card-in-
  card nesting.

- **Do:** give each screen one forward vector and a directional, asymmetric
  composition that ends on the next step.
- **Do:** let records and hero objects be the visual centerpiece; pace with space.
- **Don't:** center-stack everything, ship uniform 3-column icon-card grids, stage
  competing CTAs, or nest cards inside cards.
- **Don't:** fill space with decorative panels when a record, spine, stamp, or
  ledger would carry the same beat with meaning.

## 10 — DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. **Momentum you can trust.** Every surface moves the household toward the
   exhale, and the calm — space, one accent, flat records — is the proof it can
   move that fast without rattling. If a screen doesn't advance the ask, it's
   decoration; if it advances loudly, it's hype. Aim for inevitability.
2. **The accent is a verb.** Eucalyptus only ever marks motion or arrival — the
   next move, the reached step, the landed outcome. It is spent, never sprinkled.
   This is the whole colour discipline in one line.
3. **Records are texture, not theatre.** Receipts, stamps, and proof spines are
   the brakes that let the car go fast — shown as exact, quiet, mono-marked
   artifacts, never dressed up as trust badges, never made to perform. The
   swagger of a receipt is its exactness.
4. **Show the outcome, not the machinery.** We render the ask getting handled —
   never the protocol performing (no AI-sparkle, no gateway/manifest jargon, no
   epistemic ledger, no certified-verdict seal). Trust is plumbing; momentum is
   the personality.
