Posture carried forward: AE is an action brand. **We will book, charge, and
confirm** — that's the destination, stated with conviction. Today the shipped
rung is a **qualified inquiry in writing and a record you keep**; the business
still confirms timing, quote, and availability. Personas below describe real AU
metro people, the afternoon that sends them to AE, and how AE resolves it — the
shipped rung now, the full "sorted" we're building toward. No fabricated
capability, no marketing-profile gloss.

---

## 01 — AUDIENCE STATEMENT

AE is first for the **AU metro household caught in the halfway** — the person who
asked the smartest tool on earth for a plumber, an accountant, a hand with the
thing they've been dreading, got a tidy list of "the best businesses in your
area," and got handed back all the real work: deciding, contacting, chasing,
agreeing. They are not short on options. They are short on *done*. On the mirror
side, AE is first for the **owner-operator sick of paying for leads** — buying
the same resold job five other tradies also bought, paying to talk to a customer
who was already asking for them. Both sides want the same thing from opposite
ends: the work handled, in writing, without the runaround.

---

## 02 — PRIMARY AUDIENCE PROFILE (household)

**Demographics (the basics):**
- Age 28–65; skews 30–50 for the acute "get this handled" jobs.
- AU metro-first: inner and middle-ring Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane (Marrickville,
  Coburg, Paddington and their neighbours), then other capitals.
- Renters and owner-occupiers both; household income mid to upper-mid.
- Time-poor: working, often parenting or caring for a parent, juggling.

**Psychographics (the real stuff):**
- Value their own time and hate feeling mugged off by strangers or platforms.
- Already fluent with AI assistants for answers — and quietly disappointed the
  answers stop short of getting anything done.
- See themselves as capable and busy, not helpless; they don't want hand-holding,
  they want the loop closed.
- Trust the written word over the phone call. If it isn't written down, it didn't
  happen.
- Admire tools that respect their time (Google Maps, a good banking app) and
  resent tools that turn a task into a project.

**Behavioral patterns:**
- Research-light, decision-fast once they can actually compare like-for-like.
- Trigger: a thing breaks, a deadline lands, or a chore they've dreaded finally
  can't wait — hot water gone, switchboard tripping, tax due, mum needs help at
  home.
- Discover via the assistant they already opened, then a search, then a mate who
  "knows a guy." AE has to meet them at the first step, not the fifth.
- Abandon when it turns into ten tabs, three quotes that don't line up, and
  voicemail.

## 03 — AUDIENCE PERSONAS

### HOUSEHOLD

---

**Priya, 31 — marketing manager, renter, Marrickville (inner-west Sydney)**

*"I don't have time to ring around six plumbers between meetings. Just tell me who
can actually come and let me sort it in writing."*

**Their World:** Priya rents a two-bedroom terrace with her partner. She lives in
her calendar and her assistant. When the hot water system died on a Tuesday, she
asked ChatGPT for "a good plumber in Marrickville," got a confident list, and
then sat there — because a list can't come fix a hot water system.

**The halfway afternoon:** 8:40am, cold shower, standup at 9. She's got the list
open in one tab, three plumber websites in three more, a half-typed message in
the building group chat asking if anyone "knows someone," and one plumber's
voicemail. By lunch she's spoken to nobody, compared nothing, and the water's
still cold. The tool that promised to change everything left the hard part exactly
where it found it.

**Goals:** Get it handled before the day's over. Compare who can actually do it,
side by side. Keep it in writing so she's not re-explaining it three times.

**Frustrations:** Phone tag with strangers. Quotes she can't compare because none
measure the same thing. Being handed a list and wished luck.

**What she needs from a brand like this:** One ask, out once, to businesses that
do the work — and a record she can point to instead of a call she has to
remember.

**Decision triggers:** *Buys in* the second one ask replaces the ten tabs.
*Hesitates* if it looks like another form-farm that resells her details.

**Channels:** Her AI assistant first; Instagram, Sydney inner-west community
groups, Google Maps.

**How AE resolves it:** Today — she says what she needs once and AE sends one
qualified inquiry in writing, with a receipt she keeps; the plumber replies with
timing and quote. Where we're going — AE books it and confirms it, and the same
record carries the whole way.

**Dave, 44 — project manager, dad of two, homeowner, Coburg (inner-north Melbourne)**

*"I just want it done properly and I want it in writing. I've been burned by 'yeah
mate, no worries' too many times."*

**Their World:** Dave owns a weatherboard he's slowly doing up while raising two
kids and keeping an eye on his dad across town. He's practical and skeptical —
he's used hipages before and hated the pile-on of five sparkies all ringing about
the same job.

**The halfway afternoon:** Saturday, the switchboard's tripping every time the
kettle and heater run together. He describes it to his phone's assistant and gets
"here are highly-rated electricians near you." So he requests three quotes, and by
Sunday night he's got three numbers that measure three different things, two
follow-up calls he didn't ask for, and a nagging feeling he's about to overpay.
The deciding, the chasing, the pinning-down — all of it landed back on him.

**Goals:** A real like-for-like comparison. No lead-fee pile-on. A written trail
so there's no "that's not what we agreed."

**Frustrations:** Being resold as a lead. Vague verbal quotes. Chasing tradies who
never call back.

**What he needs from a brand like this:** To reach the right electrician directly,
once, in writing — and eventually to book and pay through the same record so
there's no ambiguity.

**Decision triggers:** *Buys in* at "no lead fees, direct, in writing."
*Hesitates* if he smells another quote-marketplace.

**Channels:** Google, Reddit (r/melbourne, trades threads), word of mouth, his
assistant.

**How AE resolves it:** Today — one qualified inquiry straight to the electrician,
in writing, with a receipt; no resold-lead pile-on. Where we're going — AE will
book and confirm the job on the same written record he already trusts.

**Margaret, 63 — semi-retired teacher, Bardon (inner Brisbane), arranging help for her mum**

*"I don't want to cold-call ten places and explain Mum's situation ten times. I
just want to line up the right help and have something to show my brother."*

**Their World:** Margaret is winding down from teaching and has become the default
organiser for her 88-year-old mother — home help, a dentist who does home visits,
the small stuff that piles up. She's comfortable asking her phone for answers but
uneasy about doing anything important that leaves no trace.

**The halfway afternoon:** Wednesday, she asks her assistant for "aged care home
help near Mum in Bardon," gets a neat list, and then stalls. She doesn't want to
ring strangers and re-tell her mother's needs to each one. She doesn't want to
pick wrong. And whatever she chooses, she wants it written down so she can forward
it to her brother, who'll ask "did you actually sort it?" The list gave her names.
It gave her none of the calm.

**Goals:** Line up trustworthy help without the phone marathon. One clear written
record to share with family. Not to feel like she's gambling on strangers.

**Frustrations:** Re-explaining a sensitive situation over and over. No paper
trail. The quiet fear of choosing badly for someone she loves.

**What she needs from a brand like this:** To say what her mum needs once, reach
the right providers in writing, and hold a record the family can see.

**Decision triggers:** *Buys in* at "in writing, one ask, something to point to."
*Hesitates* at anything that feels like a hard-sell marketplace or hides what
happens next.

**Channels:** Google, Facebook (community + carer groups), her phone's assistant,
her GP's recommendations.

**How AE resolves it:** Today — one qualified inquiry in writing to the right
providers, and a receipt she can forward to her brother. Where we're going — AE
will confirm and, where money's involved, charge cleanly, all on that same
shareable record.

### BUSINESS OWNER (the mirror side)

---

**Sam, 38 — licensed electrician, two-van operation, Parramatta (western Sydney)**

*"I'm done paying for leads I don't win. Five of us buy the same job, we all ring
the poor bloke, and I've paid for the privilege of losing."*

**Their World:** Sam runs a small sparky business with one apprentice and a second
van he's still paying off. Word of mouth and repeat work carry him, but the lead
platforms have him over a barrel: he pays per lead, the lead gets resold to
competitors, and Oneflare — one of the ones he used — is shutting down 30 June
2026 anyway.

**Frustrations:** Lead fees on jobs he never wins. Resold leads and the race to
the bottom. Paying to talk to a customer who was already looking for exactly what
he does. A platform-owned profile he can't control.

**Goals:** Fewer, clearer jobs that come to him direct. Own his own page. Spend
his evenings quoting real work, not bidding against four other vans.

**What he needs from a brand like this:** Work that arrives in writing, direct, no
lead fee — and a page that's his.

**Decision triggers:** *Buys in* at "no lead fees, no resold leads, claim your
page free." *Hesitates* if it's yet another subscription that quietly sells his
inquiries on.

**How AE resolves it:** Today — inquiries reach him direct and in writing, no lead
fee, on a page he claims and controls. Where we're going — when AE books and
charges, the job and the payment land on the same record he owns, not a
middleman's ledger.

book and confirm through that same owned front door, on the practice's terms.

## 04 — AUDIENCE LANGUAGE

**How households describe the problem:**
- "It gave me a list and then just… stopped."
- "I don't have time to ring around."
- "I've got ten tabs open and I've still sorted nothing."
- "None of these quotes measure the same thing."
- "I ended up messaging a mate who knows a guy."

**How households describe the desired outcome:**
- "Just get it sorted."
- "Can I do it once and have it in writing?"
- "I want something I can point to / show my partner / forward to my brother."
- "No phone tag."

**How business owners describe the problem:**
- "I'm paying for leads I don't win."
- "The same job gets resold to five of us."
- "I'm paying to talk to my own customer."
- "AI's saying stuff about us that isn't right and I can't fix it."

**Why they've chosen competitors (the language they use to justify it):**
- "Everyone uses hipages / it's just what you do."
- "At least I get *some* quotes." / "At least I show up on Google."
- Resignation, not loyalty — they use the incumbents because there wasn't a
  better door, not because they like them.

**Brand-building implication:** Speak the afternoon back to them in their own
words — "ten tabs," "phone tag," "knows a guy," "get it sorted," "in writing," "no
lead fees" — never in economist or platform language. Say what ships plainly; say
the destination (booked, charged, confirmed) with conviction. Their resignation
toward incumbents is the opening: they're waiting for a better door.

## 05 — WHO THIS BRAND IS NOT FOR (first)

- **Bargain-hunters chasing the cheapest quote above all.** AE competes on the
  outcome and the record, not on being the cheapest lead auction.
- **Businesses that want a lead-resale firehose.** If the pitch someone wants is
  "buy 200 unqualified leads a month," AE is structurally the wrong tool — no
  resold leads is the point.
- **Enterprise / national procurement.** AU metro households and local
  owner-operators first; not corporate sourcing.
- **People who want a phone-first, no-record experience.** AE's whole value is
  that it's in writing.
- **Anyone wanting fake verification or invented capability.** Claimed is not
  verified; today's rung is stated plainly, the destination as conviction.

## 06 — AUDIENCE IMPLICATIONS

**Messaging implications:** Lead with the exhale — "ask once, it gets sorted" —
and name the enemy (the halfway) in the household's own afternoon. Carry the
business ROI mirror in parallel: work in writing, no lead fees, own your page.
State the shipped rung as plain fact; state book/charge/confirm as the future
we're building.

**Channel implications:** Meet households at the assistant and the search, where
the halfway actually happens — not five steps later. Meet owner-operators where
they vent about lead fees (trades communities, word of mouth, local groups).
AU-metro targeting first.

**Voice implications:** Household-real, not economist. Future-tense conviction
about the destination; concrete nouns on product surfaces. See
`VOICE-2026-07-05.md` for the register matrix.

**Product/experience implications:** The record is the product truth every
persona trusts — keep it central and shareable (forward to family, keep as
receipt, owner-controlled page). Booking, charging, and confirming, as they ship,
must land on that same record — that continuity is what makes closing the loop
feel safe rather than reckless.
