3. **Sequencing gate (strategist premortem).** The manifesto push is gated on

## THE ONE FOUNDER DECISION — the name

Unanimous council reject (3-4/10, all six): reads eco/echo before economy, needs
its own pronunciation guide, AECO Energy collision unscreened, category
compression means nothing to a household, the verb is assigned not earned.

The category-claim job is ALREADY done by the corporate name (Agentic Economy).
The consumer name's only job is the household mouth — and the register the
council unanimously kept is "Ask once. It gets sorted."

**Options:**
- **A. Keep Aeco.** Accept the pronunciation tax, run the TM screen, spend
  awareness budget teaching the name. Founder-locked root preserved.
- **B. Re-run naming, new brief:** outcome-energy word in the master-promise
  register (the "sorted" family), zero pronunciation ambiguity, pub-test clean,
  verb-able, AU mouth; Agentic Economy keeps the category claim at corporate
  level. Council evidence says this is the higher-percentage path.
- **C. Pause naming** until after wedge liquidity proof; launch product-first
  under neutral copy, name later with real users to test against.

Recommendation: **B.** Not defensiveness — aim. The council kept every weapon
except this one.
