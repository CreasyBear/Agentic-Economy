## Brand in one breath

**Agentic Economy is the action layer for the household economy.**

People ask assistants for help and get a list. Then the real work comes back to
them: choosing, contacting, chasing, comparing, remembering. AE starts where the
list stops. One ask goes out in writing. One record stays with the household.
The business gets a clear approach, direct, without lead-fee theatre.

**Master promise:** Ask once. It gets sorted.

## Posture

- **Action brand, not trust brand.** Receipts and records are the plumbing, not the personality.
- **Momentum over hedging.** Short sentences. Verbs. Completion language.
- **Household-real language.** Phone tag, ten tabs, quote chaos, “knows a guy,” sorted.
- **Owner economics in the open.** No lead fees. No resold leads. Own your page.
- **Ambition stated as trajectory.** Big destination, clean shipped truth.

## Claim discipline

Today ships: **qualified inquiry in writing + record.**

Say that plainly when a contract line is needed. The brand voice stays forward:
one ask, one written path, one record kept.

Public product surfaces must not claim live booking, payment, dispatch, or
confirmation until those paths ship. The internal/founder flag — **WE WILL BOOK.
WE WILL CHARGE. WE WILL CONFIRM.** — is destination language, not site copy.

## Voice

Default register: **swagger with receipts.**

Do:

- “Ask once. It gets sorted.”
- “The list was never the finish.”
- “It reaches the business in writing — no phone tag, one record you keep.”
- “Clear inquiries. No lead fees. No resold leads.”
- “Where asking stops being research and starts becoming action.”

Do not:

- over-explain the boundary;
- say “trusted marketplace,” “AI-powered solution,” “seamless,” “revolutionary,” or “world-class”;
- use economist language like “domestic trade”;
- lead with “we do not / cannot / will not”;
- use purple-gradient AI slop, badges, seals, shields, or certification theatre.

## Surface rules

### Home

One drenched brand moment. The hero sells the exhale, not the mechanism manual.
Use the master promise and one concrete bridge into the written ask.

### Registry

Help the household compare and move. No ratings theatre. No “trusted provider”
claims. Lead with useful facts and the written next step.

### Listing

Show what the business offers, where it serves, how to reach it, and what happens
when the ask goes out. Plain labels beat internal kickers.

### Inquiry

The action is simple: tell the business what you need. One mechanism line:
“it reaches them in writing.”

### Receipt

The receipt is the product’s first proof object. Momentum first: what was sent,
when, to whom, and what can happen next. One contract line may appear exactly
once when required: “AE has not booked, charged, or confirmed.” State it flat.
No chorus.

### Agent JSON / assistant payloads

Machine-facing payloads can carry explicit boundary fields. Human public surfaces
should not expose internal terms like manifest, capability, gateway, MCP,
OpenAPI, callable, autonomous, source-owned, readback, or operator.

## Visual system

The old seal is dead. Slipstream is the replacement mark direction after final

Astryx first. Tailwind is glue. No new bespoke CSS system.

## Launch gates

Before public push:

1. final consumer name decision recorded;
2. TM/domain/social screen cleared enough to proceed;
3. supply gate passes: enough real listings and owner routes for the first wedge;
4. founder click-through proves hero → registry → listing → inquiry works;
5. copy scan passes with no fake live booking/payment/dispatch/confirmation claim.

## Delete reflex

If a doc repeats this guide without adding a launch asset, legal fact, product
truth, or design decision, delete the doc. If a paragraph exists only to calm an
imagined critic, delete the paragraph. The brand is not a deposition.
