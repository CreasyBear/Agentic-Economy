# Competitor Brand Scan — 2026-07-04

Scanned against the lens locked in `.planning/brand/BONES-2026-07-04.md`: AE is an
**action brand** (not a trust brand), voice = **visionary swagger**, enemy = **the
halfway** (AI gets you a list, then abandons you with the deciding/contacting/
chasing/agreeing), hero = the household's **"it's sorted" moment**. Ten players
scanned: seven quote/lead/review incumbents (AU + US) plus three agentic-era
answer surfaces. Promise rung scale used throughout: **inform** (shows you
things) → **decide** (helps you compare/choose) → **connect** (facilitates
contact/quote/hire) → **transact** (completes payment/booking inside the
platform).

---

## 1. Airtasker

- **Tagline:** "Get anything done & earn more money"; "Post any task. Pick the
  best person. Get it done." [1][2]
- **Promise rung:** connect → transact. Airtasker is the one legacy incumbent
  that actually closes its own loop for gig-style tasks — post, bid, hire, pay
  — inside one two-sided marketplace. [1]
- **Voice register:** friendly-populist achievement talk ("get more done,"
  "everything's done") — completion-flavored but consumer/gig-scale, zero
  economy-level ambition or challenger framing. [3]
- **Visual gist:** photography-led, everyday-hero imagery; a 2023 "insights-led"
  rebrand pushed for global scale, not a swagger repositioning. [3]
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** the loop only closes *inside Airtasker's own
  marketplace* for tasks/gig work it already intermediates — it has no
  discovery-to-action claim across arbitrary local businesses, and never uses
  "economy" or "agentic" vocabulary at all.

Citations: [1] https://www.airtasker.com/au/ · [2] https://www.airtasker.com/us/about/
· [3] https://www.qualtrics.com/blog/airtasker-brand-revolution/

## 2. hipages

- **Tagline/platform:** "Behind every great home"; "Change The Way You
  Tradie"; brand idea "Trade Up." [4][5]
- **Promise rung:** connect (quotes/lead marketplace) — now expanding into
  SaaS/CRM tooling for tradies, i.e. doubling down on the supply side rather
  than closing the consumer-side action gap. [6]
- **Voice register:** warm, earnest, civic partnership language ("great
  Australian partnership," "building better lives") — celebratory, zero irony
  or challenger energy. [4]
- **Visual gist:** 2024 FutureBrand refresh built on a bright "hipages Orange"
  with Steel/Plaster neutrals and Blueprint/Hi-vis pop accents; an upward
  arrow evolved from the house-symbol signals "Trade Up"; imagery is authentic
  on-site trade photography, not manifesto-style art direction. [6]
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** explicitly stops at "get quotes, find and hire" —
  never claims to finish the job for the homeowner or collapse the
  after-quote friction (negotiating, scheduling, chasing); "Trade Up" upgrades
  the tool, not the completion promise.

Citations: [4] https://campaignbrief.com/hipages-champions-tradies-in-latest-brand-campaign-behind-every-great-home-via-bmf/
· [5] https://www.bandt.com.au/hipages-launches-new-digital-tradie-campaign/
· [6] https://futurebrand.com.au/projects/hipages

## 3. Oneflare

- **Tagline:** "Where to-do, gets done"; "the one connection you need to get
  jobs done ... first time and every time." [7]
- **Promise rung:** connect (quotes marketplace) — same shape as hipages/
  ServiceSeeking, no payment or completion inside platform. [7]
- **Voice register:** plain transactional efficiency, no swagger.
- **Visual gist:** acquired by Airtasker in 2022; folded/retired as its own
  brand — **Oneflare is closing on 30 June 2026.** [7]
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** literally branded its promise around the halfway
  ("to-do, gets done") and is shutting down anyway — direct evidence that the
  category-standard "quote marketplace" model, even with a completion-flavored
  name, isn't durable or differentiated enough on its own.

Citations: [7] https://www.oneflare.com.au/about · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oneflare

## 4. ServiceSeeking

- **Positioning:** built explicitly as "an alternative to clicking links
  through Google and calling a tradie to find that they are too busy, charge
  too much or don't answer their phone." [8]
- **Promise rung:** connect (post a job, receive quotes, compare, hire) — self-
  describes as a "disruptor" of Google search-and-call-around. [8]
- **Voice register:** pragmatic, understated disruptor — not visionary; states
  the problem plainly rather than dramatizing it.
- **Visual gist:** quote-comparison marketplace UI, reviews-forward listing
  pages (general site observation, not brand-campaign sourced).
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** ServiceSeeking names almost the *exact* enemy AE
  names ("Google ... cannot quote you a price ... or offer the opportunity to
  speak to a real person online") [8] — but its own fix only automates the
  *request* step; negotiation, decision, and completion stay fully manual.
  Trustpilot rates it "Poor" (2.2/5) [9], suggesting a real execution gap
  between the stated promise and delivered experience.

Citations: [8] https://rocketreach.co/serviceseekingcomau-profile_b5d0dcc3f42e418a
· [9] https://www.trustpilot.com/review/serviceseeking.com.au

## 5. Yelp

- **Tagline/mission:** early tagline "Real people, real reviews"; enduring
  mission "to connect people with great local businesses." [10][11]
- **Promise rung:** inform (reviews/discovery) with light decide-support via
  ratings; no connect/transact claim in the core identity. [11]
- **Voice register:** consumer-community, authenticity-first, folksy trust
  language ("authenticity, tenacity, and collaboration") — retrospective and
  reputational, never prospective or action-oriented. [12]
- **Visual gist:** star-rating iconography and UGC photo grids as the core
  visual unit — reviews as artifact, not action or completion.
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** Yelp is arguably the purest incumbent embodiment
  of "the halfway" — its brand promise ends at discovery/trust-building and
  it has never claimed to get anything done. It is the closest thing to a
  direct foil for AE's enemy framing.

Citations: [10] https://longreads.com/2024/08/05/everybody-gets-a-star/
· [11] https://portersfiveforce.com/blogs/mission/yelp
· [12] https://blog.yelp.com/life-at-yelp/living-yelps-mission-and-values/

## 6. Thumbtack

- **Tagline:** "Thumbtack makes caring for your home easier"; self-description
  as "a technology company helping millions of people confidently care for
  and improve their homes." [13]
- **Promise rung:** decide + connect. Its "Home Care" layer (Home Profile,
  Seasonal Upkeep Guides, and a 2026 AI feature that interprets a described
  problem from text/photo/voice and suggests "next steps") is a real decide-
  layer bolted onto the classic hire-a-pro connect marketplace. [14][15]
- **Voice register:** caretaking and reassuring ("confidently care for"),
  calm competence, explicitly ongoing-relationship framing ("from moving in
  to moving out") — nurturing, not swaggering. [13][14]
- **Visual gist:** home-app product aesthetic (navy/teal-leaning, general
  observation) built around a persistent "Home Profile," not a manifesto or
  campaign-driven identity.
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** **the single closest incumbent flank.** Thumbtack
  explicitly claims to be "the first to create the technology to proactively
  care for the home" [15] and layers AI interpretation + guidance on top of
  pro-matching — but its rung stops at guidance and matching; the AI narrates
  and suggests, a human still does the deciding, contacting, and agreeing.
  It owns "proactive home care," not "economy" or "agentic action."

Citations: [13] https://www.thumbtack.com/ , https://www.thumbtack.com/about
· [14] https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220518005437/en/

## 7. Angi

- **Tagline:** current "jobs done well"; mission "make it easy to love your
  home"; 2021 rebrand tagline was "And done." [16][17]
- **Promise rung:** connect (hire pros/leads), with a 2025 "AI Helper" adding
  an emerging decide-support layer. [18]
- **Voice register:** reassuring, quality-first, warm consumer tone ("love
  where you live," "done well") — no swagger, no named enemy at the category
  level. [17][19]
- **Visual gist:** modernized post-2021 consumer branding (general knowledge;
  no primary color-system source located).
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** Angi's newest campaign ("Welcomes You to the
  Internet, Where (Unfortunately) Anyone Can Be Anything") [19] actually names
  a version of the halfway — you can't trust who's real online — but resolves
  it with a *verification/trust* claim, not an *action/completion* claim. The
  "then what happens" gap (deciding, contacting, chasing, agreeing) stays
  open, same structural gap as hipages/Oneflare/ServiceSeeking.

## 8. Perplexity (agentic-era)

- **Positioning:** "a free AI-powered answer engine that provides accurate,
  trusted, and real-time answers ... with source citations." [20]
- **Promise rung:** decide (cited comparisons) → **already live at transact**
  for retail: "Buy with Pro" lets US Pro users check out for select products
  from select Shopify-powered merchants without leaving Perplexity. [21]
  Not yet extended to local household-service hiring/booking.
- **Voice register:** credibility-first, research-grade ("trusted,"
  citation-obsessed) — analytical, positions itself as a confident-research
  tool, not an economy-builder. [20]
- **Visual gist:** minimal, citation-card UI; utilitarian, no manifesto
  imagery or brand swagger.
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** Perplexity is the sharpest live proof that
  "answer → act" is technically shippable *today* — for retail SKUs. It has
  not pointed that mechanic at local services (plumber/electrician/aged-care
  category), which is exactly AE's wedge, but it is the most credible
  near-term threat if it does.

Citations: [20] https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/shop-like-a-pro
· [21] https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/shop-like-a-pro

## 9. ChatGPT / OpenAI local & shopping (agentic-era)

- **Positioning:** shopping research + comparisons inside ChatGPT, plus
  business listings with map previews for "where to go" / "what to buy
  nearby" queries. [22][23]
- **Promise rung:** decide (shopping research, buyer's guides) with early
  connect signals (mapped local listings) — but OpenAI **retreated from
  transact** in March 2026, dropping in-app Instant Checkout because it "did
  not offer the level of flexibility" OpenAI wanted, in favor of richer
  discovery tools. [24]
- **Voice register:** infrastructural, small-business-empowerment framing
  ("Main Street growth engine," ads that "will never bias" recommendations)
  — trust-building and economic-opportunity language, not a challenger
  swagger about ending the halfway. [25]
- **Visual gist:** conversational assistant UI, product-comparison grids and
  map-preview cards — no distinct marketplace or brand-forward identity.
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** the largest AI distribution surface (900M weekly
  users, ~50M shopping queries/day) [26] has *proven* demand for the decide
  rung and *publicly struggled* with the transact rung — validating the size
  of the halfway-gap while showing that "bolt on a checkout button" doesn't
  survive contact with reality. That is a structural opening for a
  purpose-built action layer rather than a checkout feature.

## 10. Google AI Overviews / AI Mode local search (agentic-era)

- **Positioning:** AI Overviews now appear in 80%+ of local-service queries
  (up from ~20% in early 2024), citing 1–3 businesses per answer; AI Mode has
  passed 1B monthly users. [27][28]
- **Promise rung:** inform/decide dominant today — **but Google announced at
  I/O 2026 it is "expanding agentic booking capabilities in Search to local
  experiences and services," and "for select categories like home repair,
  beauty or pet care, you can ask Google to call businesses on your behalf,"
  rolling out to everyone in the US "this summer."** [29] That is explicit
  connect→transact-adjacent territory — an agent contacting a business on the
  household's behalf.
- **Voice register:** feature-level product language inside "Search," not a
  distinct brand voice — no household framing, no named enemy, no economy-
  level ambition. It's a capability shipped inside an existing utility, not a
  brand making a promise. [29]
- **Visual gist:** none distinct — inherits Google Search's neutral, ambient
  UI; there is no dedicated "AI Mode local action" identity, mark, or
  campaign.
- **Halfway-gap exposure:** **the critical finding of this scan.** Google is
  the one incumbent actively building the exact mechanic AE is planting its
  flag on — an agent that acts on a household's behalf for home-repair-style
  categories — but it is shipping it as a hidden feature inside general
  search, with zero brand construction, no named enemy, and no "household
  economy" framing. The *capability* is being commoditized inside a utility
  layer while the *brand territory* remains unclaimed by anyone, including
  Google itself.

Citations: [27] https://www.growthmarketingfl.com/blog/google-ai-overviews-service-businesses-2026
· [28][29] https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/search-io-2026/

## GAPS: unoccupied brand positions

1. **No incumbent uses swagger.** Every legacy player (Airtasker, hipages,
   Oneflare, ServiceSeeking, Yelp, Thumbtack, Angi) sits in a warm/earnest/
   reassuring/pragmatic register — "great home," "great partnership," "love
   where you live," "done well." None declare a future in first-person-plural
   conviction or throw challenger jabs. AE's visionary-swagger voice is
   genuinely open ground among these seven. [3][4][13][17]

2. **No incumbent names "the halfway" as an enemy.** Pieces of it surface
   implicitly — ServiceSeeking vs. "calling around" [8], Angi's "anyone can be
   anything" trust gap [19], Thumbtack's proactive-care narrative [15] — but
   none frame the AI-gives-you-a-list-then-abandons-you moment as a manifesto-
   grade enemy. This framing is unclaimed.

3. **No incumbent claims "household economy" / "domestic agentic economy"
   scale.** All use "home," "jobs," "tasks" — service-marketplace vocabulary,
   not market-making language. Open ground, but per BONES §2 this must stay
   trajectory/conviction language, not a claim that the market already exists.

4. **Refute the naive version of "open territory."** At the *capability*
   layer it is not open: Google is already shipping the actual connect/
   transact mechanic — "ask Google to call businesses on your behalf" for
   home-repair, beauty, and pet-care categories, US-wide this summer [29] —
   and Perplexity already has a live answer→purchase rail for retail via "Buy
   with Pro" [21]. OpenAI proved the demand for decide-stage AI shopping at
   massive scale (900M WAU, ~50M shopping queries/day) and then *publicly
   retreated* from its own checkout feature in March 2026 for lacking
   flexibility [24][26] — evidence the transact rung is contested and hard,
   not vacant.

5. **Confirm the territory is open at the BRAND layer.** Nobody — including
   Google — has attached a name, a voice, a named enemy, or a household-scale
   ambition to the "act on your behalf" mechanic. Google ships it as a
   feature inside Search with zero identity; OpenAI frames it as small-
   business economic opportunity, not household momentum; Perplexity frames
   it as research credibility, not completion. **"The action layer of the
   agentic economy for households" is unclaimed as a brand position today —
   confirmed** — but it is a race against capability-commoditization, not
   occupation of an empty room. AE's moat has to be the named enemy, the
   swagger voice, and the receipt/evidence plumbing that makes the action
   trustworthy — not a claim to have invented "an AI that can act," which
   Google, OpenAI, and Perplexity are all independently proving out in
   parallel right now.
