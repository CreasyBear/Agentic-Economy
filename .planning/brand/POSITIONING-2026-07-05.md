## 01 — CATEGORY DEFINITION

- **Stated category:** local business discovery + agent-readable business
  registry. What the product literally is on day one.
- **Actual category (what people compare us against):** the moment after they ask
  an assistant for help. The real rival is the blinking cursor after ChatGPT
  hands over "the best businesses in your area." People compare AE against *doing
  it themselves with ten tabs open*.
- **Opportunity category:** the **action layer of the agentic economy for
  households.** Everyone above us builds answers; everyone beside us builds
  marketplaces. Nobody has named — or claimed — the layer that carries a
  household all the way to **booked, charged, confirmed.** That layer is the
  category AE is building.

**Recommendation:** frame AE as the action layer, not "a directory" and not "an
AI search tool." The directory is plumbing. The answer is somebody else's
commodity. **Done — booked, charged, confirmed — is the category we own.**

Where each scanned player sits:

- **Yelp** — bottom-left. Reviews and discovery; the purest incumbent embodiment
  of the halfway. A brand, but its promise ends at "here's who exists."
- **hipages / ServiceSeeking / Oneflare / Angi** — mid-left cluster. The
  quote/lead marketplaces. They *connect* (get you quotes) but stop before the
  after-quote work — negotiating, scheduling, chasing, agreeing, paying. Warm,
  earnest register; no named enemy. Oneflare closing 30 June 2026 is direct
  evidence the plain quote-marketplace model isn't durable on its own.
- **Airtasker** — mid-right, mid-height. Genuinely closes its own loop (post,
  bid, hire, pay) — but only inside its own marketplace for gig-style tasks it
  already intermediates, never across arbitrary local businesses, and with no
  economy-level ambition or challenger framing.
- **Thumbtack** — center-right, low. The closest incumbent flank: a real AI
  decide-layer bolted onto pro-matching. But the AI narrates and suggests; a
  human still decides, contacts, and agrees. Owns "proactive home care," not
  "booked, charged, confirmed."
- **Perplexity** — right, low. Already *transacts* for retail SKUs ("Buy with
  Pro"). Sharpest proof that answer→pay ships today — but pointed at retail, not
  local household services, and framed as research credibility, not a promise.
- **OpenAI / ChatGPT** — center-right, low. Proved massive demand for the
  *decide* rung (900M weekly users, ~50M shopping queries/day), then publicly
  *retreated* from in-app checkout in March 2026. Evidence the transact rung is
  contested and hard — framed as small-business opportunity, not household
  momentum.
- **Google AI Mode** — far-right, bottom. The critical finding: Google is
  shipping the exact mechanic — "ask Google to call businesses on your behalf"
  for home-repair, beauty, pet-care, US-wide — but as a hidden feature inside
  Search, with zero brand, no named enemy, no household framing.

**Where AE stands:** top of the vertical axis, flag planted at the far right —
on **done: booked, charged, confirmed.** AE is the only player pairing a *named
household promise* with the intent to carry the household all the way. The honest
present-tense read: AE ships the **in-writing rung today** (a qualified inquiry
that reaches the business, and a record the household keeps) and is building the
booking, charging, and confirming that finish the loop. The flag marks the
destination; the rung marks where we've shipped to so far.

**Unclaimed territory:** the entire top-right quadrant. No incumbent has swagger.
No incumbent names the halfway. Nobody — including Google — has attached a name,
a voice, an enemy, or a household-scale promise to closing the loop. That
quadrant is empty of *brands*. It is not empty of *capability*.

## 03 — POSITIONING TERRITORY

**The Space:** the action layer of the agentic economy for households — where an
ask becomes a booked, charged, confirmed outcome, in writing, without gambling on
strangers.

**The Audience Owned:** the AU metro household that just asked an assistant for a
plumber, an accountant, a hand with the thing they've been dreading, got a tidy
list, and got left with all the real work. AE is the obvious next move for
whoever is staring at that list.

**The Competitive Moat (honest about the capability race — sharpened by
council convergence, 2026-07-05, `COUNCIL-CONVERGENCE-2026-07-05.md` #5):**
The capability — "an agent that can act on your behalf" — is **not** the
moat. Google, Perplexity, and OpenAI are each proving it out right now;
Google is shipping the closest version, US-wide, this summer, as a Search
feature. Winning is not about being first to book, charge, or confirm —
those mechanics are commoditizing.

**The named enemy is the campaign hook, not the moat — and the hook has an
expiry date.** The halfway is real and sharp today, and it is the best
hook in the category while it lasts: when a household feels the
abandonment, AE is the only brand that has already put words to it. But
the enemy's lifespan is set by the incumbents, not by AE — the day
Google's "call businesses on your behalf" (or an equivalent from
Perplexity or OpenAI) ships and just works, the halfway stops being a felt
problem for whoever holds that tool. Use the hook while it's true; never
build the company's permanent identity on a competitor's temporary gap.

**The durable moat is what survives after the enemy expires — the record
you own + the economics:**

1. **The business-side economics incumbents can't copy.** No lead fees, no
   resold leads, own your page. hipages, Angi, Thumbtack, and the quote
   marketplaces *make their money* selling and reselling leads; they
   cannot match "no lead fees" without breaking their own model.
   Structural wedge, not a slogan — and it holds regardless of who closes
   the capability gap first.
2. **The record that makes booking, charging, and confirming
   trustworthy.** When AE books, charges, and confirms, every step is
   written down and reconstructable — the mandate, the receipt, the dated
   thread. A capability-only competitor acts and vanishes; it hands the
   household no durable record they own. That plumbing is what makes it
   *safe* to let the loop close — and it's AE's real differentiator,
   today as a record and tomorrow as the ground under every booking and
   charge.
3. **Built for where people now ask.** AE pages are built to be read by
   the assistants households already use, and by the household, off the
   same page.

The moat is **the record + the business economics**, compounding while
the capability commoditizes around us and the named-enemy hook does its
short-term work. AE will book, charge, and confirm — and it will be the
only brand that does it as a household promise, with a receipt.

## 05 — POSITIONING PROOF POINTS

Shipped-today facts are marked *(shipped)*; the destination is marked
*(trajectory)* and stated as future-tense conviction, never as a present feature.

- **Proof 1 — One ask, one qualified inquiry, in writing.** *(shipped)* A
  household says what it needs once; AE sends one clear inquiry to the business
  and hands back a record.
- **Proof 2 — A record you keep.** *(shipped)* Every inquiry produces a receipt
  and a written thread the household can point to — not a hope, not a call nobody
  remembers.
- **Proof 3 — Compare who does what, side by side.** *(shipped)* Businesses
  publish what they actually do, on pages built to be read by people and their
  assistants alike.
- **Proof 4 — No lead fees. No resold leads.** *(shipped)* Inquiries reach the
  business direct; claiming a page is free.
- **Proof 5 — You own your page.** *(shipped)* Claim it, keep it right, set your
  contact boundary — versus platform-owned profiles.
- **Proof 6 — We will book, charge, and confirm.** *(trajectory)* The distance
  from a qualified ask to a booked, paid, confirmed outcome is the distance we
  are closing. Every step will land in the same record. Stated as conviction —
  the receipt that today says "not booked" is the line we are here to erase.

## 06 — WHAT THIS BRAND REFUSES TO BE

- **AE is not another quote marketplace.** We will never sell or resell leads, or
  charge a business to reach a customer who was already asking for them.
- **AE is not a reviews-and-stars discovery site.** We will not compete on
  reputation theatre. The record beats the rating.
- **AE is not an answer engine.** A paragraph is not an outcome. We start where
  the list stops and we do not quit until it's done.
- **AE will never dress today's rung up as the whole loop, or dress the
  destination down.** We state what has shipped as plain fact and name the
  destination — booked, charged, confirmed — with full conviction. We do not
  fake a feature as live, and we do not pretend our ambition is smaller than it
  is.
- **AE is not a defensive brand.** Where the contract needs a boundary line
  today, it is a fact stated once — and a line we intend to make obsolete — never
  a personality we perform.
