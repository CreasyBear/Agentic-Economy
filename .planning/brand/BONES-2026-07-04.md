# AE BRAND BONES — locked 2026-07-04 (grill session)

Supersedes the old trust-led strategic layer and defensive brand posture.
Eucalyptus survives; seal, old voice, old messaging, and old story do not.

## 1. What kind of brand
**An ACTION brand, not a trust brand.** Trust (receipts, written threads, stamps) is
plumbing — brakes in a Porsche. The brand is momentum: the distance between asking
and done, collapsing.

## 2. North star (internal, felt not said)
AE plants its flag on the **transact / market-making rung**: WE WILL BUILD THE
DOMESTIC AGENTIC ECONOMY. WE WILL FACILITATE AGENTIC DECISION MAKING.
Sharpened, founder-confirmed 2026-07-05: **WE WILL BOOK. WE WILL CHARGE.
WE WILL CONFIRM.** That is what "done" means.

**INTERNAL FLAG — never public copy (council convergence, 2026-07-05,
`COUNCIL-CONVERGENCE-2026-07-05.md`).** The triple above is founder-voice
conviction and internal orientation only — it does not appear on any public
or campaign surface, manifesto included. Public and campaign copy carries the
destination in the **promise register** instead: plain confidence, household
language, the enemy named through the lived afternoon — "show, don't
announce." A challenger with an empty registry earns swagger by shipping, not
shouting. Product surfaces continue to state the shipped rung as plain fact
(no claiming booking/charging before it ships). Vision staging:
`.planning/vision/2026-07-04-PLATFORM-ANATOMY.md`. "Domestic trade" as a
phrase is banned — economist language.

## 3. Brand hero — one brand, two doors (council convergence, 2026-07-05)
**The household's "it's sorted" moment.** The customer's exhale when the thing
is handled — in writing. **Co-lead:** the business-owner promise — no lead
fees, own your page, everything in writing protects the owner too — is not
sales collateral, it is elevated to co-lead brand hero (`COUNCIL-CONVERGENCE-
2026-07-05.md` #2; Sam: "the only pitch that's ever gotten my attention out of
every one of these platforms"). One brand, two doors — the household and the
owner — one record that serves both.

## 4. The enemy
**The halfway.** AI search gets you a list, then abandons you with all the real
work: deciding, contacting, chasing, agreeing. "Best businesses in your area" is
where ChatGPT stops and where AE starts. The agentic economy is a broken promise
until answers become action.

## 5. Voice
**Visionary swagger — internal/founder register only** (council convergence,
2026-07-05, §2 above). Future-tense conviction, first-person plural
declarations, zero hedging — the internal and founder-voice default, never
the public one. **Public and campaign copy, including the manifesto, uses
the promise register instead:** plain confidence, household language, the
enemy named through the lived afternoon — "show, don't announce," not war
declarations or challenger jabs. On product surfaces the conviction stays
but nouns get concrete (the swagger of a receipt is exactness and momentum,
not adjectives) — that discipline is unchanged. Never: defensive caveats as
personality, marketing hollow-words (unique/revolutionary/world-class), "In
conclusion".

## 6. Name architecture — ROUND-2 RE-RUN (council convergence, 2026-07-05)
Unanimous council reject of "Aeco" (3–4/10, all six members —
`COUNCIL-CONVERGENCE-2026-07-05.md`): reads eco/echo before economy, needs a
pronunciation guide, the AECO Energy collision was never screened, category
compression means nothing to a household, and the verb is assigned rather
than earned. **"Aeco" is demoted from founder-locked root to a prior
candidate** — eligible on the round-2 shortlist, not pre-selected.
- **Category compression is retired as the naming mandate.** `Agentic
  Economy` already owns the category claim at the corporate level; the
  consumer name's only job is the household mouth, in the register the
  council unanimously kept — "Ask once. It gets sorted."
- **Round-2 brief:** an outcome-energy word in the master-promise register
  (the "sorted" family), zero pronunciation ambiguity, pub-test clean,
  verb-able, AU-comfortable. Filters that still apply: ≤3 syllables,
  pub-test spellable, domain-pragmatic at shortlist stage.
  If the shortlist returns to an AECO-derived form, re-screen the known
  collisions: Architecture/Engineering/Construction/Operations industry
  acronym, the Alberta gas hub, and AECO Energy (AU-operating, moderate).
- **Agentic Economy** = company/category claim; stays as the corporate frame
  regardless of the round-2 outcome.

## 7. Visual carryover
- Eucalyptus #40614F stays as the accent. Palette (Ink/Canvas/white cards) stays.
- **The octagonal AE seal is dead.** Replacement mark to be designed from the
  action-brand posture: a mark that reads as *economy being built / motion /
  completion*, not a trust badge.
- Grotesk + mono-as-record type system survives review but serves swagger now.

## 8. Wedge posture
Beyond services — the wedge is **domestic economies** broadly. Data model stays
wedge-agnostic. AU first, one metro, but the brand is built for the whole domestic
economy, not a trades directory.
