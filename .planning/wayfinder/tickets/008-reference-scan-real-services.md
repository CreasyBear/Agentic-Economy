# Reference scan of real services/platforms

- Type: research
- Status: closed
- Assignee: Main
- Blocked by: none (frontier; graduated from user steer)

## Question

What real services/platforms that people already use are meaningful parallels for
AE — across local-services marketplaces, answer engines with citations, registries,
receipt/trust objects, warm-but-strict brand language, and comparison surfaces?
For each: the one pattern to borrow and the one thing to reject; then the north
stars and anti-references.

## Answer

Detail: [`.planning/brand/REFERENCES.md`](../../brand/REFERENCES.md).

**North stars.** Perplexity — citation-first, every claim beside source/freshness.
Stripe receipts/dashboard — unique IDs, itemized payload, timestamps, parties, event
timeline for the inquiry receipt. Google Flights — fast explainable comparison
without pretending to own price/availability/fulfillment. Companies House — dated
record gravity (named fields, source hierarchy) without government cosplay.

**Anti-references.** Angi/hipages/Bark lead economics (no hidden multi-sold leads);
Booking.com urgency theater (no manufactured scarcity/implied availability); generic
AI source-washing (no decorative citations without a durable proof object).

**Sharpest insight.** AE = **"the receipt-backed handoff layer," not "AI that finds
local services."** Sourced facts become one bounded, auditable inquiry.
