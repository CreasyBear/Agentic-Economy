# T36 — Durable identity for durable projects

Labels: `wayfinder:grilling` (HITL). Map: [Framework](../MAP-framework.md).

## Question

A months-long project cannot be owned by a pseudonymous session cookie. Decide the demand-side
identity model: when does a project require an account (at creation? at first lock? at first spend?),
what carries continuity across devices (Clerk person accounts? magic-link claim like the existing
saved-Request device flow?), how anonymous try-before-account works and migrates, household/shared
access (two partners on one wedding), and how identity binds to the spine exit contract
(projectId/generation) and mandates. Blocks T33 cutover; informs T30 re-entry views.
