# T8 — ICP and positioning: agent SEO for local businesses

Labels: `wayfinder:grilling` (HITL). Status: **resolved 2026-07-30 by founder decision** (interjections during the round-5/6 loop).

## Question

Who, specifically, is AE talking to on each side, and what is the one-line position?

## Evidence gathered

- agentic.market sellers = developers with APIs; motivation is revenue with zero billing plumbing (x402 per-call USDC). Their seller path 404'd during the round-5 comparator; AE beat them 4/5 on the driver job.
- Poncho (tryponcho.com) = demand-side aggregation for agent users: "3000+ tools, one account, skip setup/subscriptions/API keys."
- Soar (flysoar.ai) = one business that made itself agent-native (MCP; public search keyless, OAuth to book). Not a platform — the exemplar.
- Round-5 blind comparator: AE lost 2/5 to Airbnb on the rider (consumer web) job — craft/trust/mobile. The consumer-destination-site fight is likely the wrong fight.

## Candidate answer (prepared brief — decision stays with founder)

- **Driver ICP:** local service SMBs with no API (dentist, plumber, accountant). Offer = agent SEO + AE-hosted instant-price endpoint. Copy is reader-first and concrete: "be found by AI assistants", "answer with a price, even at 2am".
- **Rider ICP:** assistants (ChatGPT/Claude/agents) acting for people — served via llms.txt, /api/v1/services, MCP (T6). The human web view becomes the owner's mirror + the assistant's hand-off landing page, not a browse destination.
- **Position:** "Agentic Economy makes local businesses agent-native. When AI assistants shop for their humans, your business gets found — and answers with a price."
- **Consequences if adopted:** T6 and T5 jump the queue; consumer-web polish loop re-aims at the claim funnel + hand-off page; T7 (agentic.market catalog import) moves out of scope (their supply is APIs, off-ICP); public copy sweep removes marketplace/booking baggage.

## Resolution

Adopted by founder: position = "AE makes local businesses agent-native — found by AI assistants, answering with a price." Driver ICP = local no-API SMBs; rider = assistants (llms.txt/API/MCP), with the human site as owner's mirror + hand-off page. The UI/UX/IA must support this: the directory wall of services is the enemy — `/` becomes positioning hero (owner funnel) + an ask-box answer engine (top 1–3 answers), executed in loop round 6. Copy is reader-first with concrete outcomes. T7 (agentic.market catalog import) moves out of scope as off-ICP.
