## Scope and Coverage

The review covers UX continuity, visual hierarchy, information architecture, cognitive load, token ownership, mode coherence, state/feedback, accessibility seams, and performance-in-design.

This is a source review rather than rendered certification. **Not inspected:** browser screenshots, screen-reader output, measured contrast ratios, 320px/200% zoom behavior, emitted route bundles, and live authentication/readiness behavior. No tests, linters, builds, or source changes were run.

## Verdict summary

The system has an unusually good product spine: the warm canvas (`--ae-bg`), near-black action ink (`--ae-fg`/`--ae-primary`), one semantic blue (`--ae-brand`), role-pure `--ae-font-sans`/`--ae-font-mono`/`--ae-font-display`, and the small `8 → 16 → 32 → 48 → 72 → 96` spacing ladder give it a clear point of view. `DESIGN.md` and `.planning/BRAND.md` correctly make the market, not a generic dashboard, the product. Square rows, hairlines, aligned values, restrained motion, and explicit evidence are stronger than decorative “terminal” styling.

The canonical Operation model is also strong. `toOperationInspectorModel` is shared by compact and full inspectors (`src/components/ae/market/operation-detail/operation-inspector-model.ts:32-85`), and the full view makes identity, maximum authorization, readiness, observed evidence, exact contract/effects, and one continuation legible (`AeOperationInspector.tsx:99-147`, `AeOperationContinuation.tsx:30-72`). Route and chat states generally preserve honest pending/refused/outcome-unknown semantics rather than claiming success.

The remaining quality gap is at the seams: the same Operation can receive different access decisions depending on where it was opened; evidence outages read like a valid lack of activity; entry links say “Use” while navigating to inspection; the home-to-market path is not actually search-first; and shared defaults still allow public type, Operator grammar, primitive tokens, and third-party/auth styling to leak across modes. These are not aesthetic preferences. They weaken the user’s confidence at the exact moment a buyer decides whether to authorize a paid outside result.

## Verification

Checked the complete map set: `.planning/design-system/FOUNDATIONS.md`, `COMPONENTS.md`, `LAYOUT.md`, `PATTERNS.md`, `CONVENTIONS.md`, `ACCESSIBILITY.md`, and `CONCERNS.md`; the governing `DESIGN.md` and `.planning/BRAND.md`; `src/styles/globals.css`, `src/styles/base.css`, and `components.json`; and the representative current sources named in Scope and Coverage.

Confirmed the named tokens and contracts: `--ae-bg`, `--ae-fg`, `--ae-primary`, `--ae-brand`, `--ae-success`, `--ae-warning`, `--ae-danger`, `--ae-font-sans`, `--ae-font-mono`, `--ae-font-display`, `--radius`, `--radius-card`, `--radius-nav`, `--ae-touch`, `--ae-focus-ring`, `--ae-overlay-scrim`, `--shadow-soft`, `--shadow-overlay`, `duration-fast/base/slow`, `ease-standard/emphasized`, `ae-nav`, and `ae-rail`. Confirmed the five-step Operation order, public/market/operator mode rules, cache-aware state vocabulary, reduced-motion fallback, 44px convention, and explicit evidence wording against source.

**Not verified:** rendered contrast and visual balance; screen-reader announcements and focus return; 320px/200% zoom and touch geometry; live auth credential presence; emitted bundle sizes, route chunking, or the magnitude of hydration reflow. Those require a browser/runtime or bundle-analysis pass and are not treated as observed runtime facts here.

## Recommendations (priority order)

1. Unify credential presence and unavailable states across `AePublicShell`, `CommandPanelProvider`, the operation route, and both inspector variants. This is the highest-risk continuity break.
2. Preserve evidence read failures as `unavailable`, not `empty`; add in-place retry and explicit `status`/`alert` semantics.
3. Rename catalogue/chat entry actions to `Inspect`/`View Operation`; keep call language inside the actual continuation.
4. Make `/` genuinely search-first and reduce Market’s first decision to one task, one search, or one grouped browse control. Keep the row/table relationship and reveal secondary facts progressively.
5. Encode explicit Market and Operator mode variants: sans Market heading, inset Operator work well, ordinary operational labels.
6. Close the primitive contract: central focus/touch/reduced-motion behavior, tokenized alert-dialog, explicit empty-state role, and an intentional Clerk/theme-meta bridge.
7. Measure the operation-detail route and defer the single-feature chart; remove the hydration-driven layout reflow before adding more data visualizations.
