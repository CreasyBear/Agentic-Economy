# Typography Review — Agentic Economy Design System
**Analysis Date:** 2026-09-01
**Lens/Skill:** better-typography — `.claude/skills/better-typography/SKILL.md`
**Scope:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` (Typography), `CONVENTIONS.md` (token-first, copy/value roles, density), `LAYOUT.md` (responsive density and content measure), and `CONCERNS.md` (typography drift, dependencies, performance); verified against `DESIGN.md`, `.planning/BRAND.md`, `src/styles/globals.css`, `src/styles/base.css`, `package.json`, installed Fontsource CSS, and targeted `src/components/ae/`, `src/components/ui/`, and route callsites. I did not inspect every text node or run a browser rendering, test, build, or asset-request measurement.

## Scope and Coverage
The review covers family pairing and delivery, the declared and observed type scale, heading hierarchy, weight and tracking discipline, numeric and identifier treatment, measure and wrapping, truncation, and Geist Pixel containment across Public, Market, and Operator modes. The source review spot-checked shared typography owners (`AePageHeader`, `AeSiteType`, `AeSection`, `AeFactList`, `Table`, and `AeCopyCommand`) plus representative market, operation-detail, supplier, and operator surfaces. Semantic heading/a11y correctness belongs to the accessibility lens; color-pair measurement belongs to the colors lens.

## Verdict summary
The direction is coherent and recognisable: Inter Variable is a strong interface/prose face, DM Mono gives prices and identifiers a credible market-terminal register, and Geist Pixel is a distinctive public-only display face when used through `AeSiteHeading`. The public heading scale in `AeSiteType.tsx:18-24`, 65ch prose measures in `AeSiteType.tsx:48-58`, `AeSection.tsx:34-36`, and `AeRecordHeader.tsx:48-49`, the `1.5` body line-height in `src/styles/base.css:29-35`, and global `text-wrap: balance` for headings in `src/styles/globals.css:298-313` are good foundations. Fontsource imports use compressed web faces with `font-display: swap`, and the base numeric recipe is explicit.

The system needs changes before it is a dependable typography contract. The highest-confidence issues are the public display face leaking into the Market header, operational labels using uppercase tracking by default, no shared product type-size scale outside the public helper, requested font weights that are not delivered by DM Mono or Geist Pixel, and inconsistent enforcement of mono/tabular treatment for aligned values. One operation decision heading also overrides the global balance rule with `text-pretty`.

- Confirmed delivery declarations: `package.json:77-79` pins Fontsource 5.3.0 packages; Inter Variable declares `font-display: swap`, variable `font-weight: 100 900`, and WOFF2 variation files; DM Mono and Geist Pixel declare WOFF2 plus legacy WOFF fallback with `font-display: swap`. DM Mono's imported declarations are 400/500 and Geist Pixel's index is 400 only.
- **Not verified:** browser-rendered hierarchy, actual line wrapping at 320px and 200% zoom, glyph fallback/synthesis in each browser, emitted CSS/font request counts, or dynamic-value layout stability while live prices/counters update. Those checks should follow the fixes, especially for the Market header, long Operation titles, and numeric columns.

## Recommendations (priority order)
1. Add an explicit Market typography decision and remove the `AePageHeader` default display leak; independently normalize Operator navigation labels to ordinary case and tracking.
2. Define the cross-surface semantic type/leading roles in the existing `globals.css`/`@theme inline` bridge. Keep the public `AeSiteHeading` scale, but make heading defaults and shared meta/code sizes reusable rather than caller-defined.
3. Align requested weights with delivered Fontsource faces. Standardize Geist Pixel to 400 and DM Mono to its loaded 400/500 range unless a real supported face is intentionally added.
4. Make one numeric/value contract authoritative. Apply mono plus tabular figures to aligned prices, identifiers, counts, timestamps, and money; remove blanket tabular treatment from non-numeric facts and either use `[data-numeric]` or update the convention to the chosen utility.
5. Replace the operation decision `text-pretty` heading override with `text-balance`, and stop truncating the exact Operation title without a full-value path. Verify real long labels at narrow widths.
6. Measure the root stylesheet's emitted font CSS and actual subset requests before deciding whether Fontsource index imports need pruning or route-level delivery.
