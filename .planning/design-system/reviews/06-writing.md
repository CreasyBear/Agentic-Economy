# Writing Review — Agentic Economy Design System
**Analysis Date:** 2026-09-01
**Lens/Skill:** better-writing — `.claude/skills/better-writing/SKILL.md`
**Scope:** `CONVENTIONS.md` (Copy and voice, Component states, Density and surface rules), `PATTERNS.md` (Operation inspect and call, Evidence and provenance, Market browse/search/compare, Command panel, Console/credit/account work, Forms, Loading/empty/error/recovery), `LAYOUT.md` (Canonical Operation composition, Responsive behavior, Content rail and density), plus `BRAND.md` (Messaging rules and Voice), `DESIGN.md` (canonical Operation composition and hard constraints), and real copy in market, operation-detail, command-panel, console, feedback, layout, settings, and related route/state files. I did not inspect every website, supplier, or chat copy path, and did not run browser, localization, test, lint, or build checks.

## Scope and Coverage
The review applies the better-writing rules to the mapped design system rather than to a single page: direct/useful/calm voice, concrete nouns, verb-first actions, destination-specific links, explicit empty/error/loading/recovery copy, one capitalization policy, and evidence-language discipline. The locked brand vocabulary is `Catalog`, `Operation`, `Supplier`, `Price`, `Ready now`, `Calls`, `Latency`, `Rating`, `Activity`, `Usage`, `Access`, and `Publish`; `Operation` is the transaction noun and `tool` is only a familiar browse word.

I spot-checked the live implementation of `AeMarketPage`, `AeMarketToolbar`, `AeOperationCard`, `AeOperationTable`, `AeMarketComparisonView`, the full and compact operation inspectors and contract sections, command-panel search/inspect pages, `AeCreditTopUpPanel`, `AeOwnerCredit`, `AeAgentOperatorConsole`, `AeWorkspaceGeneral`, shared empty/route/error states, and the canonical market evidence/status projections. The strongest positive pattern is the existing evidence-aware language: `30 days · AE-observed`, `Unrated`, `No ratings yet`, `No completed calls yet`, `Not reported`, and `What you can do next`.

## Verdict summary
The design system has a clear verbal direction and mostly follows it. `BRAND.md`'s short, concrete examples are visible in `AeMarketPage.tsx:54-55` (`Inspect price, access, and readiness without an account. Only available Operations can be called.`), `AeOperationContinuation.tsx:70-72` (`Authorization covers one bounded result. Completion and quality appear only when AE observes evidence.`), and the search empty state that names the query and supplies `Clear search` and `Browse current Operations`. The mapped patterns correctly insist on one exact Operation, one valid next action, and explicit evidence classes.

The blockers are semantic, not cosmetic. An evidence-read failure is rendered as zero activity, a price observation can be called `Last verified`, and a successful payment is titled `Payment verified` while the body says credit was not granted. Several public errors expose implementation vocabulary (`descriptor`, `commercial facts`, `canonical ledger readback`, `catalogue`, `references were refused`), while a number of state branches either offer a futile retry or omit a recovery action. Terminology and action labels also drift between `Catalog`/`catalogue`, `Operation`/`tool`, and `Open`/`Use`/`Inspect`.

## Recommendations (priority order)
1. Fix the two evidence boundaries first: preserve `evidence_unavailable` separately from genuine no-sample data, and split readiness/price observations instead of displaying a merged `Last verified` value.
2. Change the payment success state to state the remaining credit-readback boundary before any release; keep the explicit no-duplicate-payment instruction and make the retry/reconcile control match the state.
3. Remove implementation vocabulary from public errors and money status copy. Reuse the existing `ui-state.ts`, `status-presentation.ts`, and `brand-copy.ts` seams rather than adding a second copy system.
4. Establish one copy checklist for every new state: what is absent, what evidence exists, whether it is stale/unavailable, and the one next valid action. Apply it to no-result, inspect-unavailable, partial-charge, settings, and payment-form branches.
5. Standardize `Catalog`, `Operation`, and the exact `Maximum authorization` label across full, compact, table, route, command-panel, and metadata surfaces; make visible and accessible action labels match the destination.
6. Replace raw settings enums, internal references, `Next 12`, and `loaded page` with human labels and concrete actions; finish the low-severity grammar and dialog-title cleanup after the semantic changes.

## Open questions for the owner
- Is `availability.observedAt` an AE readiness observation, a qualification check, or another evidence class? The answer determines whether the UI may ever say `verified`, and which source label must accompany it.
- When payment evidence is successful but the canonical credit readback is pending, should the approved product state be `Payment confirmed; credit balance pending`, `Payment received`, or another exact phrase?
- Should an Operation row/card that opens inspection always say `Inspect`, or should the product intentionally retain `Use` for routeable entries and add an explicit “opens details” cue?
- Is `Catalog` the required spelling in every public, metadata, operator, and accessible string, with `catalogue` retired completely?

**Verdict:** Block
