1. Does the current customer product make one truthful contract agree across

2. Which one real request family and independently useful task should AE invest

[Rebuild the public brand from proven customer value](https://github.com/CreasyBear/Agentic-Economy/issues/132).
The second begins the field-evidence frontier for
[Choose the first valuable task and participating cohorts](https://github.com/CreasyBear/Agentic-Economy/issues/182).
They must not be collapsed: contract agreement is engineering proof; observed
burden reduction is customer-value proof.

## Operating model

### Lane A — finish the claimed customer-product development gates

Work one earliest failing transition at a time on the exact issue branch.

#### A1. Gate 4: emitted-contract agreement

Entry is permitted only after both Gate 3 cohorts have:

- an exact revision and clean scoped checkout;
- a checksummed proof artifact;
- equal frozen customer facts and provider set;
- matching cost constraints and exact totals;
- least-data, evidence, result, replay, interruption, and recovery checks;
- a `pass_for_declared_class` verdict with no hidden operator step.

Those conditions are recorded for procurement and itinerary. Before Gate 4
execution, rerun the focused Gate 3 regression suite against the exact chosen
Gate 4 base revision. If integration of concurrent work changes any owned seam,
the Gate 3 comparison must be rerun rather than inherited.

Gate 4 begins with one red source-owned evaluator. From one exact development
origin and revision it must read:

- `/`;
- `/SKILL.md`;
- `/llms.txt`;
- agent discovery;
- Request schemas and registered action descriptors;
- human and agent projections of the same Request;
- executed Request observations.

It compares public origin, current supported actions, required inputs,
authentication and authority, idempotency, refusal and downstream-failure
behavior, customer-visible states, businesses and choice, progress, result
digest, evidence, recovery, and current supply/readiness.

The loop is:

1. Write the red eval at the highest real development seam.
2. Execute it and record the earliest disagreement.
3. Change the smallest source-owned producer of that disagreement.
4. Rerun the focused eval plus affected unit, integration, copy, schema,
   accessibility, type, lint, build, replay, and recovery checks.
5. Review against both the issue specification and repository standards.
6. Commit one green vertical slice and update the claimed issue without closing
   it.
7. Repeat until the complete agreement matrix passes from one exact origin and
   revision.

Gate 4 fails on one contradiction. An average or partial pass cannot hide an
unreachable action, stale origin, unsupported write, missing recovery action,
human/agent disagreement, stale supply, or prohibited fulfilment claim.

Blast radius is limited to public contract emitters, action metadata, Request
schemas/projections, the development evaluator, and the smallest source seam
that owns a detected mismatch. It does not authorize a new lifecycle, provider
integration, product claim, broad UI redesign, production configuration, or
deployment.

#### A2. Gate 5: cold comprehension and completion

Start only after Gate 4 passes in development. Use five cold human reviewers and
three isolated cold external-agent sessions. Simulation may generate
adversarial cases but cannot count as a reviewer.

Every reviewer is scored independently on:

1. when AE is useful;
2. what AE currently supports;
3. what happens before commitment;
4. what remains under customer control;
5. what happened or remains unknown;
6. the safe next action.

Every human must answer all six materially correctly. Every agent must discover,
resume the same Request, and avoid private protocol assumptions. Any reviewer
who promotes sandbox evidence into real supply, fulfilment, or customer value is
a hard failure. Record microfrustrations by transition, severity,
reproducibility, and disposition; fix release-blocking defects through the same
red-eval loop.

Gate 5 is product-comprehension evidence on the committed development surface.
It is not independent-provider or production proof.

#### A3. Gate 6: production wave

Remain blocked until Gates 1–5 pass and the founder explicitly authorizes a
production wave. Inclusion in this plan is not authorization. No production
deployment, credential provisioning, or hosted claim is allowed beforehand.

## Source patterns to preserve

The implementation works with existing deep seams instead of layering another
lifecycle:

- Registered-action truth remains behind `ActionDefinition`,
  `describeActionForAgent`, and the central action registry.
- The public Request contract remains the canonical emitted contract; Gate 4
  normalizes other surfaces and runtime observations against its source-owned
  tuple rather than maintaining a second expected matrix.
- Human and agent Request observations continue to come from the canonical
  customer projection and existing cross-surface journey seams.
- Qualified inquiry is the first plausible standalone consequential action
  because it already has a registered action and attributable delivery. It is
  not wrapped globally at the registry: a future Action Invocation module
  accepts the action handler as a dependency, and only a caller explicitly
  starting or resuming an invocation crosses that seam.
- Request-owned preparation, authority, attempts, evidence, and recovery are
  analogs or adapters, not optional-lineage storage for standalone work.
- Hosts own transport, conversation, and rendering only. Consequence,
  preparation, authority, retry, evidence, and recovery semantics have one
  source-owned derivation.

Deletion tests govern the design:

- deleting Action Invocation should force authority, version, retry, and
  recovery logic to spread into multiple hosts; otherwise the module is a
  pass-through and should not exist;
- deleting a host adapter must not remove business rules;
- adding a conformant provider changes registered contracts or an adapter, not
  the Request kernel, caller prompt, or customer lifecycle;
- a direct path must remain direct when AE adds no coordination value.

The current Phase 1 and Phase 2 plans are design-complete, not
implementation-ready. Their source citations and seam assumptions must be
refreshed after the active preparation-egress/god-module changes. Phase 2
depends on implemented Phase 1 even though its current plan frontmatter does not
encode that dependency.

## Vertical implementation pattern

Every engineering slice uses the same loop:

1. Claim one tracker ticket and freeze the exact revision, cohort/case, public
   seam, expected observation, claim ceiling, and falsifier.
2. Write one failing eval through the agreed public interface.
3. Execute it and name the earliest failed transition.
4. Change the smallest source owner of that transition.
5. Run the narrow verification first, then expand in proportion to the crossed
   boundaries.
6. Execute the real development or intended surface for that evidence class.
7. Review against both the governing specification and repository standards.
8. Commit one green vertical slice, checksum the evidence artifact, and update
   the issue without overstating the proof class.

Do not implement all interfaces, storage, projections, and tests horizontally.
The smallest later Phase 1 tracer is:

`prepare -> inspect awaiting authority -> authorize exact version/digest ->
continue inquiry.submit -> inspect attributable terminal or unknown result`.

It first uses an in-memory adapter and must also prove material-change refusal
and reconcile-before-retry. Persistence is a separate tracer behind the same
interface and is earned only after the interface eval passes.

The smallest Phase 2 tracer resumes that same paused invocation once through a
human host and once through a cold external-agent host. It proves identity,
version, authority, known/unknown state, evidence, continuations and recovery
before adding broader projection families. Option comparison waits for the
selected real task if qualified inquiry does not genuinely need it.

## Proof ladder and claim ceilings

No blended “done” score is allowed. Each evidence class has an independent
gate, falsifier, and claim ceiling.

### 1. Contract agreement

Pass when Gate 4 observes one exact revision and origin with zero contradictions
across `/`, `/SKILL.md`, `/llms.txt`, discovery, schemas, action descriptors,
human and agent projections, and an executed Request. The retained artifact
names the exact revision/origin, observations, failures, verdict, claim
boundary, and checksum.

Falsifier: agreement requires a second lifecycle, authority, recovery, or
recommendation model, or an advertised behavior is unreachable. Fix the
smallest owning producer; if another lifecycle is required, stop and redesign.

Claim ceiling: source-owned contract agreement on the labelled development
surface.

### 2. Cold comprehension

Pass only when five genuinely cold humans and three isolated external-agent
sessions all complete or truthfully stop without teaching or operator
choreography. Every human answers all six Gate 5 prompts materially correctly;
every agent discovers from the public origin and resumes the same Request; no
participant inflates the evidence class; no release blocker remains.

Falsifier: teaching, privileged source/database access, lost Request identity,
hidden operator intervention, or evidence inflation. Fix the earliest
transition. Repeated conceptual misunderstanding narrows the promise or flow.

Claim ceiling: comprehensible committed development product.

### 3. Independently operated supply

Pass for one named cohort only when participating businesses independently
operate the required roles, maintain the exact information/actions used by the
product, expose attributable readiness/evidence, and fail closed when stale or
unavailable. The cohort must name an accountable field owner and measured
maintenance window.

Falsifier: insufficient recruitable coverage, facts/actions are not maintained,
freshness cannot be evidenced, or AE/sandbox wrappers are required to pretend
the supply is independent. Choose another task, narrow to information-only,
operate deliberately as a service, or stop.

Claim ceiling: participating real supply for the named cohort.

### 4. Customer value

Measure the incumbent baseline before preregistering the threshold. The
provisional general bar is at least a 30% reduction in the primary active
coordination burden without worse correctness, elapsed time, cost, privacy, or
customer control. A workflow-specific threshold replaces this only after its
baseline is measured. The direct negative control must remain simpler.

Falsifier: threshold missed, serious requirement or authority omission,
parallel tracker remains necessary, non-regression bound fails, or AE burdens a
direct task. Refine once for a bounded product defect; otherwise narrow, select
the next candidate, operate as a service explicitly, or stop.

Claim ceiling: measured value for the exact task, cohort, and evidence window.

### 5. Provider value

Pass when real participating businesses receive better-formed work with less
clarification, re-keying, and response effort than the measured incumbent path,
while retaining the customer relationship and without acquiring another hidden
inbox or process. Set numeric thresholds only after the baseline.

Falsifier: providers reconstruct the Request, clarification or re-keying rises,
relationship/control shifts to AE, or hidden AE labor makes participation work.
Fix a repeated provider-contract gap, narrow the action, service-operate
explicitly, or stop.

Claim ceiling: measured provider value for the participating cohort.

### 6. Operator leverage

Pass when customer/provider savings are not shifted into equal or greater AE
backstage work. Record manual touches, exceptions, support minutes, hidden
corrections, and cost to serve per case.

Falsifier: operator work equals or exceeds the burden removed, or hidden
concierge choreography is required. Automate only a repeated evidenced
mechanism, price/service-operate deliberately, narrow, or stop.

Claim ceiling: measured operating leverage, not platform leverage.

### 7. Transfer and platform leverage

Pass when a materially different second workflow reuses the same lifecycle and
trust modules through contracts, policies, adapters, and shared modules, with
lower measured marginal creation cost and no parallel intent, persistence,
authority, evidence, or recovery system. A direct transaction receives no
unnecessary invocation or approval.

Falsifier: the second case needs bespoke orchestration, a wedge-named common
object, breaks earlier replay, or burdens the direct path. Keep the behavior
wedge-local, narrow/supersede the architecture, or stop.

Claim ceiling: demonstrated transfer across the named workflows.

### 8. Production proof

This gate is absent until the founder explicitly authorizes it. If authorized,
it requires one clean exact commit and deployment, compatibility gates,
deployment and revision readback, observability, cold human and external-agent
completion/resume, rejection plus interruption/unknown recovery, credential
cleanup, and claims rewritten to the exact achieved class.

Falsifier: revision drift, localhost substituted for hosted evidence, missing
telemetry/readback, temporary credentials remain, or sandbox evidence is
promoted. Roll back or hold claims.

## Success conditions

The no-production milestone succeeds only when all of the following are true:

1. Gate 4 passes from one exact revision and origin with retained checksummed
   evidence.
2. Gate 5 passes its full eight-reviewer contract with zero evidence inflation,
   hidden choreography, or unresolved release blocker.
3. One task and its customer/coordinator and business cohorts are selected from
   recent real cases with a measured incumbent baseline, completion boundary,
   direct control, accountable owners, and preregistered falsifiers.
4. Several real businesses validate the provider contract, participation
   posture, and maintenance burden.
5. Customer, provider, operator, continuation, recovery, direct-control,
   transfer, and security evals return an explicit invest/narrow/refine/service
   or stop decision.
6. The architecture issue records the selected seam, rejected alternatives,
   migration/rollback, threat controls, test matrix, blast radius, and ADR
   disposition.
7. The task-first handoff issue approves one bounded implementation package or
   records a clear stop/narrow/refine decision, including material dissent.
8. If implementation is approved, refreshed Phase 1 vertical slices prove
   exact per-action authority, material invalidation, standalone and
   Request-owned entry, interruption, reconcile-before-retry, stale-generation
   protection, restart, and direct-control behavior.
9. Refreshed Phase 2 vertical slices prove seven-dimension human/agent semantic
   parity, structured equivalence, host import boundaries, reconstruction
   without transcript truth, and cold resume. Deferred gates are executed or
   explicitly excluded from this milestone, never silently skipped.
10. The exact final development revision passes proportionate source, type,
    import, Convex, integration, copy, UI/browser, replay/recovery, build, and
    two-axis review gates, and every public/internal claim is restricted to its
    evidence class.

## End conditions

The goal ends in one of four truthful states:

- **Invest:** the value, supply, operating, transfer, security, and
  implementation gates pass; the bounded product is ready for a separately
  authorized production decision.
- **Narrow:** evidence supports a smaller task, information-only product, or
  selected cohort; the broader claims and phases are explicitly removed.
- **Operate deliberately as a service:** customer/provider value exists but
  automation or platform leverage does not; human operations are named,
  costed, and presented honestly.
- **Stop:** the value, supply, safety, or leverage falsifier holds after the
  allowed bounded refinement; the project records why further substrate work is
  not justified.

“Stop,” “narrow,” and “service” are evidence-backed completions, not failed
goals. An open-ended backlog, green source tests, a demo, a simulation, a
fixture, or an unvalidated architecture is not completion.
