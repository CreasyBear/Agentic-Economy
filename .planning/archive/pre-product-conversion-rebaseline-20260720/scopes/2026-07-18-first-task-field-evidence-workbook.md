# First-task field evidence workbook

**Tracker:** Wayfinder #182  
**Protocol:** [First-task field-selection protocol](../research/2026-07-18-first-task-field-selection-protocol.md)  
**Field governance:** [WA consent, privacy, recording, and outreach protocol](../research/2026-07-18-wa-field-study-consent-privacy-recording-protocol.md)  
**Participant materials:** [Recruitment, notice, consent, session, and withdrawal drafts](./2026-07-18-first-task-participant-materials.md)
**Field owner:** Unassigned  
**Status:** Ready for owner assignment; no participants recruited  
**Evidence class:** External field only when completed from consented real cases  
**Evidence window:** To be declared before recruitment  
**Decision owner:** Founder

This workbook turns the field-selection protocol into a repeatable evidence
packet. Blank tables are instruments, not observations. Example, fixture,
synthetic, development, directory, or AE-operated entries must never be counted
as customer cases or independently operated business participation.

It supports two decisions that must not be collapsed:

1. **Stage A — #182 provisional selection:** choose the task and cohorts that
   earn the provider-contract and investment-eval gates. This requires real
   incumbent cases, cross-domain rejection evidence, a direct control,
   recruitability/freshness observations, and explicit value hypotheses. It
   does not require or claim AE-assisted customer or provider value.
2. **Stage B — post-trial disposition:** after a separately authorized
   AE-assisted field trial, compare actual outcomes and choose invest, refine,
   narrow, deliberate service, or stop. Stage B cannot be inferred from Stage A
   interviews or baselines.

## 12. Stage B — post-trial branch rules

| Branch             | Evidence rule                                                                                                                          | Required record                                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Invest             | All frozen customer, provider, operator, freshness, independent-supply, safety, direct-control, and transfer thresholds pass           | Exact supported task/cohort, residual risks, development/field claim ceiling, and Founder decision                    |
| Refine             | Evidence is incomplete or a bounded, testable task/cohort/measurement ambiguity remains without a safety failure                       | Named ambiguity, unchanged original results, one bounded re-test, owner, sample, deadline, and falsifier              |
| Narrow             | Evidence supports a smaller information, qualification, comparison, inquiry, cohort, or authority boundary and rejects the broader one | Supported smaller boundary, rejected behavior/claim, affected gates, and new thresholds                               |
| Deliberate service | Value exists only with recurring human work that is visible, owned, costed, and acceptable                                             | Human roles, minutes per case/month, cost, capacity, escalation, quality control, claim ceiling, and Founder approval |
| Stop               | A value, supply, freshness, safety, direct-control, transfer, or leverage falsifier holds and no bounded refinement would change it    | Supported falsifier, preserved evidence, cancelled/recharted downstream work, and Founder decision                    |

Only one Stage B branch may be selected. A branch cannot be chosen merely
because another branch lacks evidence. Refinement cannot weaken a consent,
safety, correctness, or authority threshold; deliberate service cannot hide
operator work as product automation.

## 14. Claim boundary

A completed workbook may evidence only the named population, task, locality,
period, direct control, and measurements actually captured. It does not by
itself prove production behavior, broader-market demand, independently operated
supply outside the evidenced participants, booking, payment, dispatch,
fulfilment, certification, statutory approval, ADR acceptance, or readiness to
implement Action Invocation.
