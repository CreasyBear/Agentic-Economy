## 1. The structural claim

**AE lowers the minimum viable size and raises the feasible complexity of market coordination between independent counterparties.**

Derivation (AgenticEconomyShape, Coase/Williamson-grounded): when search, bargaining, and enforcement costs collapse, minimum viable transaction value falls from `coordination cost + production cost` toward production cost alone — viable transaction count explodes far faster than transaction value. Firms don't disappear (physical risk, asset specificity, liability persist); boundaries go porous and temporary multi-party production units form per objective. The expanded market's binding need is verifiable identity, bounded authority, disputable evidence — the kernel, derived from economics rather than product taste.

The new production unit of an agentic economy is the **governed, machine-cleared action** — not the listing, lead, chat, or payment.

Australia test-bed fact (ABS, 30 June 2025): 2,729,648 actively trading businesses; **1,735,470 (63.6%) non-employing** — the population current coordination costs tax hardest.

## 2. The commitment lattice <!-- founder: 2026-07-13 -->

**Every agent action is a bounded commitment**, never a naked act:

```
commitment = {
  principal, delegationChain,
  intent (canonical, digested — K12),
  scope, expiry (K4), costCap, resourceCaps,
  revocation, evidenceObligations (K3)
}
```

**The lattice of outstanding commitments IS the market.** A demand-side mandate is a bid; a supply-side capacity stake is an ask; matching is the intersection of two commitment lattices. Price discovery, capacity allocation, and risk pricing become functions over live commitments, not over conversations.

This reframes K2 from defensive gate (permission checked at send) to **generative market primitive** (the information carrier of the agentic economy). Binding consequences:

<!-- economy-thesis: E-1 --> **E-1 · Symmetric commitment schema.** Demand mandates (K2) and supply capacity stakes (NativeBehaviors §3 "sole-trader capacity broadcast") are instances of ONE object class: same bounds vocabulary, expiry semantics, canonical digest, evidence obligations. Do not build two half-markets. Applies when the supply-stake object is first schematized (R2-era); K12 canonical encoding must not preclude it.

<!-- economy-thesis: E-2 --> **E-2 · Disclosure gradient is first-class.** For counterparties to adjust around a commitment, some projection must be visible; a fully visible commitment leaks intent (microstructure: order flow reveals information → adverse selection, front-running analogs). Commitments carry consent-gated projection levels: existence → category/area → bounds → full terms. This is K3's redaction-safe projection machinery generalized from historical evidence to LIVE commitments.

<!-- economy-thesis: E-3 --> **E-3 · Stake semantics without custody.** A lattice where posting and breaking commitments is free is a spam machine (cf. spoofing). AE's non-custodial skin-in-the-game is **K7 telemetry as reputational bond**: dishonored capacity stakes, expired-unanswered mandates, and non-converting principals accrue attributable, aged, evidence-backed history that the market adjusts around. The stake is the record. Preserves receipt≠outcome; requires no money movement.

<!-- economy-thesis: E-4 --> **E-4 · Sell the tape, never the rank.** Anonymized aggregate projections of the commitment lattice (demand density, cap distributions, response-latency by cohort) are a neutrality-compatible revenue class (exchange market-data precedent), consistent with `KERNEL-CEILING.md` C-5. Paid rank, lead resale, and placement remain kill conditions.

## 4. Frequency answer + behavior capture map (NativeBehaviors)

Unit discipline: count **coordination events** and **physical jobs** separately; conflating them is a thesis-killing error. Agent-native behaviors turn 2–6 jobs/yr/household into monthly-weekly machine events without necessarily raising job count.

- **Platform-captured — do not chase:** proactive detection, reordering, deal-watching, single-business booking, machine payments (Alexa+/Amazon, Meta, x402/Cloudflare own these).
- **AE-shaped (spans independent parties):** portfolio tenders, insurer↔policyholder↔trade dispatch, neighbourhood demand pooling, cross-assistant negotiation, warranty/compliance evidence chains (best kernel fit, weakest acquisition wedge).
- **Named R2-era candidate: expiring capacity stakes + always-on quote refresh.** Business signs short-lived availability assertion; consented customers receive scope-stable refreshed quotes; booking stays business-confirmed. Daily supply events; extends admitted registry + R1 lineage; exercises K4 temporal truth cross-party; instantiates E-1 symmetry. Gated behind R1 proof per WEDGE-LADDER — named here so R1/K12 schema decisions don't preclude it.

## 5. Falsifiers

1. **Frequency:** coordination events and physical jobs measured separately per active principal; thesis rejected if only the former grows and nobody pays for it.
2. **Cross-party necessity:** share of useful runs spanning ≥2 independent systems/principals. One-platform runs do not validate neutrality.
3. **Supply freshness:** % of capacity/quote assertions updated before expiry and honored (E-3 input).
4. **Governance value:** duplicates/refusals/drift prevented; records reopened/exported/cited; disputes replay-resolved (aligns KERNEL-CEILING §6.1).
5. **Capture test (rules them all):** one consumer assistant + one trade CRM + one independent business endpoint against ONE unchanged envelope. Proprietary per-party semantics ⇒ no neutral moat ⇒ AE is an adapter business; report it, don't rename it.

## 6. Is this AE? — the curriculum framing <!-- founder: 2026-07-13 -->

Three-part answer, all true simultaneously:

1. **Structurally AE.** R1 already IS the commitment object at n=1 (principal, delegation, canonical intent, scope, expiry, one-use cost, revocation, evidence). The lattice needs no different kernel, company, or repo.
2. **Not commercially AE-today.** A lattice is a market of standing commitments; markets cannot launch for instruments with zero literate participants. Building lattice surfaces now is exactly the future-surface cosplay WEDGE-LADDER bans.
3. **The bridge is an adoption curriculum, not a product roadmap.** Precedent: BankAmericard (single-city Fresno charge card, 1958) preceded "Visa the network"; Chicago bilateral grain forwards preceded standardized futures by decades. Standardized markets EMERGE from accumulated bilateral practice; they are never launched ahead of it.

<!-- economy-thesis: E-5 --> **E-5 · Rungs are literacy stages.** Each rung's release precondition is the previous rung's trust having actually formed in the relevant party — customer (bounded delegation is safe), business (answering agents pays; the record protects them too), economy (commitments are reliable enough to rely on). Rung-gate arguments MUST be made in literacy terms (measured behavior of real parties), not feature-readiness terms. Persona re-run gates and WEDGE-LADDER release gates are the measuring instruments.

<!-- economy-thesis: E-6 --> **E-6 · R1 surfaces teach the grammar.** Every R1 interaction is a service AND a lesson. Surfaces make bounds FELT, not hidden: expiry visibly honored, revocation visibly instant, the record visibly answering a later question. These moments are the curriculum that creates demand for standing commitments (R2+). Consistent with de-hedge rules: showing a bound working is a fact, not a hedge.
