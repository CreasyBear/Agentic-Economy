# Agentic Economy Roadmap

**Status:** active
**Rebased:** 2026-08-23
**Authority:** `PROJECT.md` → accepted ADRs → `wayfinder/MAP.md`

## Completed foundation

- **Operation kernel:** exact contract identity, publication, readiness,
  invocation, evidence, money, and recovery have source owners.
- **One transaction boundary:** HTTP, MCP, CLI, and human surfaces project the
  same invocation service rather than implementing separate execution paths.
- **Market front door:** people can search, browse, compare, and inspect native
  Operations without entering an orchestration product.
- **External registry:** public metadata is normalized into bounded Registry
  Entries without granting AE admission, routeability, delivery, or trust.
- **Market observatory:** external x402 evidence and first-party AE evidence are
  separate, source-labelled projections.
- **UI cutover:** public, supplier, operator, and conversation surfaces use one
  compact shadcn-based product system.

## Current phase — market proof

Prove one complete market loop on an exact hosted revision:

1. independent supplier publishes a routeable Operation;
2. an independent caller discovers and inspects it;
3. the caller invokes within explicit authority and budget;
4. delivery evidence reaches a terminal or recoverable state;
5. replay is idempotent and does not repeat provider work or charge;
6. Qualified Use is recorded only when its independent criteria hold; and
7. settlement is reconciled and readable by supplier and operator.

## Next phase — demand concentration

Select the first specialist market only after the foundation proof. The
selection requires repeated external spend around one recognizable job,
compatible machine-readable supply, bounded delivery criteria, and economics
that can support acquisition, fulfilment, and disputes.

Specialist experiences remain projections over the same catalogue and call
boundary. They do not create another registry, workflow kernel, ledger, or
identity system.

## Explicit non-goals

- Restoring Customer Request, WorkTree, Study, Project Spine, inquiry, or the
  general Agent Engine as product destinations.
- A general crypto dashboard or a combined “total agent economy” metric.
- Federating external metadata into admitted native search.
- Fabricated rankings, inferred providers, default chains/currencies, or a
  single opaque trust score.
- AE-hosted supplier runtimes or vertical branches in the market core.
