## 3. Factual research-call opening

Use only after the call controls are approved. Call only within the approved
time window, display a return caller-ID number, and end immediately if asked.
This script may be used only when the final purpose and wording have been
approved as research-only. Any promotional or commercial purpose requires a
separately approved compliant script.

> Hello, my name is [NAME], calling for [LEGAL ENTITY / AGENTIC ECONOMY]. We are
> conducting a short study of how people currently complete [ORDINARY-LANGUAGE
> TASK FAMILY]. This call is only to explain that study invitation;
> participation is voluntary. I obtained your number from [SOURCE]. Is now an
> acceptable time for me to explain why I am calling?

If no:

> Understood. Would you like no further contact about this study? I will record
> that request and end the call.

If yes:

> We are looking for adults who have recently [ELIGIBILITY CONDITION]. The study
> involves [TIME] to discuss one real case and, only if you choose, review
> redacted records. [PAYMENT/BENEFIT OR “There is no payment or direct
> benefit.”] Nothing will be recorded unless everyone separately agrees. May I
> send you the participant information notice so you can decide without
> committing now?

Do not collect case details, sensitive information, or consent during the
unscheduled recruitment opening.

## 5. Participant information notice

### Study title

[PLAIN-LANGUAGE STUDY TITLE]

### Who is responsible

[LEGAL ENTITY] is responsible for this study. The accountable researcher is
[FIELD OWNER, ROLE, CONTACT]. Privacy questions can be sent to [PRIVACY
CONTACT]. Concerns or complaints can be sent to [COMPLAINTS CONTACT].

### Why we are doing the study

We want to understand how people and businesses currently complete
[ORDINARY-LANGUAGE TASK]. We are measuring actions, time, waiting, repeated
contact, information freshness, handoffs, corrections, and unresolved work. We
will use the evidence to decide whether Agentic Economy should test a bounded
way to reduce this coordination or should choose a different task.

This study does not offer booking, payment, dispatch, fulfilment,
certification, professional advice, or statutory approval.

### Why you were invited

You were invited because [CUSTOMER/COORDINATOR ELIGIBILITY OR INDEPENDENT
BUSINESS ELIGIBILITY]. We obtained your contact details from [SOURCE]. You must
be at least 18 years old.

### What participation involves

If you agree, we will:

1. spend approximately [TIME] discussing one recent real case or current
   business process;
2. ask about actions, waits, repeated information, decisions, authority,
   evidence, failures, corrections, completion, and next ownership;
3. review only the redacted records you separately choose to share; and
4. optionally ask you to validate a summary of your case or process.

We will not ask you to share passwords, payment credentials, health
information, tenancy records, information about children, government-held
personal data, or unrelated third-party records. Remove third-party names and
details before sharing a record.

### Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may skip a question, pause, refuse recording,
or end the session. Choosing not to participate or withdrawing will not affect
your relationship with Agentic Economy or another participant.

### Recording and transcription

Participation does not require audio, video, screen recording, or automated
transcription. We will ask separately before using each approved recording
method. We will not start or continue a recording unless every person present
has agreed. [NAME EACH RECORDING/TRANSCRIPTION SERVICE, PROCESSOR, LOCATION, AND
USE.]

If recording is declined, the researcher may take non-attributable written
notes with separate consent.

### Information collected

We will collect [EXACT APPROVED FIELD LIST]. Identity/contact records are kept
separately from case notes and measurements. We minimise names, exact dates,
rare details, quotations, and other combinations that could identify someone.

### Use and sharing

We will use the information only for [APPROVED STUDY PURPOSES]. Access is
limited to [NAMED ROLES/PEOPLE]. We will report aggregated or de-identified
findings by default.

Intended audiences and channels are [EXACT INTERNAL RECORDS, ISSUE UPDATES,
PARTNER RECIPIENTS, PUBLICATIONS, OR “restricted internal decision evidence
only”]. A participant results summary [WILL / WILL NOT] be offered by
[METHOD/DATE]. No other dissemination is approved.

We will request separate permission before sharing an attributable quotation,
recording, transcript, screenshot, case record, or identifiable description
with a business, another participant, the public, or an unnamed service. Study
material will not be used for product/model training, sales, public product
claims, provider ranking, or automated decisions unless that use receives a
separate approved basis and permission.

### Risks and possible benefits

Possible risks include discomfort discussing a difficult case, accidental
disclosure of another person's information, re-identification from unusual
facts, and unauthorized access to study material. Controls include voluntary
questions, redaction, separate identifiers, restricted access, encryption,
date-based deletion, and a re-identification review before material leaves the
restricted workspace.

## 7. Session opening and recording check

Read this at every session, including follow-ups.

> Today is [DATE/TIME]. I am [RESEARCHER] from [LEGAL ENTITY]. Present are
> [EVERY PERSON AND ROLE]. We are discussing [STUDY PURPOSE]. Participation is
> voluntary; anyone can skip a question or stop.
>
> The approved recording methods are [METHODS/SYSTEMS], processed by
> [PROCESSORS] for [USES] and deleted by [DATE/RULE]. No meeting bot,
> transcription service, observer, or additional person may join without
> separate consent.
>
> Before I start: does each person separately consent to [EACH METHOD]? Does
> anyone want written notes only?

Record each answer and only then start the approved method. If a new person or
processor joins, stop and repeat consent before continuing.

## 8. End-of-session check

Before ending:

1. confirm the participant's preferred contact and whether a summary should be
   returned for validation;
2. restate recording, retention, and deletion timing;
3. restate withdrawal/data-removal contact and cutoff;
4. identify any shared record requiring further redaction or removal;
5. ask whether any statement should be treated as off-record; and
6. record new restrictions rather than relying on memory.

## Claim boundary

collection procedure. It would not prove participant consent before consent is
actually recorded, legal compliance, ethics approval, task selection,
independently operated supply, customer/provider value, production behavior,
booking, payment, dispatch, fulfilment, certification, or implementation
readiness.
