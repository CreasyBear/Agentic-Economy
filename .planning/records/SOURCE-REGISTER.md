## Source rules

- Cite the source owner, not a commentary page, for current product behavior.
- Record the evidence cutoff in every research record.
- A changed source does not silently rewrite project history. Update the
  research record, then explicitly retain, supersede, or reopen the decision.
- Marketing claims from a company are evidence of its positioning and offered
  contract, not independent proof that the contract produces customer value.
