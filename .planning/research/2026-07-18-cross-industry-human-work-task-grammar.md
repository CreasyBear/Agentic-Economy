# Cross-industry human commercial-work grammar

**Owner:** Product and Engineering
**Status:** Active
**Maturity:** Target research
**Question:** Which recurring pieces of human commercial work survive across
travel, procurement, event management, and supplier management once authority,
evidence, failure, repair, and completion are examined?
**Decision affected:** D-006 / ADR-009
**Tracking:** [Distil the recurring human work across commercial lifecycles](https://github.com/CreasyBear/Agentic-Economy/issues/183)
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-07-18
**Review by:** 2026-08-18
**Supersedes:** None
**Superseded by:** None

## Executive finding

**INFERRED:** Four official cases support one small evaluation grammar:

> bounded objective and rules → attributed facts and unknowns → admissible
> candidates → attributable gathering → supported comparison or assessment →
> accountable consequential decision → identifiable external effect →
> evidence → exception → referenced repair → exact completion boundary and
> next owner

**INFERRED:** The grammar describes questions that must be answered. It does not make the
answers interchangeable and does not justify one shared lifecycle, schema,
status enum, ranking function, endpoint, or persisted Task object.

**OBSERVED:** The four official cases are:

1. a same-day Sydney–Canberra itinerary made unusable by an airline-controlled
   cancellation, with a disputed refund remedy;
2. Victoria's 2026 Commonwealth Games host commitment, failed delivery model,
   withdrawal, settlement, and continuing wind-up;
3. a Panama Canal Commission circuit-breaker procurement whose unsupported
   evaluation was successfully protested after contract performance was too far
   advanced to unwind; and
4. an imported-food supplier-verification program whose files did not prove
   that required supplier controls and corrective action were implemented.

**INFERRED:** The smallest recurring structure is therefore not “search, compare, buy.” It is
an exact object and objective, current attributed evidence, accountable
authority, an identifiable attempt/effect, an honest resolution, linked repair,
and an explicit next owner.

This is desk research. It does not select AE's first task or cohorts, measure
human burden, recruit providers, prove independently operated supply, authorize
Action Invocation, accept ADR-009/010, prove customer or provider value, or
establish production behavior or fulfilment.

## Evidence method

The synthesis uses only the two primary-source case records produced for this
ticket:

- [Travel and event-management task grammar](./2026-07-18-travel-event-task-grammar.md)
- [Procurement and supplier-management task grammar](./2026-07-18-procurement-supplier-task-grammar.md)

Those records cite the owning regulator, standards body, carrier, auditor,
government, procurement authority, and supplier-control sources directly. The
older [partial-entry lifecycle crosswalk](./2026-07-17-partial-entry-lifecycle-crosswalk.md)
supplies broader standards context but is not used to turn the four cases into
a universal model.

The synthesis distinguishes:

- **observed recurrence** — the official case records directly show people or
  accountable organisations performing the named work in all four cases; and
- **inferred cross-case control question** — the question is necessary to
  evaluate all four cases, but one or more official records do not expose the
  actual human step.

An inferred control question is not evidence that the same recurring human task
was observed. In particular, the travel case does not publish its pre-purchase
candidate rules, information-gathering sequence, comparison, or communication
detail. No domain record, authority, evidence standard, legal effect, retry
rule, or completion condition is treated as interchangeable.

## Rejected false equivalences

**INFERRED:** Each row rejects a cross-case generalization drawn from the
observed domain records. It does not claim the domains never share a document,
provider, contract, or regulation.

| Apparent commonality                                    | Why it is false                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Available option = eligible candidate                   | Search presence does not prove fare suitability, procurement conformity, event feasibility, or supplier-control adequacy. Each requires its own current admission rule and evidence.                                                                                           |
| Quote = airline offer = event cost case = supplier file | These records bind different objects, expiry, authority, risk, and next steps. A supplier file may not be a commercial offer at all; an event cost case is not a vendor quote.                                                                                                 |
| Compare = rank                                          | Travel may compare explicit timing and fare constraints; procurement must use declared factors and documented trade-offs; supplier management may assess adequacy against a safety requirement without ranking; event choice can require public-value and political judgement. |
| Clarify = send another message                          | Procurement exchanges can be fairness- and disclosure-constrained; supplier questions can require qualified technical review; airline and event communications have different rights and external effects.                                                                     |
| Choose = approve = award = accept                       | Preference, recommendation, government scope decision, contract award, supplier approval, carrier remedy, inspection, and regulatory acceptance belong to different principals and authorities.                                                                                |
| Confirmation = completion                               | Booking confirmation does not prove carriage; award does not prove performance or lawful evaluation; a supplier document does not prove an operating control; a settlement can end a dispute while wind-up continues.                                                          |
| Cancellation = one effect                               | Airline operational cancellation, passenger refund request, host-contract termination, paused procurement, supplier discontinuation, and settlement alter different obligations and require different authority and evidence.                                                  |
| Retry = repeat the call                                 | Repeating a search or calculation differs from repeating a communication, purchase, award, import, refund, termination, or settlement. Unknown prior effects must be reconciled.                                                                                               |
| Provider assertion = verified truth                     | Airline, offeror, event party, and supplier records remain attributable claims until the named domain process establishes the required observation or acceptance.                                                                                                              |
| Swap provider = equivalent substitution                 | Itinerary coupling, engineering design, contract terms, food hazards, supplier history, event topology, and transition obligations can make candidates non-fungible.                                                                                                           |
| Original ambition achieved = work complete              | Useful completion may instead be refund, contained failure, protest remedy, supplier discontinuation, termination, or settlement. Completion attaches to the exact obligation and evidence.                                                                                    |
| Procurement is the whole event                          | Procurement is one subordinate event workstream. It cannot represent sports, venues, transport, security, public authority, contract rights, operational control, legacy, or political judgement.                                                                              |

## Smallest repeating trust-property set

**INFERRED:** The two supporting records initially grouped the same residue as
eight properties for travel/events and ten for procurement/supplier management.
The synthesis retains the smaller eight-property grouping. The procurement
record's “admissibility before comparison” belongs inside decision rule and
unresolved judgement; its “attributable gathering and clarification” belongs
inside sourced facts/evidence plus actor/attempt identity. Keeping them as
separate properties would improve emphasis but would not add an independent
trust invariant.

The smallest supported set is:

1. **Exact object and bounded objective.** The exact itinerary, offer,
   requirement, supplier-food pair, contract, scope decision, effect, or dispute
   and its intended completion boundary are named.
2. **Sourced facts, rules, freshness, and unknowns.** Material constraints,
   criteria, terms, evidence, provenance, revisions, non-response, and missing
   evidence remain recoverable; presence is not silently upgraded to
   admissibility.
3. **Actor, principal, authority, and next owner.** Evidence authors,
   assessors, recommenders, decision owners, effect owners, inspectors, and
   repair owners remain distinguishable; gathering and clarification stay
   attributable.
4. **Decision rule and unresolved judgement.** Mechanical admission/comparison
   is separated from preference-sensitive, professional, legal, political,
   physical, or regulatory judgement.
5. **Attempt and external-effect identity.** Communication, release, and
   consequential effects remain separately identifiable, including whether a
   possibly released effect is unresolved.
6. **Attributable evidence and honest resolution.** The observation and source
   supporting confirmed, refused, disputed, contradictory, or unknown state are
   explicit; confirmation is not silently upgraded to completion.
7. **Referenced exception and repair.** Disruption, protest, deficiency,
   investigation, correction, termination, settlement, and new evidence link
   forward without rewriting prior history.
8. **Completion boundary, residue, and next owner.** Completion names what
   closed, what proves it, what remains open, who owns it, and when it must be
   revisited.

### Irreducibility check

| Remove or merge                      | Unsafe ambiguity exposed by the cases                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exact object/objective               | An airline segment, coupled itinerary, supplier-food pair, event host obligation, contract performance, and protest remedy can be mistaken for the same work. |
| Facts/rules/freshness/unknowns       | An expired offer, unstated procurement factor, assumed event funding, or supplier document can be treated as current decision evidence.                       |
| Actor/principal/authority/next owner | A traveller preference, evaluator score, supplier assertion, government recommendation, or record holder can silently become consequential authority.         |
| Decision rule/unresolved judgement   | A ranking can replace a lawful trade-off, qualified hazard judgement, political decision, or context-dependent consumer remedy.                               |
| Attempt/effect identity              | A message, purchase, award, import, refund, cancellation, termination, or settlement can be duplicated or contradicted after an uncertain effect.             |
| Evidence/honest resolution           | Check-in, award, credit, supplier file, or settlement can be reported as carriage, lawful selection, paid remedy, operating control, or full wind-up.         |
| Exception/repair lineage             | Protest, deficiency, disruption, correction, termination, or settlement can erase the defective prior record or be retried generically.                       |
| Completion/residue/next owner        | A completed subtask can be projected as lifecycle completion while refund, performance, surveillance, wind-up, or control improvement remains open.           |

**INFERRED:** No two remaining properties can be merged without recreating one
of those ambiguities: object/knowledge, ownership/judgement, effect/evidence,
and repair/completion are independently variable in the four cases.

The set intentionally excludes domain nouns and does not require identical
payload fields, lifecycle states, user interfaces, provider adapters, legal
meanings, or persistence tables. It is an eval rubric, not a proposed schema.

## Work that remains human-owned or unsupported

**INFERRED:** The cases do not authorize an agent to own:

- a traveller's preference-sensitive trade-off, airline safety/operations,
  physical carriage, or authoritative consumer-law dispute outcome;
- elected-government host, scope, withdrawal, funding, or settlement decisions;
  official advice, site and safety judgement, negotiation, or live event
  control;
- public-procurement requirements, lawful procedure, engineering judgement,
  source selection, award, termination, or protest remedy; or
- qualified food-hazard analysis, supplier approval/discontinuation, physical
  audit/testing, import admission/refusal, recall, or regulatory determination.

An agent may help extract, organize, compare declared facts, expose
contradictions, draft questions, preserve lineage, and show the supported next
step. Whether any of that removes enough real work to justify AE is a field
question owned by the task-and-cohort selection gate.

## Falsifiers carried forward

The cross-industry grammar fails or narrows if:

- domain experts cannot map a case without giving one trust property conflicting
  authority, evidence, retry, or completion meaning;
- a cold reviewer needs hidden history to identify the safe next action or
  mistakes an unknown effect for a safe retry;
- neutral machinery requires travel, event, procurement, or supplier nouns;
- the selected task requires a parallel authority, attempt, evidence, or
  recovery lifecycle;
- real provider or operator work offsets the customer's saved effort;
- supplier matching is not the dominant burden and AE merely hides the actual
  judgement/coordination work; or
- the contrasting direct workflow is cheaper and equally safe without AE
  orchestration.

## Decision impact

**INFERRED:** This record resolves the bounded desk-research question of which
human-work observations and control questions recur across the four comparison
industries. It supports the next
task-first decisions:

- choose one recognizable task and real customer/coordinator and provider
  cohorts;
- test that task against the eight trust properties and its domain-specific
  residue;
- preserve domain contracts and accountable human decisions;
- measure direct, customer, provider, and operator burden; and
- extract a shared source seam only after two real callers preserve the same
  trust meaning.

It explicitly rejects a universal Task, Operation, Candidate, Quote, Decision,
Cancellation, Completion, or shared lifecycle schema.

This record does not authorize source implementation, persistence, endpoints,
ADR acceptance, production deployment, independently operated supply, provider
fulfilment, booking, award, import, event delivery, customer value, or public
claims.

  compare supported public information, send a qualified inquiry, and exercise
  the separately evidenced authenticated Customer Request path. It does not
  presently perform the consequential travel, event, procurement, or supplier
  work described here.
- **Target behavior informed by this research:** a domain-owned action may
  preserve the eight trust properties when invoked directly or from a Customer
  Request, while accountable domain records and people retain their exact
  meaning and authority.
