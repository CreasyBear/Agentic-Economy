# First task and participating-cohort field-selection protocol

**Field owner:** Unassigned

**Decision affected:** D-005 / D-006 / ADR-009

## Decision this protocol supports

Select one task framed in ordinary human language, one customer/coordinator
cohort, and one independently operated business cohort only after recent real
cases show:

- material recurring coordination burden;
- a bounded completion point and meaningful standalone result;
- recruitable businesses that can provide the required facts or supported next
  step;
- facts current enough for the decision;
- provider and AE-operator effort that does not erase the customer gain; and
- a direct path that remains simpler when AE adds no coordination value.

The possible decisions are:

- **select** the task and cohorts for the next provider-contract gate;
- **refine** the task boundary and run another bounded field round;
- **narrow** to information gathering, qualification, comparison, or
  human-mediated inquiry;
- **operate deliberately as a service** with named and costed human work; or
- **stop** this candidate and test another.

No source implementation, Action Invocation work, ADR acceptance, or production
deployment follows merely because this protocol exists.

## Entry criteria for a candidate

A candidate enters field comparison only when:

1. the task has one ordinary-language beginning and standalone completion
   boundary;
2. five recent real cases can be reconstructed without asking participants to
   imagine AE;
3. the cases include at least one exception, refusal, non-response, material
   change, or uncertain outcome;
4. the customer/coordinator can describe their direct incumbent path;
5. at least three independently operated businesses in the relevant cohort can
   be approached about their real participation, facts, evidence, refusal, and
   maintenance work;
6. one named field owner can obtain consent, schedule participants, retain
   evidence, and record deviations; and
7. the candidate does not require AE to book, charge, dispatch, fulfil, certify,
   approve, or exercise professional/statutory authority.

Failure to meet entry criteria is evidence against immediate selection, not a
reason to substitute fixtures or simulations.

## Evidence populations

### Recent customer/coordinator cases

For each leading candidate:

- minimum: five cases completed, abandoned, refused, or materially interrupted
  within the previous twelve months;
- preferred: cases from at least three unrelated customers/coordinators;
- include: at least one ordinary completion and at least two materially
  different exception/repair paths;
- exclude: hypothetical journeys, retrospective composites, AE-created
  simulations, and cases where the participant cannot distinguish memory from
  retained records.

### Participating businesses

For the candidate being considered for selection:

- minimum: three independently operated businesses able to speak for their own
  operations;
- preferred: five businesses spanning at least two relevant business roles;
- exclude: AE-operated fixtures, friendly simulations, directories presented as
  participation, and a business that cannot identify who will maintain the
  material facts or handle the supported next step.

### AE operator

Name the person who would perform recruitment, corrections, evidence checking,
exception handling, and support during the field round. If no operator is
assigned, operator burden is unknown and the task cannot be selected.

## Case reconstruction

Reconstruct the incumbent path before showing or describing an AE-assisted
alternative.

For each case, record:

### Identity and boundary

- case reference and evidence window;
- task in the participant's own words;
- customer/coordinator role;
- participating business roles;
- start event;
- completion criterion;
- actual terminal or unresolved state;
- consequence if the task is wrong, late, duplicated, or abandoned.

### Human work

Record each action in sequence:

- state or restate facts;
- search or identify a participant;
- check eligibility or suitability;
- request information or evidence;
- clarify;
- compare or assess;
- obtain authority;
- communicate a consequential instruction;
- observe status or outcome;
- chase, correct, cancel, reconcile, dispute, or escalate;
- hand off to the next owner.

For every action capture actor, active minutes, elapsed wait, channel, repeated
fact/evidence, input source, output record, next owner, and whether the action
was avoidable.

### Trust and failure

Use the eight-property rubric from the
[cross-industry task grammar](./2026-07-18-cross-industry-human-work-task-grammar.md):

1. exact object and bounded objective;
2. sourced facts, rules, freshness, and unknowns;
3. actor, principal, authority, and next owner;
4. decision rule and unresolved judgement;
5. attempt and external-effect identity;
6. attributable evidence and honest resolution;
7. referenced exception and repair; and
8. completion boundary, residue, and next owner.

Record where the direct path preserves each property, where it loses it, and
which person repairs the loss.

## Customer/coordinator interview

Ask for a real recent case, then follow the retained records. Do not teach AE's
model or ask whether the participant “likes AI.”

1. What were you trying to get completed, and where did your responsibility
   begin and end?
2. What happened first, and what record do you still have?
3. Which facts did you provide more than once, to whom, and why?
4. Which participant or requirement was hardest to identify?
5. What information had to be current before you could decide or proceed?
6. Which comparisons were mechanical, and which required your judgement?
7. Which decisions required another person's authority?
8. Where did you wait, chase, correct, or reconstruct context?
9. What failed or changed, and how did you learn what could safely happen next?
10. What evidence let you call your part complete?
11. What work continued after your part was complete, and who owned it?
12. If one piece could have been completed for you without taking over the
    whole project, which piece would have saved the most work?

After reconstructing the case, ask the participant to choose one—not combine
them:

- gather and normalize current information;
- check a supplied shortlist against declared requirements;
- obtain comparable responses or quotes;
- preserve and compare supplied options;
- send one authorized inquiry or instruction;
- inspect a supplied commitment or status;
- recover an uncertain or failed attempt;
- none: the current direct path is preferable.

Record why the rejected tasks would not help.

## Business-participant interview

Use the participant's current process and records.

1. Which customer/coordinator requests can you answer consistently?
2. What inputs are required before you can answer or act?
3. Which facts can you keep current, who owns them, and how often do they
   change?
4. Which facts require confirmation for each case?
5. What makes you refuse, redirect, or ask for clarification?
6. What record proves you received a request?
7. What evidence can you return for success, partial result, refusal, or
   uncertainty?
8. What can safely be retried, and what must be reconciled first?
9. Who handles corrections, cancellation, disputes, and non-response?
10. What work would AE remove, and what new work would AE add?
11. Would you operate this path without an AE employee translating, chasing, or
    repairing it? What would prevent that?
12. Who would maintain the required facts and evidence for thirty days?

Do not call a business “participating supply” until it has performed the agreed
maintenance and response behavior through the evidence window.

## Measurement workbook

### Customer/coordinator burden

For each case:

- active minutes;
- elapsed duration;
- number of participant organizations;
- contacts and follow-ups;
- channels;
- repeated material facts/evidence;
- clarification loops;
- decisions and authority handoffs;
- exceptions and repairs;
- parallel trackers maintained;
- correctness/completeness defects;
- privacy-sensitive disclosures;
- task completion or exact unresolved state.

### Provider burden

- intake and clarification minutes;
- facts/evidence supplied;
- repeated entry;
- response attempts;
- refusal/redirect effort;
- exception and reconciliation effort;
- maintenance minutes over the evidence window;
- added systems/channels;
- independently completed versus AE-operator-assisted cases.

### AE operator burden

- recruitment and onboarding minutes;
- data correction and normalization;
- evidence checking;
- provider/customer chasing;
- rule interpretation;
- exception handling;
- manual state repair;
- support contacts;
- participant training;
- work that would recur per case or per month.

### Supply and freshness

- businesses approached, eligible, willing, configured, responsive, and
  independently completing the task;
- material facts required;
- fact owner and authoritative source;
- observed change frequency;
- last-confirmed time;
- stale/contradictory observations;
- correction time;
- cases blocked by missing or stale facts.

## Baselines and comparisons

### Incumbent baseline

Measure before setting the improvement threshold. Retain the actual email,
forms, calls, spreadsheets, portal states, and handoffs participants used,
subject to consent and redaction.

### Direct-path negative control

Use a competent single-provider path for a task the provider already completes
end to end. Record the same burden and trust measures. AE fails the control if
it adds a durable invocation, approval, question, wait, or operator action
without a named safety or continuity benefit.

### AE-assisted comparison

An AE-assisted trial is permitted only after the task and participating
businesses are provisionally selected from the incumbent cases. The comparison
must use the same task boundary and cannot teach participants hidden workflow
state.

The provisional selection resolves Wayfinder #182 and earns only the next
provider-contract and investment-eval gates. It does not claim AE-assisted
customer value, provider value, operator leverage, or implementation readiness.
Those claims require the later real comparison and its frozen thresholds.

## Preregistered decision rules

Thresholds are frozen after the incumbent baseline and before an AE-assisted
trial. The default candidate thresholds are:

### Customer value

- at least 30% reduction in the primary measured coordination burden;
- no worse material correctness, elapsed completion, total customer cost,
  privacy exposure, or decision control;
- no continuing need for a parallel customer tracker.

### Provider value

- no increase in median provider active effort;
- fewer repeated or unusable requests, or a clearly valued improvement in fit,
  evidence, or handoff quality;
- at least three businesses complete the supported behavior independently;
- required material facts remain current for the declared evidence window.

### Operational leverage

- AE-operator active effort is lower than the customer and provider effort
  removed;
- no recurring manual translation of the same input/output;
- no hidden operator decision replaces the named customer, provider,
  professional, or authority owner;
- exceptions have a bounded owner and repair path.

### Task-first product fit

- a person or cold agent can stop after the task with a usable result, honest
  unknown, or explicit next owner;
- the task does not require a synthetic full Customer Request;
- the direct and Request-owned forms can plausibly retain the same eight trust
  properties;
- the task is valuable beyond discovery and does not require unsupported
  booking, payment, dispatch, fulfilment, certification, or statutory action.

## Falsifiers

Reject or change the candidate when any of the following holds:

- fewer than five recent real cases can be reconstructed;
- the claimed burden is mostly expert judgement or physical work rather than
  removable coordination;
- the direct path is simpler and equally safe;
- fewer than three independently operated businesses will participate;
- material facts cannot remain current enough for the decision;
- provider clarification or maintenance work offsets customer saving;
- AE-operator effort equals or exceeds the effort removed;
- a parallel tracker remains necessary;
- task completion cannot be evidenced independently;
- the task requires AE to infer consequential authority;
- safe recovery requires a second lifecycle or domain-specific neutral kernel;
- a serious correctness, privacy, safety, authority, or duplicate-effect failure
  occurs.

## Decision packet

The field owner returns one packet containing:

1. selected task in ordinary language;
2. customer/coordinator cohort;
3. participating business cohort;
4. exact completion boundary;
5. why now;
6. five or more case reconstructions;
7. measured incumbent baseline;
8. provider, freshness, and recruitability evidence;
9. direct-path control;
10. customer, provider, and operator hypotheses;
11. rejected candidates and reasons;
12. principal risks and residual unknowns;
13. field owner and evidence window;
14. preregistered thresholds and falsifiers; and
15. Stage A recommendation: provisionally select, refine the field boundary,
    reject and test the next candidate, or stop task selection.

The Founder resolves the ticket from that evidence. Agents may prepare,
instrument, analyze, and challenge the packet; they cannot supply the real
cases, independent business participation, accountable ownership, or final
investment judgement.

After a separately authorized real AE-assisted field trial, the same workbook
records the distinct Stage B decision: invest, refine, narrow, operate
deliberately as a service, or stop. Stage B cannot be declared from the Stage A
baseline or interviews.

## Immediate blocking decision

The next decision is not which candidate wins. It is:

> Who is the accountable field owner who can obtain consented access to at least
> five recent real cases and approach at least three independently operated
> businesses for the leading candidate?

Until that owner and access path are named, the ticket remains claimed and open,
the candidate ranking remains a hypothesis, and Phase 1/2 implementation
remains gated.

## Current-versus-target check

- **Current evidenced behavior:** AE's current safe assistant surface supports
  public business information, comparison, and qualified inquiry; the
  authenticated Customer Request path has separately bounded development and
  hosted evidence. No independently operated cohort in this protocol has been
  selected or proven.
- **Target behavior under evaluation:** one selected task removes measured
  coordination burden for a named cohort while participating businesses
  maintain the required facts and supported next step without hidden AE
  operator work.
- **Claims not authorized:** this protocol does not prove customer/provider
  value, recruitable or independently operated supply, production behavior,
  fulfilment, booking, payment, dispatch, certification, statutory approval,
  ADR acceptance, or Action Invocation readiness.
