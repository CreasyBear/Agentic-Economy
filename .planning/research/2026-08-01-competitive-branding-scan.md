# Competitive branding scan — how agentic-economy brands actually position (2026-08-01)

Trigger: founder judgment that "Your AI knows who to call" is vague posturing; direction to anchor on
specific niche requests demonstrating an agnostic plan-building/execution framework. Founder named
Monid (monid.ai) as the closest reference brand.

## The field

### 1. Economy-layer / infrastructure brands (agent-as-customer)

**Monid** (`monid.ai`, `withmonid.com`) — the founder's reference, and the sharpest brand in the space.
Positioning arc across three launches: "One wallet, every paid tool your agent needs" (PH, Apr 2026) →
"OpenRouter for agent tools" (PH 2.0, May 2026) → "the economic layer for AI agents" (withmonid).
What the live homepage actually does:

- Headline is a capability claim wired to an immediate demo: "Connect your agent to every tool it
  needs." followed by a literal one-liner to paste: `$ set up https://monid.ai/SKILL.md` — "and let it
  take it from there." **The mechanism is the hero.**
- Proof is a wall of *named, specific* supply: Apollo, ElevenLabs, Exa, Apify, TikTok scraping, Wokelo
  private markets, "Saperly — agent phone". Not "thousands of integrations"; actual logos with niches.
- Live numbers everywhere: "Live · 1300+ tools", "$0.0013 debited from one balance", "Start with $1 in
  free credit". Prices render inside the demo flow (`monid.discover()` → 12 candidates ranked by fit &
  price → `monid.run()` → pay per call).
- The category thesis ("Agents are becoming customers", "economic layer for AI agents") lives on the
  *secondary* site (withmonid.com) and in investor copy — never as the product headline.
- One enemy named concretely: "We killed the subscriptions."

**Stripe**: "Infrastructure for the agent economy" — category-claiming, credible only because Stripe
already owns payments. **x402 / agentic.market**: a catalog of priced callable services; the brand is
the catalog itself ("$0.001 USDC/search" rows).

### 2. Consumer agent-commerce brands

- **Perplexity**: "Buy Now" — a verb on a button, 2M monthly shoppers. Brand = the action.
- **OpenAI**: "Buy it in ChatGPT" — verb + surface; retreated from checkout to discovery in Mar 2026,
  and the *copy retreated with the capability* (now "assisted discovery"). Copy tracks boundary.
- **Google**: protocol brands (UCP/AP2) aimed at merchants, not consumers.

### 3. Local-services incumbents (AE's demand-side category)

- **Thumbtack** (most instructive): replaced search with an AI-guided flow; the 2026 campaign opens on a
  *concrete scenario* — bad advice from a friend about a specific home problem — then shows describing
  the actual problem (text/photo/voice). CEO frame: "recreate word-of-mouth confidence at scale."
  Also published *prompt-shaped examples* for Operator/Claude: "book an emergency plumber to fix a leak."
- **Angi/hipages/Airtasker**: category-generic "find trusted local pros" copy; none anchor on niche
  demonstrations; all being commoditized into supply for OpenAI/Anthropic surfaces.

## The pattern (what wins)

Nobody winning leads with an abstract identity slogan. The working formula, consistent across Monid,
Thumbtack, and Perplexity:

1. **The demonstration is the headline.** A concrete instance (a pasteable command, a described leak, a
   Buy button) — not a claim about the company.
2. **Named, niche supply as proof.** Specific tools/pros/products with real prices beat any adjective.
3. **Honest live numbers.** Counters and prices sourced from the running system; copy contracts when
   capability contracts (OpenAI checkout retreat).
4. **The category thesis is secondary-band copy** — for investors, providers, and docs, not the hero.
5. **One concrete enemy.** Monid: subscriptions. Thumbtack: bad word-of-mouth advice. (AE's natural
   enemy: the dead-end search — ten open tabs and still nobody to call.)

Verdict on prior AE copy: "Your AI knows who to call." fails tests 1–3 — it is a claim with no witness,
in a field where every strong brand shows the witness first. The founder's instinct matches the market.

## AE brand direction (recommendation)

AE is to *real-world local services work* what Monid is to paid data/tools: the place where agents find
priced, actionable supply and carry work to completion — with the extra leg Monid doesn't have: the
plan. So borrow the Monid formula, not their words:

- **Hero = a niche ask in quotes** ("Find a fortepiano restorer in Coober Pedy.") that visibly runs —
  the ask box is our `$ set up` one-liner. The stranger the job, the stronger the proof of an agnostic
  engine.
- **Proof strip = runnable example asks** (funeral director in Parramatta, dentist in Adelaide this
  week, photography for a wedding of 20) — each one click from a live plan. These are our tool logos.
- **Mechanism line, not identity line**: "authors a plan, works listed businesses first, searches the
  web when nothing is listed, returns options you can act on" — describes the loop the person watches.
- **Live numbers only when real**: listed-business count, plans authored, ms latency — from
  source-owned truth, added when the numbers exist and flatter honestly. No invented counters.
- **Category thesis demoted** to the business-facing band: "Businesses publish what they do once, then
  earn whenever agents bring them work."
- **Copy tracks capability boundary** (Phase 1 = read/quote + cited web discovery): no "get it done"
  claims until execution exists; the OpenAI retreat shows the cost of leading capability.
