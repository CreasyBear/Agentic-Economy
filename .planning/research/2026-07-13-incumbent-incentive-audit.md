# Structural incentive audit — neutral governed agent-to-SMB routing

**As of:** 2026-07-13  
**Question:** Why have incumbents not built a cross-platform layer with admitted supply, transparent response telemetry, canonical intent, deterministic evidence/replay, no paid rank, and no lead resale—and when would they?

## 1. Scope and evidence discipline

- **OBSERVED (AE):** AE’s target is below assistants and above transports/payment rails: admission, principal-bound authority, canonical digest, duplicate-safe execution, and replayable evidence for arbitrary business actions ([`PRODUCT.md`](PRODUCT.md#L21-L46); [`KERNEL-CEILING.md`](.planning/design/KERNEL-CEILING.md#L10-L23)). R1 is one explicitly approved inquiry to one claimed, routable business—not lead fan-out ([`WEDGE-LADDER.md`](.planning/design/WEDGE-LADDER.md#L20-L26), [L249-L264](.planning/design/WEDGE-LADDER.md#L249-L264)).
- **OBSERVED:** “Nobody is building this” is false. OpenAI/Stripe’s ACP already offers organic unsponsored product rank, explicit confirmation, scoped payment tokens, and merchant-controlled fulfilment; Google/Shopify’s UCP+AP2 approaches the commerce subset. The remaining gap is one adopted envelope combining all six controls for **arbitrary non-payment action** ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/); [`KERNEL-CEILING.md`](.planning/design/KERNEL-CEILING.md#L37-L45)).
- **Method:** P&L impacts below are sensitivities, not forecasts. For an incumbent revenue pool $R$, share of use cases migrated $m$, incumbent monetization lost $d$, and replacement capture $c$, net exposure is $R·m·(d-c)$. Exact local-routing revenue is generally undisclosed; those cells are **UNKNOWN**.
- **Theory:** Christensen & Bower found capable incumbents funded innovations demanded by powerful current customers and starved technically feasible projects aimed at emerging markets ([1996 paper, pp.197–199](http://www.iot.ntnu.no/innovation/norsi-pims-courses/Levinthal/Christensen%20&%20Bower%20(1996).pdf)). Helmer counter-positioning requires a new model whose adoption damages the incumbent’s existing model—not mere feature delay ([7 Powers](https://7powers.com/)).

## 2. Incumbent-by-incumbent audit

### A. Google: attention auction with the strongest technical counterattack

- **Revenue/distortion — OBSERVED:** Alphabet FY2025 revenue was about **US$402.8b**; Google Search & other about **US$224.5b**, Google Services about **US$342.7b** ([Alphabet FY2025 10-K table](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1652044/000165204426000018/R12.htm)). Local Services Ads explicitly auctions leads: bids affect rank; higher budgets/bids can yield more leads ([Google LSA](https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/10125017?hl=en-GB)). Local-services share of Search revenue is **UNKNOWN**.
- **Incentive:** Neutral rank removes bid-to-rank; direct structured replies compress result pages and ad impressions; replay telemetry reveals whether paid exposure produced a reply rather than letting clicks/leads remain the billable proxy. Google would trade a proven high-margin auction for an initially tiny clearing fee.
- **Cannibalization sensitivity — INFERRED:** If only 3–8% of Search revenue is local/commercial-service intent, pool = **US$6.7–18.0b**. If agent routing migrates 20–40% and destroys 50–80% of auction yield while a neutral action fee recaptures 10–25%, annual exposure is roughly **US$0.3–5.0b**. Range is intentionally wide because segment revenue is undisclosed.
- **Steelman:** Search quality already rewards relevance; Google possesses Maps supply, identity, ads demand, Gemini distribution, UCP/AP2, and global anti-abuse. It can separate organic routing from sponsored discovery, as OpenAI has done.
- **Attack:** “Neutral” under Google governance is contestable: its advertiser/customer and adjudicator roles remain combined. Yet commerce-protocol progress proves cannibalization is not an absolute block.
- **Move trigger / lag — INFERRED:** Trigger: ≥10–15% of high-commercial-intent queries complete inside rival assistants, advertiser ROI falls, or DMA/antitrust remedies force ranking/data portability. Likely response: **0–6 months** for Gemini/Maps action features; **12–24 months** for cross-platform evidence semantics. **Side of history:** closer to successful self-cannibalizer because the new layer protects the query interface, but likely non-neutral by default.

### B. Meta: engagement/data lock-in; economically conflicted, strategically peripheral

- **Revenue/distortion — OBSERVED:** Meta FY2025 revenue was **US$200.97b**, operating income **US$83.28b**; ad impressions rose 12% and average price/ad 9% ([Meta FY2025 results](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)). Its filings identify reliance on advertising, engagement, and data signals. Exact local-SMB ad revenue is **UNKNOWN**.
- **Incentive:** A neutral router lets intent leave Instagram/WhatsApp, limits disclosure to purpose-bound fields, makes rank auditable, and returns replies to a portable record. That reduces impressions, retargeting data, and inbox lock-in—the three inputs Meta monetizes.
- **Cannibalization sensitivity — INFERRED:** Using a conservative **US$195–199b** ad pool, if 2–5% concerns local-service discovery/messaging and 15–30% migrates, with 40–70% yield loss and 5–15% fee recapture, exposure ≈ **US$0.2–1.8b/year**.
- **Steelman:** WhatsApp Business is already the operator inbox for many SMBs; Meta can make messaging the transport while keeping the business relationship intact.
- **Attack:** A portable evidence ledger reduces Meta’s control without materially enlarging its core ad market. It is more rational to offer proprietary business-agent actions inside WhatsApp than neutral clearing.
- **Move trigger / lag — INFERRED:** Trigger: WhatsApp becomes the losing endpoint because assistants require signed portable receipts, or regulation mandates interoperability. **6–12 months** for proprietary receipts; **18–36 months** for genuine portability. **Side of history:** Microsoft-mobile/newspapers side unless routing defends WhatsApp itself.

### C. Yelp / hipages / Airtasker: the strongest wedge-level conflict

- **Revenue/distortion — OBSERVED:** Yelp FY2025 net revenue **US$1.465b**; Services ads **US$948m** plus RR&O ads **US$444m** = **US$1.392b, 95.0%** of revenue. Clicks fell 7% while average CPC rose 10% ([Yelp FY2025](https://www.yelp-ir.com/news/press-releases/news-release-details/2026/Yelp-Delivers-Record-Net-Revenue-in-2025-Accelerating-Investment-in-AI-Transformation/default.aspx)); Yelp charges each click at a supply/demand-dependent CPC ([Yelp CPC](https://biz.yelp.com/support-center/article/How-does-Yelp-s-Cost-Per-Click-CPC-advertising-program-work)). hipages FY2025: **A$83.1m** revenue, **A$80.8m recurring (97%)**, **36.6k** subscribers, **A$2,267 ARPU**, **1.382m jobs**, **2.8m connections**, 24% EBITDA margin; management prioritised “lead pricing mechanics” ([ASX FY25](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20250822/pdf/06n5s3y9lqpx9j.pdf)). Airtasker’s escrow/task-fee model differs; exact local lead resale exposure is **UNKNOWN**.
- **Incentive:** They monetize scarce/opaque access to demand: paid visibility, CPC, subscriptions justified by lead flow, or take-rate. AE’s one-recipient consent, no resale, no paid rank, and reply-rate evidence lets an SMB compare **outcomes**, exposing low-quality clicks and eliminating multi-sold leads.
- **Cannibalization sensitivity — INFERRED:** Yelp: migrating 20–40% of ad-supported service intent with 50–80% ad-yield loss and 10–20% neutral-fee recapture exposes **US$76–265m/year** on the US$948m Services-ad pool. hipages: if 30–50% of subscription value is lead access, neutral routing migrates 25–50%, and 50–80% is unrecovered, exposure ≈ **A$3–16m/year** (4–19% of revenue). Airtasker can preserve economics when it remains merchant-of-record/recourse provider; pure routing is less attractive.
- **Steelman:** These firms have local supply, reviews, sales teams, fraud operations, and workflow. hipages is explicitly moving from marketplace toward Tradiecore; Yelp announced an OpenAI agreement and action-oriented AI.
- **Attack:** Their best customers pay precisely because demand access is non-neutral. Publishing comparable reply/conversion telemetry invites churn and adverse selection; no-paid-rank removes the pricing lever. A neutral product can be launched only as a separated unit with a different payer.
- **Move trigger / lag — INFERRED:** Trigger: organic assistant referrals exceed paid-site leads, CAC/lead disputes drive churn, or consumer law constrains lead resale/ranking disclosure. **3–9 months** to expose APIs/agent feeds; **12–24 months** to change contracts and telemetry. **Side of history:** newspapers-vs-Craigslist for Yelp/hipages; Airtasker is less exposed because execution/recourse can remain valuable.

### D. Vertical SaaS: single-tenant depth, not marketplace opacity

- **Revenue/distortion — OBSERVED:** ServiceTitan FY2026: **US$961.0m** revenue; **US$712.3m subscription**, **US$213.1m usage**, ~**10,800** active customers, >95% gross retention, >110% NDR, and **US$82.1b GTV** ([ServiceTitan FY2026](https://investors.servicetitan.com/news-releases/news-release-details/servicetitan-announces-fiscal-fourth-quarter-and-full-fiscal/)). Revenue/customer is ~**US$89k** (not disclosed ACV; includes usage/services). ServiceM8-class SMB tools use lower-ACV subscriptions, but private-company class economics are **UNKNOWN**.
- **Incentive:** Their power comes from owning one business’s system of record and workflow. Neutral cross-business ranking is outside buyer demand, creates multi-tenant governance/liability, and makes their customer data portable. However, receiving governed work into dispatch/CRM increases seat/usage value and does **not** necessarily cannibalize SaaS.
- **Cannibalization sensitivity — INFERRED:** If an open binding reduces 5–15% of platform/usage pricing power across only 10–25% of customers, ServiceTitan exposure is **US$5–35m/year**; offset can be positive if routed jobs expand usage. The real conflict is control/commoditization, not immediate revenue destruction.
- **Steelman/attack:** Best-positioned supply adapter and live threat: schema depth, authenticated businesses, schedules, estimates, invoices, and response outcomes. Worst-positioned neutral ranker: it sees only its tenant base and is rewarded for ecosystem depth, not cross-vendor fairness.
- **Move trigger / lag — INFERRED:** Trigger: customers demand “be reachable by every assistant,” or OpenAI/Google makes portable action endpoints an acquisition channel. **0–6 months** for MCP/ACP/A2A connectors; **12–24 months** for replay/evidence portability. **Side of history:** Adobe/Netflix side for adapters; not credible independent neutral governor.

### E. Assistant platforms (OpenAI / Anthropic): the live platform threat

- **Revenue/distortion — OBSERVED:** OpenAI already operates ACP Instant Checkout: 700m weekly ChatGPT users at launch, organic unsponsored rank, merchant fee on completed purchases, explicit confirmation, merchant-of-record preserved ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/)). Anthropic originated MCP as an open connection layer, but no public filing discloses revenue mix or a neutral SMB-action P&L. Private revenue numbers are **UNKNOWN**, so press estimates are excluded.
- **Incentive:** Their core metric is assistant usefulness/subscription/API usage, so routing protects the user interface rather than cannibalizing it. Conflict appears later: assistant-owned rank, proprietary conversation context, take-rate, preferred partners, and refusal to let an external governor own receipts or adjudicable telemetry.
- **Cannibalization:** Near-term **low/negative**—actions increase retention. If a third-party clearing layer charges per action and owns cross-assistant supply/telemetry, it captures a strategic control point. Hence platforms will adopt open merchant protocols but may internalize mandate, rank, and evidence.
- **Steelman:** ACP’s no-sponsored-rank design directly refutes a universal “incumbents cannot be neutral” claim. Assistant platforms can fund the transition because it expands their primary product.
- **Attack:** ACP is commerce-specific and platform-mediated; it does not prove neutral arbitrary-action admission, deterministic replay across assistants, or independent governance. Platform neutrality remains revocable policy, not structural separation.
- **Move trigger / lag — INFERRED:** Already triggered by shopping demand and rival agents. **0–6 months** for local-services pilots; **6–18 months** for mandates/receipts; **12–24 months** to close much of AE’s spec gap. **Side of history:** Amazon→AWS/Netflix side—the new model enlarges and defends the core interface.

## 3. Historical test: what actually distinguishes failure from successful cannibalization?

- **Failure pattern — OBSERVED/INFERRED:** Kodak’s film economics, newspapers’ classifieds, Microsoft’s Windows/Office PC complement, and Google+’s attempt to graft social onto Google all had current profit pools/customers demanding the old optimization. The relevant mechanism is not stupidity; it is resource allocation and channel/customer power, consistent with Christensen–Bower. Analogies are directional, not causal proof.
- **Success pattern — OBSERVED/INFERRED:** Netflix streaming defended “watching” while replacing delivery; Adobe subscription increased predictability and retained the same creative workflow; AWS commercialized Amazon’s internal capability into an additive market. Successful cases had (a) a stable higher-order job, (b) a new model that defended/expanded that job, (c) separable economics and executive commitment, and (d) a credible migration path.
- **Classification:** Google and assistants can define the higher-order job as “answer/act for the user,” so they can cannibalize ads/links when interface control is at risk. Vertical SaaS can add routing as an acquisition adapter. Meta can act if WhatsApp is defended. Lead marketplaces must destroy the scarcity/opacity they sell: the cleanest genuine counter-positioning target.

## 4. Verdict and strategic consequence

- **Verdict — INFERRED:** AE has **real but narrow and temporary counter-positioning** against Yelp/hipages-style lead markets; **weak counter-positioning** against Meta; and **no durable counter-positioning** against assistant platforms or Google once user demand shifts. Against vertical SaaS, AE is complementary before it is competitive.
- **Window:** **12–24 months** for protocol semantics and **24–48 months** for cross-vendor deployment, matching AE’s own ceiling analysis. The wedge-level local-market window may last **18–36 months**, but only if AE accumulates admitted supply, independently measured response reliability, and portable evidence before assistants bundle it.
- **Live threats:** (1) **OpenAI/assistant platforms**—incentives align and ACP proves willingness; (2) **Google**—Maps supply + Gemini + UCP/AP2; (3) **vertical SaaS** as the fastest supply-side adapter. **Paper threats:** Meta as independent governor; Yelp/hipages building truly no-paid-rank/no-resale routing before external pressure.
- **Kill test:** Counter-positioning disappears if AE funds itself through paid rank, lead resale, or biased take-rate. It survives only if the payer is the party creating/avoiding operational risk and basic business admission/response rights remain unpriced, consistent with [`KERNEL-CEILING.md` C-5](.planning/design/KERNEL-CEILING.md#L64-L66).
- **What AE must win before imitation:** not “the protocol,” which commoditizes, but (i) claimed/admitted local supply, (ii) longitudinal reply/delivery telemetry unavailable to ad platforms without exposing their own quality, and (iii) principal↔agent↔business claim/evidence relationships. Even these are a race, not an entitlement.
- **UNKNOWN / decision gate:** Whether enough consequential non-payment actions occur to support independent clearing rather than assistant-native logs. If record reopen/export/dispute use stays negligible after 1,000 admitted sends, AE’s own kill condition says retain the kernel internally and abandon the network claim ([`KERNEL-CEILING.md`](.planning/design/KERNEL-CEILING.md#L70-L74)).
