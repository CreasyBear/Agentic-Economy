# AE becomes the next Yelp — the cautionary worldline

## 1. Verdict

**Analogy strength: 8/10 if AE stalls at R0/R1** — AE already contains the exact attractor basin (free local pages, shortlist utility, legitimate off-platform exits, and no current participation in the commercial outcome), while its planned claimed-owner rail, TX ledger, agent gateway, and R2–R4 ladder are credible escape hatches but are not yet proven end to end (`WEDGE-LADDER.md` §§2–4, §7; `JOURNEY.md` §1; `J6-owner-activation.md` Status).

## 2. What made Yelp Yelp

1. **A user-generated local reputation graph became the discovery product.** Yelp aggregated business pages, reviews, and star ratings; that content gave consumers a reason to start their local decision there and gave pages search-distribution value. The Ninth Circuit described the core service as an online directory whose users review and rate businesses ([*Levitt v. Yelp*, 2014](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2014/09/02/11-17676.pdf)).
2. **High-intent attention was monetized mainly through merchant advertising, not ownership of the completed service transaction.** Yelp now supports quote requests, reservations, and some transactions, so “never transacts” would be historically inaccurate. But the economic center remains ads: in 2025 Services advertising was **$948m** and Restaurants/Retail/Other advertising **$444m**, together about **95% of $1.465bn net revenue**; paying locations fell 3% while revenue per location hit a record ([Yelp FY2025 results](https://www.yelp-ir.com/news/press-releases/news-release-details/2026/Yelp-Delivers-Record-Net-Revenue-in-2025-Accelerating-Investment-in-AI-Transformation/default.aspx)). The plateau mechanic is therefore: sell scarce visibility around decisions while the customer and business finish—and create the authoritative outcome—elsewhere.
3. **The reputation/advertising coupling creates a permanent trust tax, while Google makes directory parity a commodity.** Selling prominence beside merchant reputation can feel coercive even without unlawful conduct. The relevant extortion suit was dismissed: the Ninth Circuit held the pleaded facts insufficient and treated the alleged conduct as, at most, lawful “hard bargaining,” not proven extortion ([*Levitt*](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2014/09/02/11-17676.pdf)). The caution is the *shape of the incentive and merchant perception*, not an allegation that Yelp committed extortion. Meanwhile Google gives businesses free profiles in Search and Maps, including reviews and direct customer actions ([Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/us/business-profile/)); a standalone directory must continually buy, syndicate, or SEO-optimize its way back into an intent surface Google already owns. Review trust is also costly rather than self-maintaining: Yelp says it filtered nearly **500,000 suspected AI-generated reviews**, closed **1.3m+ accounts**, and placed **491 suspicious/compensated-activity alerts** in 2025 ([Yelp Trust & Safety 2025](https://trust.yelp.com/trust-and-safety-report/2025-report/)). Those figures evidence the integrity burden, not that Yelp’s reviews are presently untrustworthy.

## 3. 10-star worldline — the fully realized caution by 2031

**[INFERRED SCENARIO, not forecast]** AE has **1.5m Australian business pages**, but only **8% are claimed** and **3% satisfy `R1TargetAdmitted`**. It serves **12m annual shortlist sessions**, yet **72% terminate in call/open/copy**, **6% reach an admitted AE send**, and fewer than **2% produce an attributable business reply inside AE**. Thirty-day customer repeat use stays below **8%** because the authoritative price, availability, agreement, and fulfilment record live in phone, email, Google, or the business’s own system.

The visible product is excellent R0: SEO listing pages, natural-language local search, evidence snippets, shortlist/export, external review links, and “contact directly.” R1 is a nicer contact form with a receipt, not a compounding network rail. Calling agents consume essentially the same directory projection humans do; few providers expose admitted capability bindings, and no meaningful share of work reaches R2–R4.

**Who pays whom:** consumers pay local businesses entirely off AE; businesses pay AE for sponsored placement, enhanced pages, lead visibility, or reputation/response tooling; AE receives no material share of evidenced commercial actions. **[INFERRED]** By 2031, >70% of AE revenue comes from visibility products and <10% from evidence-bearing routed actions. Sales optimizes advertiser yield because claim activation and closed-loop outcomes never reached enough density.

The trust failure is subtle: AE does not need to fake reviews. Once paid prominence sits next to an evidence/reputation layer, every ranking change invites “pay-to-play” suspicion. If AE adds open reviews to compensate for missing transaction evidence, moderation cost rises while provenance weakens. Google and general assistants summarize the same public facts, so AE becomes a content supplier or paid acquisition customer rather than the route authority. It is a durable, potentially profitable company—and a strategic failure relative to `PRODUCT.md`, which says the marketplace is only a projection and the route graph is the product (`PRODUCT.md`, Product architecture).

## 5. Kill conditions — falsifiers that prove AE is Yelp-ing

1. **Routeability never escapes the catalog.** **[INFERRED threshold]** Within 90 days of recruitment, **<25% of qualified businesses become fully `R1TargetAdmitted`**, or admitted coverage remains **<30% of shortlist impressions** for two consecutive launch cohorts. **Earliest metric:** recruited → claimed → destination verified → admitted funnel by cohort and category. This catches claim-rate stagnation before traffic disguises it.
2. **The action/evidence loop does not compound.** **[INFERRED threshold]** After a cohort has adequate admitted coverage, **<15% of eligible shortlist sessions start R1**, **<40% of admitted sends receive an attributable reply within the declared window**, or **<15% of senders return for a second need within 90 days** for three consecutive months. **Earliest metric:** eligible-shortlist→review→admitted-send→delivery→attributable-reply funnel. Direct exits remain legitimate user successes; they become a company-level kill signal only when the governed path also fails to earn voluntary use.
3. **Revenue and trust couple around visibility instead of evidence.** **[INFERRED threshold]** Before routed-action revenue reaches 20% of revenue, AE introduces paid ranking/review suppression/enhancement—or **>50% of revenue comes from prominence ads** while **<10% comes from evidence-bearing actions**; simultaneously, >10% of surveyed claimed owners believe payment affects organic rank/evidence treatment. **Earliest metric:** revenue mix plus quarterly blinded owner answer to “Does paying AE change organic placement or evidence treatment?” Any non-zero measured ranking lift from payment outside an explicitly labelled ad slot is an immediate stop.

## 6. Star rating today

**2.5/10 on the Yelp worldline.** **[OBSERVED]** AE is still at the directory/one-send edge; the owner-activation journey is designed rather than end-to-end proven, and production loop metrics are unknown (`J6-owner-activation.md`; `JOURNEYS-TO-BUILD.md` build order). But the intended architecture explicitly rejects “directory” as the product, treats marketplace pages as a projection, preserves an evidence ledger rather than a review verdict, and has a runged agent/action escape path (`PRODUCT.md`; `WEDGE-LADDER.md`; `PRINCIPLES.md`).

**Single highest-leverage move:** prove one dense category-and-suburb **claimed-owner → admitted R1 send → attributable owner reply → customer return** loop before scaling directory pages. Instrument every predicate and transition; recruit owners manually if necessary; do not count page publication, SEO visits, copied phone numbers, drafted messages, or provider sign-ups as routeable supply. If that loop cannot clear the proposed kill thresholds, stop expanding R0 inventory—the next page would increase the Yelp attractor, not the routing moat.
