## 2026 protocol-field verdict

**As of 2026-07-13.** **OBSERVED:** the narrow claim survives: no adopted protocol found governs **target admission + principal consent/delegation + exact canonical action integrity + duplicate-safe execution + replayable evidence/dispute + authoritative temporal state for arbitrary business actions**. **INFERRED:** the broader claim “nobody is building this” is false. AP2+UCP nearly closes the set for commerce; two 2026 individual IETF drafts—DRP and AIP—target much of the arbitrary-action layer but lack standards standing and demonstrated adoption.

AE’s intended contract is unusually explicit: exact quote/recipient/data approval, immutable digest, idempotent execution, Root Run evidence and reconciliation ([PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md#L21-L46)); its R1 authority tuple, canonical serialization, one-use nonce, append-only derived state, receipt≠outcome, and deterministic dispute replay are specified in [WEDGE-LADDER.md](./.planning/design/WEDGE-LADDER.md#L90-L112). **OBSERVED repo caveat:** these are destination/design contracts, not proof of a deployed universal kernel.

### Scoring key

`✓` normative answer; `~` partial, optional, domain-limited, or implementation-owned; `—` not answered. “Exactly-once” means duplicate-safe admission/commit (same key→original result; conflicting digest→reject), not impossible exactly-once physical-world effects. “Temporal truth” means more than carrying a timestamp: expiry/current-state checks plus an authoritative clock or explicit freshness/provenance posture.

### What each actually governs, adoption, and governance

- **x402 — OBSERVED:** governs payment negotiation and verification around HTTP 402, not why the principal authorized an arbitrary action. Coinbase says v2 followed six months of use and **over 100M payments**; treat this as vendor-reported, not independently audited. Governance moved to the independent **x402 Foundation, a Linux Foundation project**, initiated by Coinbase and Cloudflare. [v2 launch](https://x402.org/x402-v2-launch/) · [foundation announcement](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/coinbase-and-cloudflare-will-launch-x402-foundation)
- **AP2 — OBSERVED:** payment-security feature inside a commerce protocol; catalog, checkout communication and retention/retrieval are explicitly out of scope. It defines merchant/credential/processor verification, open and closed mandates, signed receipts and dispute verification. Google launched it with **60+ collaborating organizations**, but support statements/reference implementations are not proof of production AP2 transactions. Google-led open repository; future standards-body path is stated, not completed. [spec](https://github.com/google-agentic-commerce/AP2/blob/main/docs/ap2/specification.md) · [launch](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-agents-to-payments-ap2-protocol)
- **UCP — OBSERVED:** vertical commerce capability/schema and lifecycle composition over REST, MCP, A2A or embedded bindings. The business selects the capability intersection; reverse-domain namespaces decentralize extensions, while `dev.ucp.*` belongs to the UCP governing body. Its site lists Google, Shopify, Etsy, Wayfair, Target, Walmart, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, Salesforce, Stripe and travel/food firms as **co-developers**; no primary evidence found that all run production UCP endpoints. [overview/spec](https://ucp.dev/2026-04-08/specification/overview/) · [partner list](https://ucp.dev/)
- **UCP+AP2 — OBSERVED:** the closest deployed-design competitor for **commerce**: capability negotiation locks the session; merchant terms are JCS-canonicalized and signed; the user/platform mandate covers the merchant signature. It still does not govern arbitrary non-commerce action or mandate a universal append-only replay ledger. [extension](https://ucp.dev/2026-04-08/specification/ap2-mandates/)
- **ACP — OBSERVED:** checkout/order synchronization and constrained delegated payment, not general delegation. OpenAI launched Instant Checkout live for US Etsy purchases; Shopify merchants were “coming soon” in the cited launch, so do not count them as live there. OpenAI+Stripe own/co-developed the open specification; merchants remain merchant of record. [live launch](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/) · [checkout](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/checkout) · [delegated payment](https://developers.openai.com/commerce/specs/payment)
- **MCP — OBSERVED:** transport/tool/context interoperability. OAuth authorization is optional and transport-level; elicitation requires clear server identity, review, decline/cancel, and URL consent, but explicitly leaves UI pattern to implementations. Anthropic reported **10,000+ active public servers**, adoption by ChatGPT/Cursor/Gemini/Copilot/VS Code, and **97M+ monthly Python+TypeScript SDK downloads** in Dec 2025. Governance is the Linux Foundation’s AAIF, co-founded by Anthropic, Block and OpenAI. [authorization](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/basic/authorization) · [elicitation](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/client/elicitation) · [governance/adoption](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-the-model-context-protocol-and-establishing-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation)
- **A2A — OBSERVED:** agent communication and task state, not principal authority. v1.0 is Linux Foundation governed by a technical steering committee with AWS, Cisco, Google, IBM, Microsoft, Salesforce, SAP and ServiceNow representatives. Official 2026 announcements show Salesforce, SAP and ServiceNow product integrations with Google Cloud, stronger than launch endorsements, though production volumes are not disclosed. [v1.0](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/announcing-1.0/) · [spec](https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/specification/) · [Salesforce integration](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-04-22-Salesforce-and-Google-Cloud-Enable-AI-Agents-to-Act-Across-Both-Platforms-with-Deep-Context-and-End-to-End-Workflows)
- **Web Bot Auth — OBSERVED:** identity/attribution using HTTP Message Signatures, not consent. Cloudflare’s earlier architecture draft was replaced; the work is now in the IETF Web Bot Auth WG, while still draft work rather than an RFC. Cloudflare added message signatures to Verified Bots; this is operational verifier adoption, not universal web adoption. [IETF WG](https://datatracker.ietf.org/group/webbotauth/about/) · [draft status](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-meunier-web-bot-auth-architecture/) · [Cloudflare deployment](https://blog.cloudflare.com/verified-bots-with-cryptography/)
- **Visa — OBSERVED:** payment-network agent recognition and transaction controls. Visa reports **hundreds** of controlled real-world transactions, **100+ partners**, **30+** sandbox builders and **20+** direct agent/enabler integrations; these are Visa-reported and include closed beta. Trusted Agent Protocol is publicly usable but Visa warns it is still being developed/deployed. [transaction/adoption evidence](https://corporate.visa.com/en/sites/visa-perspectives/newsroom/visa-partners-complete-secure-agentic-transactions.html) · [protocol](https://developer.visa.com/capabilities/trusted-agent-protocol)
- **Mastercard — OBSERVED:** card-network agent payment controls. Santander completed a live payment through live infrastructure in a controlled environment, explicitly **not a commercial rollout**. Mastercard-owned; public protocol detail is thinner than AP2/UCP. [Santander transaction](https://newsroom.mastercard.com/news/europe/en/newsroom/press-releases/en/2026/santander-and-mastercard-complete-europe-s-first-live-end-to-end-payment-executed-by-an-ai-agent/)
- **Entra Agent ID — OBSERVED:** production identity platform using confidential clients, OAuth token exchange, OBO/autonomous agents, refresh, scopes and managed identities. Microsoft-owned product; it does not define reviewed-action receipts or dispute replay. [protocols](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/agent-id/agent-oauth-protocols)
- **NANDA — OBSERVED:** MIT research proposes OAuth/OIDC-compatible authenticated, scoped and auditable delegation; no normative adopted protocol or production integration evidence found. [paper](https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/authenticated-delegation-and-authorized-ai-agents/)
- **DRP/AIP — OBSERVED:** both are **individual Internet-Drafts**, not IETF consensus or endorsed standards. DRP is the closest conceptual overlap with AE; AIP is broader and much more specified around identity, grants, approval lifecycles and replay. DRP names an SDK/hosted service; neither supplied credible third-party production adoption evidence. [DRP](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-nelson-agent-delegation-receipts-05) · [AIP](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-singla-agent-identity-protocol-03)

### Verdict, likely window, and strategy

1. **Gap reality — INFERRED, high confidence:** real only under the full conjunction and the word **arbitrary**. Individual pieces are commoditizing rapidly. AP2 already beats a loose AE claim on portable payment mandates and dispute-linked receipts; DRP beats it on trusted timestamping; AIP challenges identity/delegation/replay breadth.
2. **Window — INFERRED:** **12–24 months for specification convergence; 24–48 months for meaningful cross-vendor deployment.** Reasons: UCP already composes AP2+A2A+MCP; MCP/A2A have neutral foundations and large distribution; 2026 AIP/DRP drafts explicitly fill the upstream delegation/evidence gap. Delay factors are consent UX, key custody, retention/redaction law, cross-party clock/revocation semantics, and incentives to accept evidence generated by another operator.
3. **Most likely to close first:** **Google/UCP+AP2** for commerce; **Microsoft Entra + MCP/A2A extensions** for arbitrary enterprise actions. AIP/DRP may publish the most complete paper design first but are least likely to win adoption without foundation/vendor sponsorship. Visa/Mastercard can close payments, not arbitrary action.
4. **ADOPT, do not compete:** Web Bot Auth for caller attribution; MCP and A2A as transports; UCP as commerce capability/lifecycle schemas; AP2 for portable commerce mandates and receipts; x402/ACP/card-network protocols as payment adapters; OAuth/Entra/AIP-compatible tokens for enterprise identity. AE should never invent parallel rails or agent identity.
5. **AE’s defensible layer — INFERRED:** a domain-neutral **governance compiler and evidence kernel** that takes an action proposed over MCP/A2A/UCP/HTTP, resolves target admission, compiles principal authority into an exact canonical action digest, commits once, and projects a redaction-safe replayable Root Run while preserving `receipt ≠ outcome`. Compete on composition, refusal semantics, temporal provenance and replay—not protocol transport, checkout, or settlement.
6. **Claim wording:** “No widely adopted protocol currently combines these controls for arbitrary consequential business actions” is supportable. “No existing protocol does this” is too absolute because DRP/AIP specifications substantially overlap and UCP+AP2 does most of it for commerce.
