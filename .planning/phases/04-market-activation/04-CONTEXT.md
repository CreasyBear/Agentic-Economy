# Phase 4 — Business experience context

## Active decision

D-011 and D-012 establish a mature, customer-familiar Business Account and
routeable-supply operating surface. Acceptance authorizes documentation
reconciliation, not implementation, hosted behavior, usability, accessibility
in use, or production claims.

The persistent current-business shell is:

```text
Home
Work
Inbox
Offerings
Business settings
Help
```

Home answers whether customers can request work now, why, and what single next action matters. Work is the sole operational queue. Inbox is conversation. Help owns customer-visible support. History is evidence, not another queue. Integrations is canonical under Business settings, with contextual many-to-many links from Offerings and an optional role-aware shortcut to that same route.

Responsibilities are additive and initially exposed through familiar presets.
Protected Ownership never emerges from permission union. Availability remains
domain-owned behind a narrow shared projection. Customer, progressively
disclosed technical, and founder/backstage surfaces remain separate. The
shared experience operates one paid-digital-information-shaped seed action
through the real supply chain. Appointment and dispatch are hostile
substitution fixtures proving the shared contract is not verticalized; Phase 4
does not build three provider lifecycles.

## Current-to-target boundary

Current source already owns business identity, public catalog facts, inquiry
messages, action/work facts and results, capability contracts, capability-
supply publication/readiness facts, and the Phase 3D human/agent paid-action
handoff. It does not yet supply the complete Business Account, common account
principal resolution, Commercial, Usage, routeable operation ingress,
business-affinity Work, lifecycle withdrawal or bounded account projections.
UI projections may summarize source facts but never become command truth.

The detailed classification is in `04-SOURCE-MAP.md`; UI behavior is governed by `04-UI-SPEC.md`; source and executable behavior remain authority for what exists today.
Resolved planning conflicts are recorded in `04-CONTRADICTION-LEDGER.md`.

## Durable product rules

- `/businesses/:businessId/**` is canonical; `/businesses` switches account context; `/owner/**` is compatibility redirect history only.
- Business/domain facts remain source-owned. Business Account projections are removable.
- Release control, feature access, member responsibility, operation publication, and operational availability answer distinct questions.
- AE account billing, customer-to-business operation payment, platform Usage,
  operational metrics and future payouts answer five distinct questions.
- A transport response never proves a consequential command succeeded. Possible release remains uncertain and reconcile-only.
- Commercial and Usage follow ADR-025. No raw payment/secret material or
  fabricated invoice, paid, settled, payout, earnings, revenue or balance truth
  is stored or shown.
- Founder/customer-success tools may inspect and support but never impersonate a member or rewrite source-owned work.
