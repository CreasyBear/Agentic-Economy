# Phase 4 — Business experience context

## Active decision

D-011 establishes a mature, customer-familiar Business Account operating surface. Acceptance authorizes documentation reconciliation, not implementation, hosted behavior, usability, accessibility in use, or production claims.

The persistent current-business shell is:

```text
Home
Work
Inbox
Offerings
Business settings
Help
```

Home answers whether customers can request work now, why, and what single next action matters. Work is the sole operational queue. Inbox is conversation. Help owns customer-visible support. History is evidence, not another queue. Integrations is canonical under Business settings, with contextual many-to-many links from Offerings and an optional role-aware shortcut to that same route.

Responsibilities are additive and initially exposed through familiar presets. Protected Ownership never emerges from permission union. Availability remains domain-owned behind a narrow shared projection. Customer, progressively disclosed technical, and founder/backstage surfaces remain separate. The shared experience must support paid digital information, appointment-shaped, and dispatch-shaped offerings without vertical-specific fields in shared contracts.

## Current-to-target boundary

Current source already owns business identity, public catalog facts, inquiry messages, action/work facts and results, capability contracts, and capability-supply publication/readiness facts. It does not yet supply the complete Business Account relationship, membership, support, closure, availability projection, account dashboard, or portfolio read models accepted by D-011. UI projections may summarize source facts but never become command truth.

The detailed classification is in `04-SOURCE-MAP.md`; UI behavior is governed by `04-UI-SPEC.md`; source and executable behavior remain authority for what exists today.
