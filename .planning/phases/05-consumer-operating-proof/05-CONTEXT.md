# Phase 5 — Demand-side quote-to-close and customer operating proof

## Assigned boundary

Phase 5 is the future consumer phase. It receives the historical three-quote
and quote-to-close ideas as research inputs, not inherited acceptance.

Its eventual decision is whether AE can take a real customer need through
sourcing, comparable options, selection, close/start and an observable outcome
against independently routeable businesses. That is where demand, provider
quality, customer value and operating evidence begin to be evaluated.

## Inputs expected from Phase 4

- mature Business Account membership and relationship truth;
- routeable offering, Integration, readiness and publication source;
- reachable human/agent operation semantics;
- Commercial, Usage and operation-payment separation;
- Work, uncertainty, reconciliation, pause/withdraw/offboard and history;
- exact source/local evidence and its claim ceiling.

Phase 5 must not reinterpret Phase 4 fixtures as customer demand, provider
fulfilment, payment, revenue or production proof.

## Candidate questions, not current scope

- Which customer segment has a sufficiently valuable and frequent job?
- Which options are materially comparable and why?
- What authority, payment and fulfilment evidence does close/start require?
- Which sales, incentive, liquidity and retention mechanism improves the real
  loop without corrupting neutral recommendation or source truth?

No Phase 5 child may be dispatched from this context alone. Founder acceptance,
fresh source mapping and a dedicated plan are required.
