# Phase 5 Plan 06: Public Answer-First Comparison Summary

AE now has a source-owned, no-login customer loop from an ordinary request
through registry discovery, exact Offering inspection, shortlist and comparison.
The lead surface gives the grounded posture, decisive differences and mandatory
caveats first. The complete exact-revision evidence remains user-operated and
one disclosure away.

## What became real

- The Perth website-developer cold start asks at most one material,
  source-owned clarification and preserves that choice through Answer Thread
  restoration.
- Public routes cover `/registry`, business detail, exact Offering detail and
  `/compare`; exact selection and stated priorities survive refresh, back and
  share.
- The comparison loader re-resolves current publication and the selected
  historical revision. A newer revision is disclosed separately and never
  substitutes for the selected record.
- The presentation adapter may change bounded density and emphasis only. It is
  digest-bound and cannot invent facts, ordering, caveats, actions or disclosure
  state. The deterministic comparison remains complete without it.
- The compact renderer uses equivalent description-list facts while the wide
  renderer uses a table. Fact identity and order remain shared.
- Local browser fixtures independently own labelled exact revision records at
  the comparison read-port boundary. They do not derive historical truth from
  current registry cards and cannot activate in production configuration.

- A proposed local-E2E shortcut that reconstructed exact history from current
  registry projection was rejected. Independent immutable fixture records now
  enter through the existing exact read-port boundary.
- Canonical comparison URL state remains one bounded JSON-array `selection`
  parameter. Test helpers were corrected rather than changing production URL
  semantics.
- Browser reduced-motion assertions accept Chromium's effective-zero
  `0.00001s` duration while retaining a strict one-millisecond ceiling.
- Reflow uses the 320-CSS-pixel equivalent of 1280px at 400% zoom; artificial
  CSS zoom was rejected because it bypasses responsive media queries.

## Evidence and claim ceiling

Evidence is committed source, focused fixtures, production build and labelled
local-mock browser execution. It proves the named customer loop and its
zero-consequence behavior at this revision.

It does not prove hosted behavior, live supply, provider availability,
fulfilment, demand, customer value, broad comprehension, production safety or
real screen-reader use.
