## Other ADRs

Other ADR files remain durable decisions or historical architecture provenance
for the source they govern. They do not define the current roadmap sequence and
must not override PRODUCT.md, DESIGN.md or the current chain above.

Duplicate historical numbers such as ADR-002 and ADR-004 are preserved rather
than silently renumbered. New ADRs use the next unused number.
