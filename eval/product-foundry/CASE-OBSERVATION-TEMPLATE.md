## Actors and authority

Record each participant, what they own, what they may decide, what they may
change, and what requires another person's approval.

## Facts and selection

Record facts supplied, facts repeated, unknowns discovered, eligibility rules,
comparison criteria, exclusions, and readiness or availability checks.

## Sequence

Record each message, call, search, form, document, decision, commitment, wait,
deadline, handoff, correction, rejection, cancellation, retry, reconciliation,
and recovery event in time order.
