DirectorySavedTools adapts the browser-local wishlist context from ShadcnSpace.
Sources (retrieved 2026-09-08):
https://github.com/shadcnspace/ecommerce-shadcn-nextjs-template/blob/main/components/wishlist-context.tsx
https://github.com/shadcnspace/ecommerce-shadcn-nextjs-template/blob/main/LICENSE

hydration without initial writes, storage failure feedback, and storage-event refresh.
