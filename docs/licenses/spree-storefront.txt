DirectoryFilters adapts the controlled filter and staged mobile drawer behavior from Spree Storefront.
DirectoryToolCard adapts the responsive card structure and stretched accessible interaction from ProductCard.
Sources (retrieved 2026-09-08):
https://github.com/spree/storefront/blob/main/src/components/products/filters/ProductFilters.tsx
https://github.com/spree/storefront/blob/main/src/components/products/filters/MobileFilterDrawer.tsx
https://github.com/spree/storefront/blob/main/src/components/products/ProductCard.tsx
Adaptations: AE network/provider/maximum USD fields, existing shadcn primitives, schema validation; no Spree backend, stock, sort, or counts.
