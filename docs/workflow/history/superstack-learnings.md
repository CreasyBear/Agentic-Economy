## Patterns

### derive-institution-before-company
- **Insight:** Foundational Agentic Economy writing should begin with the changed economic behaviour, define the missing institutional state, compare credible alternatives, and derive the company’s role only after the argument stands without the company name.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** research
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### separate-thesis-from-validation
- **Insight:** A category whitepaper should state a forward-looking, conditional thesis and its failure conditions without reducing the argument to present traction, MVP maturity, market sizing, or commercial validation.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** roast-my-product
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

## Pitfalls

### payment-is-not-purchase
- **Insight:** Never treat payment settlement as proof of the whole purchase or commercial closure as proof that cash settled; principal resale has separate buyer-consideration and upstream-provider obligations, and either may remain payable after the purchase reaches a terminal state.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** roast-my-product
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### do-not-overload-supplier
- **Insight:** Keep the upstream provider, fixed buyer-facing seller, and payment recipient distinct because collapsing them into “supplier” hides the mechanism and money flow of the principal-reseller model.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** roast-my-product
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### avoid-australian-legal-overreach
- **Insight:** Present Australia as the worked jurisdictional case, not the first or uniquely complete country; EDP status is not synonymous with merchant of record, APP 8 applies to APP entities, and prepaid balances may raise non-cash-payment or financial-services questions depending on their legal design.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** research
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### no-decorative-formalism
- **Insight:** Use equations only when their actors, states, assumptions, and derived threshold are explicit; mathematical notation that merely restates a definition weakens rather than strengthens a foundational paper.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** roast-my-product
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

## Preferences

### keep-category-definition-simple
- **Insight:** Use the locked one-sentence category definition without adding mechanism, protocol, or aphoristic language: “Agentic Economy is a marketplace where agents working for people and companies find, compare, and buy services from businesses and other agents.”
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** manual
- **Date:** 2026-08-23

### use-just-in-time-procurement
- **Insight:** Use “just-in-time service procurement” as the canonical category term; avoid “late-bound service procurement,” which reads like software architecture rather than an economic behaviour.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** manual
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### foundational-not-marketing
- **Insight:** Whitepapers for Agentic Economy must be rigorous enough to stand as citable theses: technically literate, layered, source-backed, plain-spoken, and free of investor-copy slogans, simplistic openings, or premature “why us” assertions.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** unslop
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

## Architecture

### agentic-economy-product-boundary
- **Insight:** Agentic Economy is infrastructure for agents and must not assume ownership of the user’s agent, harness, planning, memory, approvals, or orchestration; product and investment thinking should remain anchored in the marketplace described by the locked category sentence.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** manual
- **Date:** 2026-08-23

### commercial-closure-core
- **Insight:** Agentic Economy’s institutional role is commercial closure for just-in-time service procurement: linking the principal, delegated authority, exact Operation, fixed seller, selected provider, consideration states, terminal delivery state, remedy, and accounting evidence into one explainable purchase.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** research
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md, PRODUCT.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### market-upstream-seller-downstream
- **Insight:** Agentic Economy is a two-sided market for provider discovery and allocation upstream, and a principal reseller at the buyer-facing boundary downstream; the provider is selected just in time while the seller remains fixed.
- **Confidence:** 9/10
- **Source:** research
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md, PRODUCT.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### principal-resale-is-conditional
- **Insight:** Principal resale is the preferred institution when buyers value one seller and common remedy, provider admission can be reused, and the intermediary controls the bounded delivery promise; it is not the only possible occupant of the role and direct integration remains superior for predictable repeated supply.
- **Confidence:** 9/10
- **Source:** research
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01

### commitment-is-decision-boundary
- **Insight:** The commitment between discovery and invocation is the critical decision boundary because it binds the principal’s standing mandate to one exact Operation revision, provider, price, terms, data treatment, effects, evidence, expiry, and retry rule before an irreversible action occurs.
- **Confidence:** 10/10
- **Source:** research
- **Files:** AGENTIC_ECONOMY_AUSTRALIA_WHITEPAPER.md, PRODUCT.md
- **Date:** 2026-09-01
