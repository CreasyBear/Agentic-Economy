## What does your AI do, and for whom?

Today, buying a service from another business requires human back-and-forth. Someone finds a vendor on a website, sends an email, waits for a quote, signs an agreement, and pays an invoice. That admin overhead means small, one-off jobs rarely happen between strangers.

Meanwhile, AI agents are beginning to run autonomous tasks inside companies. These agents can already choose tools and pay per call using micropayment protocols like x402. But an agent can only buy what software can read. Almost every useful service in the Australian economy is delivered by people, buried behind phone numbers and enquiry forms. A business might have top-tier expertise, but software cannot find it, price it, or buy it.

Agentic Economy bridges that gap. The platform gives real-world business services a machine-readable definition. It specifies required inputs, output formats, fixed prices, delivery timelines, and what happens if something goes wrong. That definition is the contract. Once defined, an AI agent can discover the service, buy it within pre-set corporate budget rules, and receive verified proof of delivery.

The seller does not have to reinvent how they work. A consultancy can deliver the work by hand today and return the output in the contracted format. When they eventually automate their process with AI, they export the exact same format at the same price. The buyer never has to renegotiate.

This is built for Australia's small and mid-sized service businesses, giving them a way to sell directly to software without hiring a sales team.

## What results can you point to?

The core platform is built and running in code.

An AI agent can already query the catalogue, inspect a contract, and complete a test run using our command line tool or our API.

We implemented cryptographic payment verification for x402 transactions. The platform verifies signed offers and receipts, checking that the payout goes to the right address and that the signing key matches the seller's domain before executing.

Every dollar movement is tracked through a double-entry ledger. Around 6,000 automated tests enforce the system boundaries. Production billing and payouts remain intentionally turned off while we finish our legal and tax reviews.

We also mapped the Australian statutory records that software agents need to buy but currently cannot access, including land titles, corporate registries, and environmental data.

We are currently participating in the AI Founder Sprint run by Spacecubed and Superteam Australia in Perth.

We do not have paying customers yet. Our immediate goal is running the first live, legally compliant transaction where an AI agent buys a real service from a Western Australian firm and generates a valid tax invoice.
