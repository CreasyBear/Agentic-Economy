# Someone has to be the seller

*A thesis. September 2026.*

## 1. What changed

For as long as businesses have bought anything, the supplier was known before the need. You needed steel, then you found a merchant. You needed a lawyer, then you hired one. The order of events never varied.

That order has broken.

Software can now pursue an objective, discover partway through that it lacks something, and go find a party that has it. The supplier becomes known after the need instead of before. This is not a faster version of the old process. It inverts it.

## 2. Why that breaks a purchase

A purchase is not a transfer of money. It is a claim. It says a specific legal person bought a specific thing from a specific seller, on stated terms, and the thing arrived or it did not. That claim has to survive an accountant, an auditor, a tax authority and a dispute.

Every part of it needs one thing. A seller with standing.

Someone has to be the party the buyer contracted with. Someone has to issue the invoice and take the tax position. Someone has to warrant what was bought and carry the remedy when it fails. Someone has to be answerable.

When the supplier is chosen at runtime, nobody is in that position. The buyer has money. The provider has a service. Neither has a commercial relationship with the other, and neither wants one for a two dollar lookup. Payment can settle perfectly and the purchase still cannot be completed, because a purchase needs a seller and there is not one.

This is the gap. It is not a payment problem. Payment works. It is a standing problem.

## 3. Why nobody has filled it

Three parties are close enough to take the role. All three decline it.

Payment networks stay neutral by design. Their business is to be everywhere and blameless. Taking responsibility for what was bought, under one country's tax and consumer law, is the opposite of that.

The model and platform companies sell intelligence and logistics. Accepting liability for a third party's data or computation, in a jurisdiction they do not operate in, is a cost they have no reason to carry.

The providers will not do it either. A specialist with one good dataset will not register for goods and services tax, issue consumer warranties and hold seven years of records in order to sell three dollar lookups to strangers.

So the role sits open. It is open because it is unattractive, not because it is undiscovered. That is the best kind of open.

## 4. What the business is

A business that holds standing for other people's work.

It admits providers. It states the terms of sale to the buyer. It fixes the price before the call. It records what was bought and whether it arrived. It carries the remedy when it did not. Upstream it operates a market. Downstream it is the seller.

The unit is not a tool and not an endpoint. It is a bounded purchase. Fixed inputs, a fixed price, a stated delivery condition, accepted terms, a stated remedy, and a record that survives an audit.

The buyer's experience is deliberately dull. They pay in their own currency, on ordinary terms, and receive one invoice. Everything unusual about how the service was found and settled happens on the other side of that invoice.

## 5. What has to be true

Three things. Each can be shown false.

**One. There is work buyers need and cannot get from a vendor they already have.** Not general capability. Specific, legally gated, jurisdiction bound or credentialed. A title record. A regulatory register. A grid constraint. A certifier's sign off. Things with no free substitute and no self serve account.

**Two. A buyer will pay someone else to hold the standing.** The test is not whether they will delegate a purchase. It is whether they would rather use a party that already carries the tax, the warranty and the record than appoint a function to carry it themselves. A five person firm cannot run procurement for forty capabilities. It can buy from one seller.

**Three. The role stays open.** Payment rails must stay neutral, and platforms must stay unwilling to accept liability in each jurisdiction they serve. Both have held for twenty years of online commerce. Neither has shown a sign of changing.

## 6. Where it starts

Australia. Not because the technology starts there, and not because the market is largest. Because the conditions for a first honest test are already in place.

One goods and services tax. One company register. One consumer law that reaches digital services. The compliant path can be built once and sold nationally instead of rebuilt for every customer.

A dense field of legally gated data. Land titles, corporate extracts, security registers, planning overlays, environmental records, energy dispatch. Each has a statutory character, a real price, and no free substitute. These are precisely the purchases that cannot be completed without a seller.

A supply side with no sales function. Around nine hundred thousand Australian firms have fewer than twenty staff, and most of them are specialists. They already hold the capability. They have never had a way to sell it one task at a time.

And a population that has already settled how it pays. Contactless is almost all in person card payments because it asked for nothing new. PayTo asked for new behaviour and has stalled for years. Any product that asks an Australian business to learn a payment method, hold a new asset or change its accounts fails on behaviour before it fails on merit. The rail has to be invisible.

## 7. How it fails

**The purchasing stays human.** Runtime spend never becomes normal, so the buyer is always a person and the product becomes an ordinary procurement tool for small firms. A real business. A much smaller one.

**The long tail is empty.** Buyers want only the thirty capabilities that platforms already bundle. Then there is no standing role to hold, because a platform can hold it for its own catalogue.

**The take rate cannot cover the responsibility.** Carrying tax, warranty and refund liability on a three dollar sale may be unprofitable at any volume. Then the role belongs to whoever can absorb it as a cost of something else.

**A platform takes the standing role everywhere.** The signal is precise. A platform accepting liability for a third party's failure, in the buyer's jurisdiction, at scale. None has. If one does, the space closes.

The fourth is the one to watch. The other three leave a smaller company. The fourth leaves no company.

## 8. The claim

Every market that let strangers trade had to solve the same problem first. Not settlement, and not discovery. Standing. Someone had to become the seller so that two parties who had never met could complete a purchase they both wanted.

Software has just started choosing its own suppliers. That creates the same problem again, in a place nobody has built for it. aecon is the seller for work chosen at runtime. It takes the tax position, the record and the remedy so that a business can let software buy without letting it become unaccountable.

The rail is somebody else's problem. The sale is ours.
