# Papercut and hostility audit, 2026-10-10

Source: the main checkout. Poteto auditors rendered each page from source and tried to break it, then haiku skeptics removed high findings they could refute.

## The read
Aecon speaks to users in its own ledger vocabulary. Words like "admitted", "assessed", "Routeable" and "readback", raw enums and 64-hex refs sit where a buyer expects a name and a price. Money is the hardest thing to read, and in one place the GST statement contradicts the calculation it describes. Pages pile up jobs instead of splitting them, so triage screens double as accounting workbenches and platform-ops panels land on customer pages. Too many states lie or dead-end: an outage looks like "you have no agents", "Not now" permanently declines a purchase, and the home page hides the market.

## Themes
1. **Internal vocabulary as UI copy.** Every Tool page shows "Title source: business published / Readiness: Routeable". Raw `replaceAll('_',' ')` enums appear in at least 6 surfaces (provenance, Plan refusals, Visibility/Disposition, notification history, saved reports, admin drawers). "AE" reaches users on market cards, the OAuth callback, the supply flow and MCP.
2. **Price hidden, duplicated or wrong.**
   - There are 4 currency labels (A$, US$, USD, USDC) across card, filter and badge.
   - The price appears 3 times on `/tools/$toolRef`.
   - The chat sheet says "Add A$5" with no total, and "GST applies to the service fee" while the code taxes principal plus fee.
   - Call receipt line items are only inside the collapsed Technical details.
   - The admin Quote page has no price, and providers see "Price 10000".
   - Times use 5 conventions: UTC, Perth, browser local, ISO and en-US.
3. **The product makes the user do its job.**
   - Users click through empty pages in 4 places: the market index filter, the `/activity` status filter, admin Purchases and Exceptions.
   - Invoices and monthly statements must be generated by hand.
   - Admin search asks for the object type, which the ref prefix already encodes.
   - The Team page locks after a successful change until you press Refresh.
4. **States that lie or dead-end.**
   - On `/agent-access`, an outage renders the new-user funnel and hides pending approvals.
   - "No published output example" is hard-coded.
   - Veteran owners are stuck on "Get a Tool result" forever.
   - The Update Price modal is mounted but never wired.
   - `/fund/cancelled` is unreachable.
5. **One page, three jobs, everything twice.**
   - `/activity` has about 12 download buttons from 5 export systems.
   - Audit hosts approvals and admin membership, Treasury hosts commercial policy, and the Credit page hosts daily close.
   - `/market` has 2 primary buttons and `/agent-access` has 3 "Connect an Agent" buttons.
   - The h1 is repeated as an h2 on Agents, Tools, Add Tool and every admin object page.
   - There are 3 footer systems and 2 brand names.
6. **Availability and trust contradicted.**
   - `/for-agents` hands out setup commands, then says at the bottom that purchases aren't open, while `/about` and the footer say you can pay.
   - Chat promises "You approve every purchase", but turning on payment allows up to A$1 per answer and A$5 a day.
   - The legal pages publish reviewer notes with no named entity or privacy contact.

## Agent-facing hostility
1. **MCP success text has no content.** It is "`${name}: ${kind}.`" (`src/lib/server/mcp-api.ts:230`). Price, output and receipt exist only in structuredContent, so text-only hosts can't show the price or return the result.
2. **MCP errors carry only the title** ("Failed precondition", `src/modules/actions/contract.ts:292`). On a read-only connection, `mcp_toolset_required` returns `next: []` and never says to reconnect without `?readonly=1` (`mcp-api.ts:603`).
3. **The MCP instructions are a 650-word paragraph with relative URLs** the model can't resolve (`mcp-api.ts:52`). They should be about 6 lines with absolute links.
4. **The CLI prints money as "50 × 10^-2 USD"** (`tools/ae/commands/call.ts:401`, `tools/ae/commands/account.ts:170`). Also, `ae call` re-quotes, so the price you inspected is not the price you pay (`call.ts:403`). Add `--quote <quoteRef>`.
5. **Getting from describe to buy is guesswork.**
   - `ae describe` lists input names with no types or example JSON.
   - Input mismatches don't name the field.
   - `ae cancel` and `ae recover` demand an idempotency key.
   - `ae fund` prints a JSON blob instead of opening the browser.
   - Root help links to a repo path (`docs/glossary.md`).
6. **The public docs are internal.**
   - `/SKILL.md` is compressed past comprehension and lists 4 buy paths with no guidance on which to use.
   - `/glossary.md` points readers at `src/` files and leaves out callRef, quoteRef and outcome_unknown.
   - `/llms.txt` pages through the whole catalogue on every request, uses none of it, and returns a 503 when Convex is down.
