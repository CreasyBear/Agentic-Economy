Static web data is dead. Only three things command a price from AI agents.

Category 01
Live, Ephemeral State

Real-time order books, power grid dispatch, live shipping rates, sub-second telemetry. An LLM cannot answer today's state from last month's training weights.

Category 02
Gated Offline Records

Statutory land titles, corporate share registers, court filings, private market cap tables. Information that never touches the open web and cannot be scraped.

Category 03
Licensed Human Authority

Fire engineering reviews, legal precedent opinions, geotechnical sign-offs. Outcomes that require licensed human accountability and professional standing.

aecon deliberately ignores generic web scraping and static articles. We anchor our marketplace entirely on the three categories that models cannot absorb for free.

The three kill conditions for the agentic economy, and how we test them.

Kill Condition 01
The Scraping Trap
The Threat: If services provide static info, AI crawlers scrape them once and models answer for free forever.
The Falsifier: We only onboard ephemeral state and statutory offline records that require fresh runtime queries.
Kill Condition 02
The Demand Lag
The Threat: If autonomous enterprise agents take two years to arrive, machine purchasing volume stays near zero.
The Falsifier: Tools sell to human developers and software scripts today. We do not wait for autonomous agents.
Kill Condition 03
The SME Conversion Wall
The Threat: Small firms will not spend time drafting API schemas for speculative software revenue.
The Falsifier: aecon wraps existing web portals and handles contract mapping on the supplier's behalf.

Every parallel in history shows that exchanges take 3 to 5 years to mature. We test these three conditions across 10 core providers in 12 months.
