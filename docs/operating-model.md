**Status:** maintained boundary summary · **Reconciled:** 5 October 2026

## Supported lane

The Customer holds AUD credit usable within the supported AE service. It is not
cash, transferable value or customer-owned crypto. For supported resale, AE is
the buyer-facing Seller and pays the Provider from its own corporate USDC treasury.
Provider performance, payee identity and the Provider obligation remain separate.

A Quote binds the specific purchase and authority before a Call. Preserve buyer
Charge, fee, refund and net use independently from Provider cost and settlement.
Settlement alone does not establish delivery, quality or purchase resolution.
Uncertainty keeps the original reference and supported recovery path.

Price presentation follows `PricingConfig` and the canonical money projection.
Do not treat an old policy proposal or research example as the current rate,
rounding rule, tax fact or charge authority.

## Alpha and broader launch

The recorded paid acceptance uses Admin authority and promotional credit.
Production remains Admin-only; the proposed 20-user invitation cohort still needs
[#288](https://github.com/CreasyBear/Agentic-Economy/issues/288)'s go/no-go.
The approved `alpha_not_screened_v1` exception explicitly defers payee screening.
Current limits and environment identities are in the [deployment map](operations/deployments.md).

The 20 mainnet purchases prove the scoped financial/execution foundation. They
do not prove external demand, open-beta funding, paid chat or a complete
Australian principal-reseller record. The [readiness record](production-readiness-plan.md)
keeps production effects, fixtures and remaining evidence distinct.

## Qualification still required

Retain the unresolved legal/AML/CTF, tax, banking and processor questions in the
[original start-line boundary](archive/2026-10-05/root/START_LINE.md#australian-production-boundary)
and the [operating-model research](archive/2026-10-05/operating-model.md).

conditions and effective date. Neither software capability nor a configured
account establishes that approval.

Before broader launch, reconcile the actual contracts and implementation for
funding fees, GST timing/documents/adjustments, refunds and disputes, retained
customer liability, treasury funding, sanctions policy, data handling, support
and production recovery. Proposed percentages, float targets, cohort credits and
beta controls remain proposals unless the effective policy and evidence establish
them. Open questions have not been closed by this consolidation.
