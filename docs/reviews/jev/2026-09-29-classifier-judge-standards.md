# Findings

**Decision surface, not a generative judge.** The installed `@typesafe-ai/sdk` declarations (`node_modules/@typesafe-ai/sdk/dist/index.d.mts:39-116`) and TypeSafe's [primitive guide](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction) permit a shared `state` plus independently evaluated `choice` (named, described alternatives), `score` (ordered descriptions indexed from zero; answer is an *expected rubric score*), and `noul` (probability of yes) questions in one request. `choice`/`score` return a probability distribution and a separate concentration-derived `confidence`; **confidence is not the probability the decision is correct** ([TypeSafe confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence)). Question IDs identify answers but are not sent to the model ([Noul guide](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/noul)); put all semantic definitions in `instructions`/`criteria`, not IDs. `scoreToolSearch` builds one `noul` question per candidate in a gateway request (`src/modules/registry/tool-reranker.ts:320-408`); `presentToolSearch` additionally puts its presentation `choice` in that same request (`:214-225`). `rankToolsForSelection` applies reliability/adoption/price/prior-use evidence without a model (`src/modules/registry/tool-selection-score.ts:27-69`); keep that independent of model availability. The fallback's `score: 0` and heuristic `noul` are **not** Jev-quality judgments (`src/modules/decision-engine/deterministic-fallback.ts:41-130`). The ~1500 ms budget is aecon's constraint, **not a demonstrated latency of any cited experiment**.

**Evidence boundary.** Anthropic's [classification cookbook](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/capabilities-classification-guide) reports simple 0.74, retrieved examples 0.94, and retrieved examples plus chain-of-thought 0.97 accuracy on **68 synthetic held-out insurance tickets**, with 68 synthetic training examples; this is an illustrative ablation, not measured transfer to Jev or commercial Tool listings. The TypeSafe docs describe concurrency but give no aecon-specific latency/accuracy trial. IR papers measure passage-relevance nDCG, not paid-tool selection or reliability. Qualify every port with held-out buyer-intent labels and measured p95 latency.

## Failure modes

| Failure | Primary URL and observed scope | Practical guardrail / affected aecon symbol |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First/last position wins independent of quality | [Zheng et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05685): GPT-4 65% preference consistency under A/B swap, Claude-v1 23.8% in their difficult similar-answer set; [Shi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07791): systematic bias across 15 judges / >150k evaluations | `tool-reranker.ts` shortlist choice: preserve stable retrieval order in production; **offline** randomize and reverse candidate order to quantify top-1 flip rate. One-call choice cannot perform the paper's double-order confirmation. |
| Verbosity beats evidence | [Zheng et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05685): duplicated-list attack succeeds 91.3% with Claude-v1/GPT-3.5, 8.7% with GPT-4, on 23 cases | `tool-reranker.ts` `candidateState`: compare equal-budget summaries, tell `noul` to require demonstrated capability; test duplicate/marketing padding. Extrapolation to Tool listings is unmeasured. |
| Self-preference / sycophancy | [Zheng et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05685) saw apparent self-favouring but explicitly **could not establish** self-enhancement causally; [Sharma et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13548) measured user-opinion sycophancy in assistant generation/preference models, **not Jev judging** | No claim Jev favours its own outputs. Offline blind candidate/provider provenance; test user-asserted favourite against task facts for `tool-reranker.ts` and observed outcomes in `tool-selection-score.ts`. |
| Label collapse / majority prediction | [Anthropic classifier](https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/capabilities-classification-guide): simple classifier recalled **zero** General Inquiries on synthetic 68-case set; [Reif & Schwartz](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02743): bias across 279 classification tasks and ten LLMs; [Gupta et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16549): majority-demo effect varies by model/task | `presentationQuestion`/`USE_CRITERIA`: non-overlapping labels and representative rare/no-match examples; offline per-label recall and balanced confusion matrix, not aggregate accuracy alone. |
| Example or rubric order changes decision | [Lu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.08786): few-shot sample order changed performance near SOTA → random on their classifiers; [Li et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.22316): score rubric order, score IDs, reference-score bias in studied LLM judges | `tool-reranker.ts` criteria variants: offline label-order and example-order perturbation tests. `score` rubric remains genuinely ordered 0→N; don't randomly reorder rubric and still interpret score as the same quantity. |
| Independent pointwise scores are not automatically cross-item calibrated | [Qin et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.17563), [Zhuang et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.09497): relative comparisons stronger on tested retrieval benchmarks, at greater inference cost | `tool-reranker.ts` `scoreToolSearch`: `noul` is per-item probability of yes, **not** probability that item is best. Keep stable ties and compare with small-set `choice`; `tool-selection-score.ts` keeps actual price/outcomes authoritative. |
| Listwise permutation failure or initial-order dependence | [Qin et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.17563): missing/repeated/unparseable passage IDs; [Sun et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.09542): initial ordering materially changes RankGPT nDCG | Do not invent a permutation question: SDK offers only `choice`/`score`/`noul`. Keep `tool-reranker.ts` pointwise results and stable retrieval ordering; offline top-1 setwise trial is not a full ranking. |
| Wrong answer or unverifiable judge rationale | [Zheng et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05685): math judge default wrong 14/20, independent-reference method wrong 3/20; [OpenAI graders](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/graders): grader reasoning is a *different* output contract | No chain-of-thought/text rationale in Jev answers. `tool-selection-score.ts` `toolSelectionReasons` may describe only real observed evidence, not a fabricated model explanation; `deterministic-fallback.ts` heuristic output is not independent verification. |

## Highest value three

1. **Sharpen the existing single-call `noul` eligibility and `choice` labels**, with explicit negative near-misses, price/availability distinction, and no-match semantics (`tool-reranker.ts`). Evidence: Anthropic's small synthetic ablation plus first-party TypeSafe primitive semantics; measure local error deltas before shipping prompt changes.
2. **Keep semantic qualification separate from observed selection evidence** (`tool-reranker.ts` → `tool-selection-score.ts`). This preserves a zero-network choice path and prevents a persuasive listing from overriding recorded failures; measure regret against independently labeled buyer winners.
3. **Evaluate a tiny-set winner `choice` only for ambiguous top-1**, while retaining per-item `noul` and deterministic ranking (`tool-reranker.ts`, `tool-selection-score.ts`). Setwise/pairwise passage results motivate a trial, **not a proven Jev improvement**; compare top-1 accuracy, no-match false-positive rate, order flips and p95 latency against current behavior.

# Rejected

- **Binary-then-explain as one Jev result:** `noul` has only a number, `choice` label/probabilities/confidence, and `score` ordered levels/probabilities/confidence; no rationale field in the installed SDK or [TypeSafe primitives](https://docs.typesafe.ai/introduction). Adding generative explanation is a second model/path, not a free port. Code-derived observed reasons remain valid.
- **Full RankGPT permutation and PRP sorting in one choice:** [RankGPT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.09542) generates a list of IDs over sliding windows; [PRP](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.17563) runs repeated A/B comparisons, including both orders. Neither is representable by a single Jev `choice` question returning one label. Don't claim the small-set winner above reproduces either ranking algorithm.
- **Per-query reversed-order voting, self-consistency, multiple samples, independent solve-then-judge:** [Zheng et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.05685) show benefits for some benchmark failures; [Xiong et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13063) report no uniformly best confidence elicitation method. All require more model work/round trips than one-call 1500 ms can guarantee. Use offline audits, not the normal path.
- **Small-model-then-escalate by default:** [Anthropic Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) gives a routing example but no measured result for Jev plus a second judge here. A serial second call consumes the remaining budget; the existing deterministic scorer is the availability path, not an invented second-model cascade.
- **Treating `choice.confidence`, `score.score`, `noul`, or fallback `confidence` interchangeably:** [TypeSafe confidence](https://docs.typesafe.ai/confidence), [TypeSafe Score](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/score), and the [confidence-elicitation experiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13063) give distinct semantics; aecon's `deterministic-fallback.ts:82-128` emits heuristic constants. No primary study here calibrates Jev against aecon outcomes.
- **Distractor-proof labels or deduplication as a proven accuracy gain for Jev:** sensible from [TypeSafe Choice](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/choice), but the fetched literature does not isolate this intervention for Jev or buyer Tool selection. Treat as a testable design hypothesis, not a performance claim.
