# Backend takedown map — 28 September 2026

**Scope and standard.** Read-only source audit of Convex cron registration, boot stages, catalogue/index/aggregate writes, background jobs, Quote/Call dependencies, reactive reads and rollout switches. Severity means **app-down** (deployment/local stack cannot start), **journey-down** (one named core action or page fails), or **degraded** (optional data, freshness or a secondary shelf fails). Counts: **2 app-down, 10 journey-down, 8 degraded**. A path marked *risk, not observed incident* has a demonstrable failure chain but no observed production failure. Estimates are rough source lines **removed/added** for the smallest correction, not a migration budget. Local `/version` answered `unknown` (backend liveness only); no production Insights/OCC telemetry or paid call was exercised. Do not confuse code risk with measured failures.

Binding decision: `docs/reviews/maturity/00-decisions.md:9-17`. Convex, identity when required, and the actual Provider are legitimate dependencies. Money, authority, settlement and data-integrity refusals must **remain**; bypassing Formance balance, live x402 requirement, MFA, permissions or a payment reconciliation state is not a fix. Optional Jev/FX refresh/directory scan/analytics are not prerequisites to deterministic browse, a last-good bounded-age price, receipts or boot. Existing last-good catalogue latching in `convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/rows.ts:60-74` is a *good* boundary; preserve it.

## Defensive-branch inventory and exclusions

- **Delete/flatten once replacements exist:** global cron disable and malformed-config throw (`convex/crons.ts:23-47`); local `--skip-scan`, `--skip-seed` and fail-closed stage ladder (`tools/dev/local-dev.ts:217-247,563-660`); four supply rollout switches (`src/lib/server/package5-rollout.ts:5-85`); indexed-search `analytics_building` refusal for ordinary results (`convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/browse.ts:107-112,193-197`); Quote `pricing_setup_required` on a transient live FX miss (`convex/lib/capabilityExecution/quotes/quoteAction.ts:88-89`). These are failure-producing branches, not protection of settled money.
- **Prefer removal rather than another fallback layer:** reporting uses `unavailable` for *index missing*, *analytics building* and *invalid query* (`convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/lookup.ts:50-63`, `reporting.ts:51-55`, `facetReads.ts:33-46`), three unrelated states; distinguish validation from infrastructure, and provide deterministic first-party results when optional index/analytics are missing. Avoid a second mutable cache or generic catch-all `unavailable` layer.
- **Keep real boundaries:** `convex/lib/capabilityExecution/quotes/quoteValuation.ts:202-217,254-299` (current balance/budget evidence), `convex/lib/capabilityExecution/quotes/quoteAction.ts:76-87,137-180` (live x402/treasury/funds), `convex/lib/capabilityExecution/callWorker/reconcileScheduled.ts:128-188` (refund/reconciliation), `convex/lib/dev/seed/identity.ts:187-197` (never seed a production principal), `convex/auth.config.ts:3-20` (Clerk identity issuer). Never convert those guards to success on error. Coinbase Bazaar is core cold-start supply and remains in search; isolate refresh, **do not cut the directory**.
- **Already bounded/good:** search page reads have 12-result and read/byte caps (`convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/browse.ts:123-149,235-242`), native scan is split into five-entry writes (`convex/x402DirectoryIndexSource.ts:63-74`), market presence uses 20-publication pages (`convex/marketPresence.ts:82-117`), and a failed refresh retains the complete-generation read latch (`convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/rows.ts:60-74`). Do not replace them with whole-table reads.

**Prioritise:** (1) decouple boot seeds/scan; (2) persist bounded-age FX; (3) harden aggregate idempotency after a reproducer; (4) separate provider publication from directory projection; (5) keep paid Call reconciliation scheduled independently. For OCC and category limits, obtain live Insights before a structural rewrite. This document proposes changes only; source was not edited.
