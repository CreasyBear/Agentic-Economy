Exact paging/priority policy is a product decision, not set here.

- Candidate disposition: **redesign** actionable read/expiry lifecycle with explicit completeness and truthful controls; **safer and more ergonomic**. Do not infer a cron is required: read-time validity and write-time lifecycle are alternative contracts for design.
