# The quiet things

## The one-paragraph truth

Joel, AEcon already has the difficult part of a real product: a searchable x402 Bazaar, an Australian buyer account, bounded Quotes and Calls, a corporate-USDC settlement path, and a record of uncertainty rather than a blind second payment. It is not yet a short, proved public purchase journey. The first-run walk reached listings but no buyer result or paid Call; meanwhile the code offers a second chat entrance, several publisher and operator products, and two frontend data paths around that core. Alpha needs one obvious route from a need to an eligible Tool, an exact AUD Quote, a funded Call and an understandable receipt. Keep the market broad and the money/identity rules strict; remove competing ways of explaining or operating the same transaction. This is a recommendation about scope, not a claim that the production legal gate has passed. [Product charter](../../../PRODUCT.md#L26-L42) · [start line](../../../START_LINE.md#L136-L180) · [first-run observation](first-run.md#L31-L42).

## The quiet things

Ranked by harm to the first public paid purchase. Each observation from the first-wave reviews was checked against the cited source; live observations remain labelled as such.

1. **The catalogue gets people to a listing more readily than to a Call.** The first-run buyer walk stopped at a Bazaar card saying no AE Quote or Call; the directory-detail component explicitly prints that limit ([observed walk](first-run.md#L77-L100), [source](../../../src/components/ae/market/public-tool-detail/DirectoryToolPage.tsx#L29-L35)). **Costs Joel:** users who mistake inventory for an executable result.
2. **The tax story cannot be two numbers for one payment.** Funding quotes tax only the top-up fee, while sandbox Call tax is zero and a separate inclusive-gross GST splitter exists; the money review observed A$0.03 at Checkout and A$0.48 on the same A$5.28 top-up in the receipt list/CSV ([funding calculation](../../../src/modules/money/internal/aud-funding.ts#L65-L71), [tax policy](../../../src/modules/money/internal/commercial-policy.ts#L100-L105), [inclusive splitter](../../../src/modules/money/internal/tax-invoice-control.ts#L72-L84), [observed discrepancy](money.md#L124-L130)). **Costs Joel:** money, accountant trust and a live-launch hold until an adviser approves one treatment.
3. **The first box asks a different product to answer.** Market's hero submits to `/t/new`, not Market search; the observed settled chat turn had no answer or card, although the catalogue could return candidates ([source](../../../src/components/ae/market/AeX402Directory.tsx#L454-L469), [observed walk](first-run.md#L48-L66)). **Costs Joel:** first-use conversion and model-dependent latency before value.
4. **The home page sends detail data before anyone asks for detail.** The loader fetches a catalogue page plus editorial rails, each rail reads the stored directory, but paints only eight entries; the measured local Vite document was 1,271,145 uncompressed bytes, mostly dehydrated state with schemas absent from the DOM ([loader](../../../src/modules/market/market-route-data.ts#L244-L263), [rails](../../../src/modules/market/x402-marketplace-home.server.ts#L16-L24), [paint limit](../../../src/components/ae/market/DirectoryHomeDiscovery.tsx#L58-L62), [local measurement](frontend-code.md#L116-L146)). **Costs Joel:** load time and extra bytes on every first visit; that byte figure is **not** a production transfer claim.
5. **A Provider must navigate a publisher platform to prove one payee.** The source accepts x402 only through an `x402_wallet` candidate and a signed URL/method/payee claim, while the form also carries free/fixed pricing and an environment choice that can disagree with the endpoint ([candidate](../../../src/modules/capability-supply/source-preview.ts#L626-L652), [claim](../../../src/modules/capability-supply/internal/supply-funnel/connections.ts#L193-L231), [extra form](../../../src/components/ae/supply/SupplyPublicationFlow.tsx#L231-L251), [mismatch](../../../src/modules/capability-supply/supply-publication-v2.ts#L145-L146)). **Costs Joel:** supply activation speed; the observed publisher walk did not reach a published Tool ([review](supply.md#L83-L85)).
6. **A read can have two lifetimes on one page.** Market's route reads Convex through HTTP-backed server functions; signed-in admin reads through `convex/react`, while its Formance fact comes from a server function ([market](../../../src/modules/market/x402-directory-index.server.ts#L115-L145), [HTTP client](../../../src/lib/server/convex-source.ts#L125-L133), [admin](../../../src/components/ae/admin/overview/useAdminOverview.ts#L28-L54), [Formance](../../../src/components/ae/admin/overview/readAdminOverview.functions.ts#L1-L10)). **Costs Joel:** separate caches, extra loading states and two ways to debug stale facts.
7. **The alpha operator is offered six destinations instead of the few actions that protect it.** Admin navigation lists Overview, Search, Exceptions, Providers, Metrics and Audit; its command envelope already carries reason, evidence, expected state and proof ([nav](../../../src/components/ae/layout/app-shell/app-shell.contracts.ts#L43-L50), [command](../../../convex/lib/adminConsole/commands/contracts.ts#L25-L45)). **Costs Joel:** operating surface and maintenance; retain entitlement, audit, suppression, source-authority review, treasury observation and recovery, not necessarily the console.
8. **The public names still disagree about the same Tool and Call.** The opaque Tool ref says `operation:v1:`, while Call is a separate stored record and purchase status is derived from its result; do not casually rename the hash or merge an Offering with a Publication ([ref](../../../src/modules/capability-supply/internal/public-tool-ref.ts#L6-L22), [Call record](../../../src/modules/capability-execution/internal/convex-schema.ts#L405-L408), [status projection](../../../src/modules/capability-execution/call-receipt-view.ts#L234-L238), [distinctions](../../../AGENTS.md#L51-L69)). **Costs Joel:** comprehension and risky cross-layer renames.
9. **The agent has three spellings of a purchase, not three implementations to maintain forever.** MCP admits actions through a named toolset; CLI separately maps `tool.quote` to `call` and `call.reconcile` to `recover` ([MCP](../../../src/modules/actions/mcp-toolsets.ts#L4-L24), [CLI](../../../tools/ae/commands/action-adapters.ts#L61-L107)). **Costs Joel:** drift for agent users; keep necessary CLI-local login/recovery help, not a second resource model.
10. **Per-screen contracts can cost more than the screen.** The market entry flows through browse, a server mapper and a card model; changing a card field crosses several representations, though the compact directory search already supplies most card facts ([browse](../../../convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/browse.ts#L243-L253), [mapper](../../../src/modules/market/x402-directory-index.server.ts#L130-L141), [compact search](../../../convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/browse.ts#L188-L225), [field trace](frontend-code.md#L103-L114)). **Costs Joel:** edit speed; isolation is worth keeping at payment, authority and true shared presentation boundaries.
11. **Some process protects settlement, while some process protects yesterday's file shape.** `check` runs import/size/copy guards but not the paid-path suite; `gate` runs unit and integration suites, while source instructions still require it per merge ([scripts](../../../package.json#L24-L42), [instructions](../../../AGENTS.md#L157-L165)). **Costs Joel:** slow fixes and a false sense that a green quick check has proved a Call.

**A claim we rejected:** the earlier health scorecard says default Market still waits on six *live* Coinbase searches and a Jev rerank ([older scorecard](../alpha/health/00-scorecard.md#L11-L20)); current rails read the **persisted** index and the current catalogue explicitly skips a live Jev wait ([rails](../../../src/modules/market/x402-marketplace-home.server.ts#L16-L18), [catalogue](../../../src/modules/market/x402-directory-index.server.ts#L142-L145)). Do not rebuild around an already removed bottleneck. Likewise, do not take the architecture review's proposal to sideline the Bazaar or drop CLI as a binding decision: Joel explicitly retained Bazaar in search and a public CLI release ([decisions](../maturity/00-decisions.md#L21-L28), [CLI decision](../maturity/00-decisions.md#L33-L37)).

## Decisions only Joel can make

Each line offers a genuine choice; the recommendation assumes the standing Bazaar, rounded-up AUD, confident-product and public-paid-alpha decisions remain binding ([decisions](../maturity/00-decisions.md#L5-L29)). "Deletes" describes the accepted direction, not a change made by this report.

| Question | Options | Mature-product precedent | Recommendation; what it deletes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Which front door buys the first Tool: Market search or chat? | Search first / chat first / equal doors | OpenRouter leads to one selectable API/model; chat products answer in the box, rather than sending a catalogue query through an unanswered second screen ([comparison](chat.md#L138-L148)). | **Search first**, chat optional after results prove its incremental value. Delete hero-to-`/t/new` as the default and duplicate Ask entry; keep the Bazaar index, compact cards and real recipient disclosure. |
| 2. Which money model and **single GST treatment** goes live? | Prepaid AUD purse / post-paid invoice / today's hybrid | OpenRouter prepays; AWS Marketplace separates buyer sale from supplier payment ([comparison](money.md#L138-L151)). | **Prepaid** with one pinned all-in AUD Quote, one corporate USDC settlement, and one versioned tax schedule used by funding, Call, ledger, receipt and export. Do not pick the taxable moment here. An adviser must reconcile fee-only GST on the top-up, inclusive-gross GST from the second splitter, and the whitepaper's GST on Calls. No live activation before that written Australian tax/legal/banking decision ([start line](../../../START_LINE.md#L148-L180)). Delete post-paid invoice logic, Connect payout lane, auto-recharge and conflicting gross-top-up GST projections; retain exact amounts and historic money-row readback. |
| 3. What can a Provider list at alpha? | Public x402 URL + payee proof only / all source types / no owner publishing | Bazaar indexes a paid, described x402 route; Smithery asks for a hosted URL ([comparison](supply.md#L112-L122)). | **URL + signed payee proof** for AE-owned listing, while **all Bazaar supply remains searchable**. Delete OpenAPI/MCP/plugin source choices from the alpha form, duplicate pricing choices and provider-facing canary steps; keep admission, identity, terms and authority checks. |
| 4. Is a custom admin console needed? | Six-page console / one restricted Provider control / internal functions and vendor dashboards | Stripe/Convex/Sentry already own payments, rows and errors ([operator review](admin-ops.md#L107-L155)). | **Internal functions plus one restricted Provider control only if needed for source verification/suppression.** Delete overview lamps, metrics snapshot, worklists and audit browser; keep admin entitlement, evidence, audit rows, treasury alerts and explicit recovery. |
| 5. How should core code shrink? | Prune in place / slim rebuild and migrate / keep expanding | A proven checkout, ledger and identity system is costly to re-prove; Joel's lean rules say preserve a working end-to-end slice before replacing it ([rules](../../../.claude/CLAUDE.md#L5-L15)). | **Prune in place** along the paid journey; allow a bounded replacement only where one component cannot be made smaller safely. Delete obsolete branches *after* proving their replacement; no speculative 10× rewrite or data-loss shortcut. |
| 6. What is the one frontend read path? | `convexQuery` + TanStack Query with loader prefetch and subscription / today's server-functions + `useQuery` split / all HTTP snapshots | The official Convex TanStack Start template shares one query definition across SSR and live updates ([verified review](frontend-code.md#L150-L179)). | **One query/cache** for Convex-backed reads, with server functions only for non-Convex joins or secret-only work. Delete redundant HTTP-backed Convex read wrappers and duplicate return shapes as each route migrates; retain signed-in authority and anonymous no-websocket behaviour where it matters. |
| 7. Which domain language is public? | Twelve nouns now in UI/API, storage later / rename storage too / keep synonyms | Product charter already calls the callable unit Tool and accepted use Call ([charter](../../../PRODUCT.md#L178-L191)). | **Account, Business, Provider, Agent, Key, Grant, Offering, Tool, Publication, Contract, Quote, Call** in new UI/API; selectively retire synonyms at touched callsites. Delete peer "purchase"/"operation" product labels, not protected `operation:v1:` IDs, OpenAPI `operationId`, idempotency `operationKey` or distinct stored Offering/Publication rows ([inventory](domain.md#L243-L282)). |
| 8. Are contracts/view-model files mandatory for every component? | Mandatory compound folders / only at real boundaries / collapse everything into routes | TanStack's sample routes preload; components render typed results without a per-card DTO ([comparison](frontend-code.md#L181-L189)). | **Only where isolation earns its cost.** Delete obligatory one-off `*.contracts.ts`, view-model and nested `components/` files when they merely copy generated query types; keep pure leaves, testable money projections and shared UI boundaries. |
| 9. Which protocols and gates survive? | All ratchets / safety-only short guide and checks / no gates | Shape Up fixes the bet and cuts scope; it does not require a census for every correction ([comparison](process.md#L142-L150)). | **One charter, one short decision page, import/security/Convex-reference fences and changed paid-path tests per change; full release gate before money-taking releases.** Delete historical shape/copy pins, duplicative registers and closed-campaign instructions, not protection against double payment or unauthorized reads ([protected fences](process.md#L112-L123)). |
| 10. Are MCP and CLI thin clients of HTTP? | HTTP as canonical JSON + thin adapters / in-process registry for all three / independent implementations | Stripe's CLI keeps local commands but sends resource operations through HTTP ([comparison](agent-surfaces.md#L10-L16)). | **One action schema/result behind HTTP, MCP and CLI**; CLI already calls HTTP for most resource verbs. Delete duplicate name/admission/envelope maps where parity is possible; retain local `connect`/`doctor`, safe `call` composition and MCP's protocol wrapper, with parity checked on buyer-visible results ([source](../../../tools/ae/commands/action-adapters.ts#L22-L107)). |

Ordered assuming those decisions. Sizes describe relative product work, **not** estimates of legal approval time. Do not delete paid evidence or change deployed money rows until replay and reconciliation remain possible.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Keep list

- **Bazaar as the searchable cold-start market**, not a side directory; compact indexed results, browse classification and describe on demand ([Joel's decision](../maturity/00-decisions.md#L21-L23), [indexed search](../../../convex/lib/market/directoryIndex/browse.ts#L188-L225)).
- **The business boundary:** AE is the buyer-facing Seller; buyer AUD credit and corporate USDC payment are distinct; the customer owns its agent and its larger task ([charter](../../../PRODUCT.md#L207-L239), [start line](../../../START_LINE.md#L11-L29)).
- **Money and authority guarantees:** exact versioned Quote, atomic reserve before signing, stable idempotent Call identity, no blind retry on uncertain settlement, real recovery and exact accounting record ([start line](../../../START_LINE.md#L53-L101), [Call identity](../../../src/modules/capability-execution/internal/convex-schema.ts#L477-L479)).
- **Provider proof and Australian obligations:** signed payee claim, seller/business identity, audit evidence, approved GST treatment and the explicit live-activation hold ([claim](../../../src/modules/capability-supply/internal/supply-funnel/connections.ts#L218-L245), [start line](../../../START_LINE.md#L148-L180)).
- **What already feels like a product:** discover's editorial rail, clear list-to-detail distinction, readable workbench typography, and compact UI primitives; keep its brand grammar while making the first action Search ([UI review](ui-composition.md#L9-L23)).
- **A public agent contract and honest claim boundaries:** typed selection then caller-supplied exact input, rounded-up AUD on screens with exact amounts in records, CLI provenance and paid-path proof before calling alpha open ([Joel's decisions](../maturity/00-decisions.md#L21-L29), [CLI release](../maturity/00-decisions.md#L33-L37)).
