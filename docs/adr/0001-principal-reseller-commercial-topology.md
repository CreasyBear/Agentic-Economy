# Agentic Economy is the buyer-facing seller

Agentic Economy will operate a two-sided market for discovery and allocation,
then act as principal reseller for supported production purchases. The Business
Principal buys the Operation from Agentic Economy as the fixed Seller. The
selected Provider performs upstream for Agentic Economy. Buyer consideration
and the Provider obligation are separate commercial facts even when one payment
rail connects them.

The credible alternatives were a neutral payment router and a disclosed agent
forming direct purchases between each buyer and Provider. Both preserve broad
Provider choice. Neither gives the buyer one counterparty, one remedy or one
closed purchase record when the Provider becomes known only during work. The
principal-reseller model accepts more tax, refund, fraud and performance
exposure in exchange for that common boundary.

This decision applies only where Agentic Economy holds enough control to admit
the Provider, state the buyer-facing terms, bind authority before Invocation,
inspect delivery evidence, suspend supply, issue adjustments and pursue
upstream recourse. A direct Provider payment or a record assembled after the
event must not be represented as the same product. Prepaid credit is the first
buyer funding model, but Funding remains separate from authority and from the
later Operation purchase. Australian GST, financial-services, privacy and
recordkeeping consequences must be determined from the final contracts and
operating facts before the model is described as compliant.
