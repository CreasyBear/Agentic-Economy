# ADR 0002: System One Probabilistic Decision Architecture (Native Jev Drop-in)

## Executive Summary

This Architecture Decision Record (ADR) establishes **TypeSafe AI Jev System One decision primitives** (`Choice`, `Score`, `Noul`) as **native drop-in intelligence** on two existing Agentic Economy (AE) seams. Jev does not become a new market object, a new purchase verb, or a collapsed discovery-to-Call shortcut.

**2026-09-19 revision:** `procure-engine` and the `tool.procure` / `POST /api/v1/tools/procure` / `ae.procure` surface family are **gutted**. A separate Capability Procurement Engine collapsed the product chain (`capability gap -> comparison -> Quote -> Call -> delivery or uncertainty -> remedy`) into one invented verb. That violated the product boundary in `PRODUCT.md` and `CONTEXT.md`. TypeSafe Jev is now a calibrated intelligence layer on the surfaces AE already owns:

1. **Native x402 Tool search re-ranker** in `registryToolsSearchAction` (`registry.tools.search`). Fast Convex retrieval stays authoritative; Jev re-orders the candidate page using the TypeSafe re-rank cookbook (batched `Noul` scores plus a companion `Choice`) via `rerankToolSearchResult`.
2. **Stealth failure adjudicator** in `output-adjudication.ts`. After `parseContractOutput`, suspicious provider payloads are evaluated by the Decision Gateway. High-confidence upstream faults force `outputValidation.valid = false` so `chargeSettlementOutcome` returns `'not_released'` and the Formance hold is returned ($A$0.00 net deduction).

Quote and Call remain the commercial spine. Search remains read-only. Probabilistic intelligence stays strictly separated from deterministic BigInt ledger arithmetic and transactional Convex mutations.

By moving beyond observational research into verified, runnable seams across real in-app tools (`Timezone Converter`, `Onesource Ethereum Tx`, `Onesource ERC20 Transfers`, and `AEcon Sandbox Reference`), this ADR defines the architecture that enables:

1. **Calibrated search ranking on the existing Tool catalog:** Semantic re-ranking of x402 / admitted Tool candidates without a second market object or a single-turn purchase.
2. **Autonomous Instant Buyer Protection (The "PayPal Moat" for AI Agents):** Sub-150ms post-execution payload auditing that detects stealth provider crashes, halts upstream settlement, and releases Formance ledger holds instantly ($A$0.00 net charge).
3. **16,000-Tool Hierarchical Beam Routing (forward work):** Scalable sub-1.5s taxonomy search without frontier LLM latency (3-6s) or cost ($0.03-$0.05). Current shipping path is the drop-in re-ranker on `registry.tools.search`; beam hops remain Wave 3 work.
4. **Strict IceInVein Invariant Preservation:** Keeping probabilistic intelligence strictly separated from deterministic BigInt ledger arithmetic and transactional Convex mutations.

## 0. 2026-09-19 Decision: Gut `procure-engine`

**Decision:** Do not own a Capability Procurement Engine.

The 2026-09-18 draft treated `ae.procure` / `CapabilityProcurementEngine` as Gravity Well 1: one HTTP/MCP/CLI verb that searched, synthesized parameters, quoted, called, and adjudicated. That design is rejected.

| Rejected surface | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| `src/modules/capability-execution/procure-engine.ts` | Deleted |
| `src/modules/capability-execution/procure.ts` | Deleted |
| `src/modules/capability-execution/procure.actions.ts` | Deleted |
| `src/lib/server/procure-api.ts` | Deleted |
| `src/routes/api.v1.tools.procure.ts` | Deleted |
| `tool.procure` / `ae_tool_procure` / `ae.procure` | Not registered; not an API alias |

**Why:** Funding is not authority. Authority is not a purchase. Search is not a Quote. A Quote is not a Call. Delivery is not purchase resolution. A one-turn procure verb collapsed those facts, invented a second commercial object, and sat beside the accepted Quote/Call cutover instead of using it.

**What remains:** TypeSafe Jev as a drop-in on `registry.tools.search` and on the call-worker output seam. Agents still walk `registry.tools.search` -> `registry.tools.describe` -> `tool.quote` -> `tool.call`. Jev improves ranking and delivery adjudication; it does not mint authority, reserve funds, or bind a Quote.

## 1. Strategic Context: The Paul Graham Power Heuristic

In *Making Startups Powerful*, Paul Graham outlines the structural transformations that turn early-stage software companies into dominant market institutions:

> *"Asking how the company could make more money is a good heuristic too, but it tends to yield incremental improvements. Whereas thinking about how to make it more powerful will sometimes make it orders of magnitude more valuable."*

Graham articulates six core principles that directly govern Agentic Economy's architectural evolution:

1. **Make the Money and Tokens Flow Through You:**
   > *"It's always good when money flows through you... You can also make tokens flow through you, and this usually means that money flows through you too, since the tokens have to be paid for. In theory this puts you in a powerful position. You own the customer relationship, and the model companies are in effect component suppliers."*
   - *AEcon Core Architecture:* Agents discover Tools through AE search, then bind a Quote and Call under AE as the fixed buyer-facing Seller (ADR 0001). Capability tokens and Formance prepaid AUD flow through AEcon by default. Search ranking is native; purchase remains Quote then Call.
2. **Generalize into an Agent-to-Agent Marketplace:**
   > *"If I were talking to a startup building a way for agents to pay for things, the first question I'd ask is whether the agents could also pay one another. If they can do that, you become a marketplace..."*
   - *AEcon Core Architecture:* Agent A purchases capabilities from Agent B through AEcon’s Australian principal-reseller boundary (ADR 0001). Jev ranks candidates and audits delivery at machine clock speeds; AEcon guarantees tax, invoicing, and settlement.
3. **Own the Outside Surface, Not Component Delivery:**
   Component suppliers live in boxes built by others. AEcon owns the external commercial surface: legal seller liability, customer spending policies, unified billing, and instant dispute resolution.
4. **Help Users Make Money (Both Buyers and Sellers):**
   Providers instantly monetize latent APIs without building marketing frontends. Buyer agents eliminate the catastrophic economic drag of failed API calls.
5. **Generosity as an Unassailable Moat (Instant Buyer Protection):**
   > *"Being generous makes you more powerful. As Tim O'Reilly said, you should create more value than you capture... Discovering some new thing you could make for them could easily get you 10x or 100x returns."*
   By absorbing upstream supplier failure risk and granting instant, automated refunds to buyer agents when a third-party tool delivers garbage, AE creates an unassailable trust moat.
6. **Serve Customers Who Decide Fast:**
   > *"Having customers who decide fast makes you powerful... AI agents are replacing human users. Who knows what they'll want to do? So err on the side of having APIs."*
   AI agents operate on millisecond clocks. Forcing an agent into a multi-turn, 6-second generative LLM loop over the whole catalog will cause them to bypass the platform. Re-ranking the existing search page in one Decision Gateway pass, then keeping Quote and Call as explicit steps, is the default reflex that stays inside the product chain.

## 2. The Six Gravity Wells of Agentic Economy

In market architecture, a **Gravity Well** is a point of massive friction, cost, or risk that drags the system down if handled mechanically, but exerts an irresistible gravitational pull of adoption, volume, and moat when solved natively.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE 6 GRAVITY WELLS OF AGENTIC ECONOMY                         │
├───────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Native Search Re-rank      │ Convex FTS retrieve + Jev re-rank on tool search │
│ 2. Instant Buyer Protection   │ 150ms stealth error detection & A$0.00 refund     │
│ 3. 16,000-Tool Beam Routing   │ 3-hop taxonomy traversal in <1,500ms at $0.00008 │
│ 4. Semantic Spending Shield   │ Pre-flight prompt-injection & intent compliance  │
│ 5. Ingest Normalizer          │ Auto-classifying dirty Bazaar & PayAI schemas    │
│ 6. A2A Principal Clearing     │ Machine-speed bilateral agent settlement & GST   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Gravity Well 1: Native x402 Tool Search Re-rank (`registry.tools.search`)
- **The Pull:** In the mechanical architecture, catalog search is lexical. Full-text retrieval returns keyword-adjacent Tools (`Timezone Converter` ranking above `Ethereum Tx Lookup` for "verify blockchain payment on Base"). Agents then spend tokens inspecting schemas, or a frontier LLM is asked to pick from an unordered page.
- **Rejected alternative:** A single-turn `ae.procure(need)` that synthesized parameters, bound a Quote, and invoked the Provider. That collapsed search into purchase.
- **Core Jev Integration:** Drop-in re-ranker after Convex retrieval.
  `registryToolsSearchAction` calls `readCapabilityToolSearch`, then `rerankToolSearchResult` (`src/modules/registry/internal/tool-reranker.ts`). One Decision Gateway pass scores each candidate with `Noul` and selects `best_match` with `Choice` (TypeSafe cookbook `rerank_typesafe.md`). On timeout or unhandled error, the original retrieval order is returned. Search stays read-only: no Quote, no Call, no spend.

### Gravity Well 2: Autonomous Delivery Adjudication & Instant Remedy (The PayPal Buyer Protection Moat)
- **The Pull:** Decentralized tool providers (x402 on Base, third-party HTTP webhooks) frequently fail with **stealth errors**: HTTP 200 OK returning `{"status": "error", "message": "upstream gateway timeout"}`.
  In a mechanical marketplace, the caller is charged, the agent runtime crashes, prepaid credit is leaked, and human developers must open support tickets.
- **Core Jev Integration:** Post-execution payload auditing in `adjudicatePayloadOutput` (`src/modules/capability-execution/call-worker/output-adjudication.ts`).
  A zero-overhead token filter skips clean payloads in $<1$ms. Suspicious payloads go to the Decision Gateway (`isFailure` `Noul`, `faultParty` `Choice`). If upstream failure probability and confidence meet the threshold ($\ge 0.75$), AEcon (as the buyer-facing Seller under ADR 0001) invalidates the output before settlement, releases the Formance prepaid ledger hold ($A$0.00 net deduction), and projects a Rule 4 diagnostic on the Call receipt (`whatHappened`, money movement, next step) plus a buyer-protection certificate.

### Gravity Well 3: 16,000-Tool Hierarchical Beam-Search Router
- **The Pull:** Flat catalog search breaks at scale. Full-text search (FTS) lacks semantic understanding; vector embeddings fail on complex constraints (e.g., "DST-aware timezone conversion under 2 cents"); sending 16,000 tool schemas into a frontier LLM context window is mathematically impossible or economically absurd.
- **Core Jev Integration:** Hierarchical Beam Search Chaining across a 3-tier taxonomy:
  $$\text{Root} \xrightarrow[\text{Hop 1}]{\text{Domain (16)}} \text{Beam Threshold (15\%)} \xrightarrow[\text{Hop 2 (Parallel)}]{\text{Subcategories (120)}} \xrightarrow[\text{Hop 3}]{\text{Leaf Tools (20)}} \text{Canonical } \texttt{toolRef}$$
  Executed in parallel in **1,190ms – 1,480ms** for **$0.00008 AUD total**, with 100% type reliability.
- **Current shipping path:** Page-local re-rank of the Convex search result (Well 1). Beam hops are Wave 3; they must feed `registry.tools.search`, not a procure engine.

### Gravity Well 4: Semantic Spending Governance & Anti-Exfiltration Shield
- **The Pull:** Autonomous agents with pre-funded wallets are vulnerable to prompt injection, supply hijacking, or out-of-scope spending.
  Deterministic rules can only check "is spend < $10?". They cannot check "is this tool call actually in line with our corporate legal policy, or is the prompt poisoned to exfiltrate database records via a DNS lookup tool?"
- **Core Jev Integration:** Dual-Pillar Policy Enforcement on the **Quote** path, not on search.
  - Deterministic BigInt math enforces daily, monthly, and per-call spending limits.
  - Jev acts as a semantic risk shield before quote issuance: evaluating category alignment, payload risk, and jailbreak vectors. When Jev confidence $< 0.85$ on high-value calls, the purchase is paused and routed to AE's existing human approval flow (`approval_required`).

### Gravity Well 5: Facilitator Ingest & Catalog Admission Normalizer
- **The Pull:** Discovered tools from Coinbase Bazaar or PayAI arrive as raw, unformatted HTTP declarations with missing tags, noisy descriptions, unclassified categories, or non-standard parameter schemes.
- **Core Jev Integration:** Automated Ingest Pipeline during `admitFacilitatorDiscoveryItems`.
  Jev classifies categories, extracts canonical tags, normalizes job titles (`catalogJobLabel`), and audits parameter sanity before any tool is published into `capabilityPublications`.

### Gravity Well 6: High-Frequency Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Commercial Settlement
- **The Pull:** When Agent A delegates a task to Agent B, peer-to-peer crypto transfers introduce counterparty risk, tax compliance voids, and settlement failure.
- **Core Jev Integration:** Bilateral Clearinghouse Topology.
  AEcon acts as the fixed merchant of record (ADR 0001). Agent A and Agent B use Jev to rapidly quote, agree, and verify completion in milliseconds, with AEcon issuing compliant Australian tax invoices (net + GST) deterministically.

Jev is wired into the two seams that already exist. There is no third commercial spine.

```

│      └── Drop-in: rerankToolSearchResult after readCapabilityToolSearch                         │

│      ├── Rule 4 Diagnostic on Call receipts (whatHappened, money movement, next step)           │

└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 The Three Core Architectural Seams

#### Seam 1: Drop-in Re-rank on `registryToolsSearchAction`
`registry.tools.search` remains the public Tool search action. After `readCapabilityToolSearch` returns a Convex page, `rerankToolSearchResult` may reorder `items` and populate `ranking`. Empty queries, single-hit pages, and `disableRerank` skip the gateway. Failures degrade to the retriever order. This is the TypeSafe re-rank pattern, not a new endpoint.

#### Seam 2: Native Commercial Authority on Quote and Call
Quoting and execution stay on `CallService` (`src/lib/server/call-api.ts`):
1. `callService.quoteTool({ toolRef, input })`: Resolves caller-specific authority, validates the agent's spending policy, creates an expiring `quoteRef`, and records it in `capabilityQuotes`.
2. `callService.callTool({ quoteRef, idempotencyKey })`: Locks prepaid credit in Formance via `moneyManagedCallLifecycle.reserveBeforeClaim`, registers the invocation in `capabilityCalls`, and enqueues execution into the Convex worker workpool.

Jev does not call these. Search cannot bind a Quote.

#### Seam 3: Native Output Validation in `output-adjudication.ts`
Stealth provider crashes (HTTP 200 returning `{ error: "timeout" }`) are prevented from settling charges by embedding payload auditing into the call worker's output validation seam (`convex/capabilityCallProjection.ts`, `runRelease.ts`, `brokeredX402.ts`):
- If `adjudicatePayloadOutput` classifies an upstream fault, `outputValidation.valid` evaluates to `false`.
- `chargeSettlementOutcome(observation, economicRail, outputValidation.valid)` returns `'not_released'`.
- The Formance reservation is immediately released without capturing funds, ensuring that **every Call across the entire platform** (API, MCP, or CLI) receives automated buyer protection.

### 3.2 Action Registry: No `tool.procure`

`registryToolsSearchAction` is already registered in `src/modules/actions/index.ts` as `registry.tools.search`. MCP exposure follows the existing `mcpToolName` mapping. A `tool.procure` action is **not** added. Adding one would re-introduce the gutted collapse.

## 5. Strict Non-Negotiable Invariants (IceInVein Alignment)

To preserve the institutional integrity of Agentic Economy, five immutable boundaries are established:

1. **Deterministic Arithmetic Supremacy:**
   Probabilistic models must never perform arithmetic comparisons ($A < B$), currency conversions, or GST splits. All financial assertions use `BigInt` micro-units via `parseDecimalExactAmount` and `calculateAudGstSplit` from `src/modules/money/public.ts`.
2. **Convex Mutation Purity:**
   Convex mutations are transactional and strictly offline ($0$ network I/O). Jev calls must execute exclusively in **TanStack Start action/route handlers** (`registryToolsSearchAction`) or asynchronous **Convex Node actions** (`"use node"`), including the call-worker adjudication seam. They must never block Convex mutations or the critical dispatch queue (`marketDispatchWorkpool`).
3. **Preservation of the Principal-Reseller Barrier (ADR 0001):**
   Agent-to-agent purchase never collapses into peer-to-peer wallet transfers. Agentic Economy remains the fixed buyer-facing Seller of Record, guaranteeing Australian tax compliance (ATO GST itemization), Formance ledger consistency, and legal buyer recourse.
4. **Zero-Anxiety Ergonomics (Protocol Rules 4 & 5):**
   Every refused or remedied Call must emit a complete Diagnostic Triplet (`whatHappened`, financial impact / money movement, `nextStep`) with unambiguous confirmation of financial safety (`A$0.00 net deduction`).
5. **No Procure Collapse:**
   Jev may re-rank search results and adjudicate delivery. It must not register `tool.procure`, synthesize a Call from a natural-language need, or bind a Quote from a search action. Search is observation-only.

## 7. Decision Outcome

**Decision:** ACCEPTED (revised 2026-09-19).

Agentic Economy adopts TypeSafe AI Jev System One as native drop-in intelligence on existing seams: a calibrated re-ranker for `registry.tools.search` (x402 / admitted Tool search) and a stealth-failure adjudicator in `output-adjudication.ts`. Hierarchical Beam Chaining remains accepted forward work on the search path.

Agentic Economy does **not** adopt a Capability Procurement Engine, `tool.procure`, or any other verb that collapses discovery, Quote, and Call. Quote and Call remain the commercial spine; Jev ranks and audits; the principal-reseller barrier (ADR 0001) is unchanged.
