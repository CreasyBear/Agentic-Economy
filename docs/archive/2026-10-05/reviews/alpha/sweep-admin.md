## Not reachable and why

The current local data has no pending approvals, no proposal audit history, and no admission or dispute cases. Approval, expiry, renewal, and those case-detail actions cannot be proved live without creating authority-changing records; no artificial fixture or command was introduced. Expired approvals intentionally leave actionable lists and remain in audit per `docs/reviews/maturity/00-decisions.md:20`; the UI hook filters them by `expiresAt > now` at `src/components/ae/admin/audit/useAdminAudit.ts:39-75`. Guarded admin writes are further blocked by current `Clerk MFA claim: Absent`: local Clerk `convex` JWT template must include `{{session.factor_verification_age}}` plus an actually fresh verified factor. This is a config prerequisite, not permission to bypass MFA; `convex/lib/adminConsole/commands/commandEnvelope.ts:144-146` refuses absent proof. Break-glass execution deliberately excluded (view only). Exhausting the sparse legacy-unset exception index was not reached after four empty pages; no actual legacy case was opened.
