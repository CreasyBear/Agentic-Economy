Primary review checked the shortlisted APIs and corrected two worker assumptions: custom-content HeroSection has natural height, and the installed comparison matrix supports one column.

## Existing adoption and rejected shortcuts

Source confirms actual Arc hierarchy in settings navigation, API-key rows, team roster, receipt invoice history, the credit funding panel, core chat/composer/run regions, market listing/detail/comparison, provider connections and Add Tool steps. MetricsDashboard is already a block port. These findings do not establish visual acceptance or completion of the broad refactor.

Credit history is a material partial-adoption example: `BillingHistory` supplies a card/header, while `AeAccountHistory` still owns the records. It is not counted as a complete ledger replacement. Shared `AePageHeader` remains a separate adaptation alongside the installed `PageHeader`; consolidating those ports is a moderate opportunity that must preserve focus and metadata.

Avoid commercial or authority mismatch: usage forecasts/subscription retention do not describe prepaid credit; a permission matrix does not assign people to roles; notification-center is an inbox rather than preference settings; a trading terminal is not the Tool market. Demo state alone is not disqualifying, but a presentation adapter must not invent commercial facts or replace Clerk, payment, key or recovery authority.

Source-only cognitive review preserves visible decision aids: Agents has one setup CTA, three FAQ questions, a three-step connection flow, copyable commands and two advanced disclosures. Keep those facts externalized. Chat follow-ups stay at three choices, record headers group title/status/reference, and receipt stages avoid a second collapsed disclosure. Touch size and visual salience were not measured.
