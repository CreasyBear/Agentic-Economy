# Refero Styles — supplementary AE reference

Checked 2026-09-24. [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/) is a searchable gallery of AI-readable, **website-derived** style analyses, not an official product design-system registry or a library of actual app screens. Each entry exposes a preview, colour roles, type scale, spacing, radii, components and an extended/compact **downloadable or copyable DESIGN.md**; the detail page also offers Tailwind v4, CSS variables and design tokens. [Refero's guide](https://styles.refero.design/ai-agents/design-md-examples) says to copy the system rather than the website. Its own disclaimer says source measurements are normalised, roles/recommendations interpreted and HTML examples reconstructed. Treat chat UI claims as inspiration, not proof of a live product's implementation. The [Perplexity search-interface entry](https://styles.refero.design/style/e9fff87a-63ce-4c19-840f-98233db62f58) is more applicable to chat than the [Perplexity Computer botanical marketing entry](https://styles.refero.design/style/7c4e9339-591c-46c3-ac3d-20c1b5b5a568); [Airbnb listings](https://styles.refero.design/style/c2325884-4391-4688-85cd-e143f5107517) are more applicable to market than [Airbnb.org](https://styles.refero.design/style/194faa2f-2f69-4bbf-9e29-290f28fa8ca2).

## Adoptable rules, adapted to AE rather than copied

1. **Make the chat text the main surface.** [Perplexity search](https://styles.refero.design/style/e9fff87a-63ce-4c19-840f-98233db62f58) centers a ~640px interaction column; [OpenAI](https://styles.refero.design/style/dc541737-8bf2-4b31-b729-0352f696e82f) uses an unboxed editorial canvas. For AE, keep a contained reading column, flat unboxed answers and **16px / 1.6** Manrope answer text per `perplexity-DESIGN.md`, not its marketing type or colours.
2. **Give the composer sole visual prominence.** The [Perplexity search input](https://styles.refero.design/style/e9fff87a-63ce-4c19-840f-98233db62f58) is centered, ~640px wide, 16px internal padding, one 1px hairline and one dark circular submit control. In AE use existing `--ae-surface`, `--ae-border`, `--radius-card` (**14px**) and charcoal submit; do not copy its 12px radius, teal glow or a second filled action.
3. **Keep starters light and scannable.** [OpenAI's prompt suggestions](https://styles.refero.design/style/dc541737-8bf2-4b31-b729-0352f696e82f) are simple outlined chips with **8px** gaps under the centered input; [Perplexity](https://styles.refero.design/style/81afaa5c-73ac-4ef4-9a99-296da325ea6c) uses compact 8px gaps and 16px card padding. AE's existing empty-state starters can use `rounded-full` and 8–12px gaps; no bordered card around the starter group.
4. **Use type/space before containers.** [OpenAI](https://styles.refero.design/style/dc541737-8bf2-4b31-b729-0352f696e82f) and [Vercel](https://styles.refero.design/style/f24daf3a-d43f-4dec-85a9-8ac1d5148a03) favour 1px hairlines, 12–16px element gaps and little/no shadow. In AE separate answer sections with 24–32px whitespace, then a 1px `--ae-border` divider only where needed; never box every answer paragraph/tool step. Do not introduce their black-at-12%-opacity token as a parallel AE border system.
5. **Preserve the warm neutral surface ladder.** [Claude](https://styles.refero.design/style/47cb86b6-cb2d-41c8-94ba-8607cd7c41cd) layers parchment, paper and stone with low elevation; [Perplexity](https://styles.refero.design/style/e9fff87a-63ce-4c19-840f-98233db62f58) uses a warm canvas and just one raised input. AE already has chalk `--ae-bg`, sunken `--ae-surface-sunken` and raised `--ae-surface`: use those three, not Claude clay, Perplexity teal or a new white palette.
6. **Keep one dark action and one functional accent.** [Claude](https://styles.refero.design/style/47cb86b6-cb2d-41c8-94ba-8607cd7c41cd) has a dark neutral CTA; [Vercel](https://styles.refero.design/style/f24daf3a-d43f-4dec-85a9-8ac1d5148a03) uses neutral filled actions against hairline secondary controls. In AE charcoal `--ae-fg` fills the one primary action; forest `--ae-brand` is for links, focus and selection only. No coral, lime, teal or gold UI accent.
7. **Let market listings carry information, not chrome.** [Airbnb's listing entry](https://styles.refero.design/style/c2325884-4391-4688-85cd-e143f5107517) uses full-bleed imagery, 12–14px corners, a **12px** image-to-text gutter, 14px/500 titles, quieter 14px metadata and explicit price. AE's existing market is frozen and uses `--radius-card` **14px** plus hairlines/optional `--shadow-float` hover; borrow only hierarchy for other listing/detail surfaces, showing Provider, evidence and price truthfully. No new card treatment on chat answers.
8. **Reserve elevation for actual raised objects.** [Linear](https://styles.refero.design/style/90ce5883-bb24-4466-93f7-801cd617b0d1) and [Raycast](https://styles.refero.design/style/3b6a17f0-3bdf-418c-a95e-0b89e5a8b2f8) use hairlines and modest surface steps instead of ambient card shadows. AE can use its existing `--shadow-float` only for listing hover and `--shadow-overlay` for popovers; answers and rows stay flat. Refero's static spec does **not** establish a usable motion duration: keep AE's existing **150/220/300ms** interaction ladder and reduced-motion policy rather than inventing Refero animation.

**Precedence:** AE root `DESIGN.md` and `src/styles/globals.css` are binding; `docs/design/reference/perplexity-DESIGN.md` governs chat composition. These Refero examples are supplementary, never a token replacement or a reason to change the frozen Market discover files.
