## Decision and AE behavior

The support page and owner-credit panels use the pristine Arc tabs API. Support retains vertical tab roles, four named topics, Arrow/Home/End focus-and-selection, and the existing active panel content/actions.

Market navigation is a documented moved-out KEEP: its tabs are real TanStack `<Link>` anchors with route search state, prefetch and native modifier-key behavior. Arc's `TabsTrigger` wraps children in its own selection and label spans and exposes no `render`/`nativeButton` slot; `liquid-tab-bar` and `segmented-control` likewise do not provide route-link triggers plus peer panels. The prior route-link-capable implementation now lives in `src/components/ae/shared/router-tabs/`, outside `src/components/arc/`; only the Market route uses it. The market empty state now imports the shared Arc edge component.
