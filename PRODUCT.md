## How to read this document

Three layers, kept distinct on purpose:

- **Corporate north star** sets the trajectory — where we are going.
- **Brand / campaign** sets the voice and the fight — how we say it.
- **Product-surface truth** sets what ships today and what public copy may claim.

When the layers seem to disagree, the product-surface truth wins for anything a
person or an assistant can read. Ambition is trajectory and conviction, never a
claim of a capability we have not shipped.

## Layer 1 — Corporate North Star

We will build the domestic agentic economy. We will facilitate agentic decision
making. The flag goes on the transact rung: the distance between a household
asking and the thing being done, collapsing toward nothing.

This is trajectory, not a shipped claim. The register is future-tense and
first-person plural — direction stated with conviction — and it never launders
itself into a feature we do not have. Read it as the bearing for every roadmap
decision, not as a description of today's surface.

## Layer 2 — Brand / Campaign

AE is an action brand. Receipts, written threads, and status vocabulary are
plumbing — the brakes that let us move fast — never the pitch.

**The enemy is the halfway.** AI search hands you "the best businesses in your
area," then leaves you the real work: deciding, contacting, chasing a reply,
agreeing on price. The list is where the assistant stops and where AE starts.
The agentic economy stays a broken promise until answers become action.

**The hero is the household's "sorted" moment.** The exhale when the thing is
handled — in writing, without gambling on strangers. Business owners get the
mirror-image pitch (work arrives in writing, no lead fees, own your page); that
is sales collateral, not the brand's soul.

**Master promise:** Ask once. It gets sorted.

**Voice:** visionary swagger — future-tense conviction, concrete nouns, kinetic
verbs, challenger jabs at the halfway. On product surfaces the conviction stays
and the nouns get exact; the swagger of a record is momentum, not adjectives. The
ten-rule voice contract in the manifesto is binding for all copy, and it replaces
any older "warm, exact" personality note.

Brand authorities are canonical; this document points to them rather than
restating them:

- `.planning/brand/MANIFESTO-MESSAGING-2026-07-04.md` — manifesto, story, pillars, master promise, taglines, and the ten-rule voice contract.

## Layer 3 — Product-Surface Truth

What ships today, stated as plain fact:

1. A customer or assistant asks for a service in plain language.
2. AE shows comparable business pages that people and assistants can read.
3. The customer sends one qualified inquiry.
4. That inquiry becomes a written record, delivered to the business.
5. The owner reviews it — accept, reject, correct, or claim the page.

The surface should read like the start of a marketplace: real inventory,
comparable choices, clear next steps. Not instant booking — beautiful pages and
one owned action.

**Copy constraint (fact, not apology).** AE ships qualified inquiries, written
records, and owner review. It does not book, charge, or dispatch, so public copy
never says it does — and never invents a capability to make a surface feel more
finished. State the boundary once, where the contract needs it (the inquiry
receipt, the send action, terms), as a clean fact about what happens next: the
business confirms timing, quote, and availability. That is a fact, not a
defensive drumbeat — one clean line, never a repeated disclaimer as personality.

## Seed Market

Start with local services in one Australian metro because the need is concrete,
the constraints are real, and wrong details have obvious cost. This is where we
plant first, not the ceiling: the data model stays wedge-agnostic and the
ambition is the whole domestic economy, one household ask at a time. AE is not a
trades directory. The larger wedge is agent-readable commerce that people and
assistants can act on.

## Users

- Customers looking for a real provider, a clear service area, and a safe next step.
- Business owners who want their service details represented accurately.
- Assistants that need structured facts, provenance, and refusal boundaries.
- Internal reviewers who resolve stale, contradicted, disputed, or unsupported details.

## First Owned Loop

The compounding loop:

1. A customer or assistant searches for a service.
2. AE shows comparable provider listings.
3. The listing explains what is published, what is supported, and what needs confirmation.
4. The customer takes one owned conversion action: a qualified inquiry request.
5. The business accepts, rejects, corrects, or claims the listing.
6. The listing gains fresher evidence and clearer boundaries.
7. Search and assistant discovery can rank better-supported listings higher.

If qualified inquiry is not live for a listing, the product says so plainly and
uses `View details` or external contact as the next step. It does not imply
booking, payment, dispatch, or autonomous execution.

## Status Vocabulary (machine surfaces)

This section is an engineering mechanic, not a brand pitch. Every status a public
page or assistant reads names its source, its freshness, and its boundary.
Public status maps to plain labels:

- `listed`: a public page exists.
- `business_supplied`: details were supplied or confirmed by the business.
- `publicly_observed`: details came from public business information.
- `checked`: a defined check passed.
- `contradicted`: sources disagree.
- `stale`: details need a refresh.
- `disputed`: someone challenged the details.
- `unsupported`: not enough evidence to present the claim confidently.

Never call a listing verified unless a specific verification standard exists and
the listing meets it.

## Product Principles

These are surface mechanics that keep the product honest and comparable:

1. Lead with real inventory. Search, cards, and provider pages carry the product.
2. Make listings comparable. The user should quickly understand provider, offer, area, source, limitation, and next action.
3. Show boundaries before action. Booking, payment, dispatch, and automation are never implied.
4. Give assistants a safe contract. They can read, compare, summarize, and route only within explicit limits.
5. Make correction easy. Provider claim and correction are part of the loop, not an admin afterthought.
6. Use proof as explanation. A badge is not enough. Every status cue names its source, its date, and its consequence.

## Anti-References

Avoid protocol theater, fake marketplace liquidity, fake reviews, fake availability, fake badges, fake dashboard previews, AI-purple gradients, generic SaaS cards, and internal vocabulary in public copy.

Do not use public-facing terms such as `source-owned`, `readback`, `manifest`, `capability`, `gateway`, `operator`, `MCP`, `OpenAPI`, `callable`, `autonomous`, or `verified` unless the product can defend the claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public, owner, and admin surfaces. Status must never rely on color alone. Focus states stay visible, touch targets meet at least 44px where practical, form labels remain persistent, and reduced-motion users get static or instant alternatives for nonessential animation.
