# Product

## Register

product

## Product Thesis

Agentic Economy is the trust and discovery layer for agentic commerce.

The first product should feel like the beginning of a marketplace, but it must not pretend to be one before liquidity, booking, payment, dispatch, reviews, and dispute handling exist. The useful model from Airbnb is not "instant booking." It is beautiful inventory, comparable choices, provider pride, trust choreography, and clear next steps.

Today, Agentic Economy publishes business-supplied service pages that customers can compare and assistants can safely read. The product shows what is known, what still needs confirmation, and what action is actually available.

## Seed Market

Start with local services because the need is concrete, trust matters, constraints are real, and wrong details have obvious cost.

Urgent help can be a seed use case, but it is not the whole wedge. The larger wedge is agent-readable commerce details that people and assistants can trust before taking the next step.

## Users

- Customers looking for a real provider, a clear service area, and a safe next step.
- Business owners who want their service details represented accurately.
- Assistants that need structured facts, provenance, and refusal boundaries.
- Internal reviewers who need to resolve stale, contradicted, disputed, or unsupported details.

## First Owned Loop

The first compounding loop should be:

1. A customer or assistant searches for a service.
2. Agentic Economy shows comparable provider listings.
3. The listing explains what is published, what is supported, and what needs confirmation.
4. The customer takes one owned conversion action: a qualified inquiry request.
5. The business accepts, rejects, corrects, or claims the listing.
6. The listing gains fresher evidence and clearer boundaries.
7. Search and assistant discovery can rank better-supported listings higher.

If qualified inquiry is not live, the product must say so plainly and use `View details` or external contact as the next step. Do not imply booking, payment, dispatch, or autonomous execution.

## Trust Contract

Trust must name source, freshness, and boundary.

Public trust states should map to plain labels:

- `listed`: a public page exists.
- `business_supplied`: details were supplied or confirmed by the business.
- `publicly_observed`: details came from public business information.
- `checked`: a defined check passed.
- `contradicted`: sources disagree.
- `stale`: details need a refresh.
- `disputed`: someone challenged the details.
- `unsupported`: not enough evidence to present the claim confidently.

Never call a listing verified unless a specific verification standard exists and the listing meets it.

## Brand Personality

Warm, exact, useful, and commercially credible.

Agentic Economy should feel like a marketplace people want to browse and providers want to be listed in. It should also be stricter than a marketplace: every attractive surface must expose its limits.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with real inventory. Search, cards, and provider pages carry the product.
2. Make listings comparable. The user should quickly understand provider, offer, area, trust source, limitation, and next action.
3. Show boundaries before action. Booking, payment, dispatch, and automation are never implied.
4. Give assistants a safe contract. They can read, compare, summarize, and route only within explicit limits.
5. Make correction easy. Provider claim and correction are part of the marketplace loop, not an admin afterthought.
6. Use proof as explanation. Badges are not enough. Every trust cue needs source, date, and consequence.

## Anti-References

Avoid protocol theater, fake marketplace liquidity, fake reviews, fake availability, fake badges, fake dashboard previews, AI-purple gradients, generic SaaS cards, and internal vocabulary in public copy.

Do not use public-facing terms such as `source-owned`, `readback`, `manifest`, `capability`, `gateway`, `operator`, `MCP`, `OpenAPI`, `callable`, `autonomous`, or `verified` unless the product can defend the claim.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for public, owner, and admin surfaces. Status must never rely on color alone. Focus states stay visible, touch targets meet at least 44px where practical, form labels remain persistent, and reduced-motion users get static or instant alternatives for nonessential animation.
