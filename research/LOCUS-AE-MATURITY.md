# Locus operating-standard baseline for Agentic Economy

**Date:** 2026-08-31; AE implementation reassessed 2026-09-03
**Question:** What must Agentic Economy prove to reach Locus operating parity on every overlapping Operation-market surface?  
**Boundary:** Locus is the operating standard, not a differentiation foil. Wallet custody, PaaS, team administration, general merchant checkout, billing SaaS, and orchestration remain outside parity only because [`PRODUCT.md`](../PRODUCT.md) explicitly assigns them elsewhere or excludes them.

## Bottom line

Agentic Economy has much of the right transaction machinery in source, but it is **not yet at Locus operating parity**.

Across the complete **175/175-page** first-party Locus corpus and its **50 decision-atomic candidates**, the completed parity registers give this rollup:

| Evidence slice | `MEETS` | `PARTIAL` | `MISSING` | `LIVE_PROOF_MISSING` | `OUTSIDE_CURRENT_CHARTER` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Current Locus Pro execution | 13 | 7 | 0 | 2 | 0 |
| Current financial controls | 4 | 8 | 0 | 1 | 1 |
| Legacy/core mechanisms | 6 | 5 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| **Overall** | **23** | **20** | **1** | **4** | **2** |

The 2026-08-31 Packages 1A–1C re-audit added operator-surface recovery proof. The 2026-09-03 connection re-audit then moved `LOCUS-009`, `LOCUS-015`, and `LOCUS-016` to `MEETS`. The 2026-09-03 funding re-audit completes the source contract behind `LOCUS-010` and `LOCUS-027`: AE now has an exact, idempotent, payer-safe Stripe-hosted handoff with public-safe receipt, durable Session-ID polling, signed-webhook/Formance fulfilment, and explicit post-funding continuation. A subsequent explicit compatibility closeout removed embedded Elements, its browser SDKs, and the publishable-key requirement; hosted Checkout is now the only accepted funding session. Both items remain `PARTIAL` until the approved Stripe sandbox and actual-client restart/webhook evidence is attached. These deltas do not supply the missing outbound-event, streaming, audit-coverage, or live-canary evidence behind the remaining `PARTIAL` and `LIVE_PROOF_MISSING` rows.

The one missing overlapping mechanism is a Tempo/MPP adapter with canonical AE receipt and reconciliation semantics; it is P2 work activated by admitted demand and a qualified rail partner, not a new product layer. The two genuine whole-candidate exclusions are generalized auto-recharge and generalized PaaS/wallet/checkout ownership. Broader candidates do not erase their overlapping standards: bounded human/composite supply, self-describing entry, outcome evidence, payment proof, and recovery remain parity work. In the Pro register, internal provider fan-out stays supplier-owned and an agent does not become an unowned legal/economic principal, but free eligibility/provenance/allocation and owner-bound headless technical onboarding/recovery are explicit parity targets.

The most important parity gaps are concrete:

1. **Inspection is not yet an executable commitment.** AE calculates an expiring maximum cost and fails closed on internal drift, but invoke does not carry the exact inspected plan, arguments, price commitment, and expiry the caller approved. Current Locus Pro makes fixed-price estimates executable approvals; legacy Locus also supports an exact-request expected-cost assertion. [Connect an agent](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/locus-pro/connect-agents) · [Legacy agent tools](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/wrapped-apis/for-agents)
2. **Discovery is not yet fully caller-viable.** AE exposes canonical readiness and can filter it, but default public search does not project whether this principal can execute now given balance, grant scope, connection, and authority. Locus Pro applies live account/credential policy throughout discovery, estimate, and execution. [Search enabled executable tools](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/api-reference/catalog/search-enabled-executable-tools)
3. **Execution authority has a mature connection path but incomplete aggregate governance.** AE now supports exact selected-Operation access, native OAuth, rotating refresh families, silent renewal, and immediate family revocation. It still lacks a general owner-selected overlap window for arbitrary non-OAuth harness keys, one principal-wide monthly committed-spend ceiling across every credential/route, and fresh-human proof across all high-risk mutations. [Create an Agent Connection](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/api-reference/authentication/create-a-scoped-agent-connection) · [MCP token exchange](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/api-reference/mcp/exchange-or-refresh-an-mcp-oauth-token)
4. **Several operating edges remain partial.** The payer-safe funding edge is now closed in source and tests. Remaining gaps are explicit supplier maturity/payable transitions, universal fail-closed audit coverage for authority/economic mutations, fresh human proof for high-risk changes, explicit streamed-delivery evidence, and outbound event retention/replay for asynchronous paid Operations comparable to Locus's durable webhook operations. [Spend controls](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/api-reference/tenants/read-workspace-spend-controls) · [Margin earnings](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/api-reference/ledger/margin-earnings) · [Durable webhook events](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/api-reference/webhooks/list-durable-webhook-events)
5. **Source parity is not operating parity.** AE has no checked-in live proof here of the complete paid path, a real supplier payout, recovery from ambiguous release, or the market behavior its own charter requires: unfamiliar gap, credible comparison, useful continuation, repeat demand or switching, supplier response, and recurrence across harnesses.

The response is an ordered parity program, not a wider product:

1. **Seal decision continuity:** make inspect produce a caller-bound, expiring execution commitment over exact Operation revision, exact arguments, total price or hard maximum, effects, authority, and readiness; require it at invoke and reject any drift before dispatch.
2. **Make discovery executable for this caller:** return canonical market truth and caller-specific viability together; record eligible candidates/provenance/allocation facts; add exact Operation/capability scopes and default-deny execution without hiding the setup action that would make valid supply usable; co-locate a machine-readable descriptor and next action on the exact Operation page.
3. **Complete the remaining credential governance:** retain the owner-bound OAuth lifecycle now implemented; add only the still-missing arbitrary-key overlap/cutover, proof-bound or shorter-lived high-risk execution identity, and fresh human authorization for payout, funding, and authority enlargement.
4. **Close operating recovery and financial-control edges:** retain the payer-safe funding create/status contract now implemented; add a principal-wide committed-spend ceiling, supplier accrued→matured→payable transitions, streamed acceptance/delivery/billing evidence when a canonical Operation streams, complete fail-closed audit coverage, fresh human proof for high-risk mutations, and signed outbound delivery/replay for asynchronous paid Operations.
5. **Pass a live economic canary:** for one exact revision, prove original paid call, identical replay, changed-material collision, provider failure, ambiguous dispatch/reconciliation, refund finality, receipt recovery after process loss, supplier accrual, and real payout.
6. **Pass the market acceptance test:** in one narrow category, observe an unfamiliar gap, multiple independent routeable Operations, an immediately useful result, a later comparable use or supplier switch through AE, incremental supplier demand, and recurrence from more than one harness.
7. **Close demand-activated rail parity:** when an admitted Operation demonstrates MPP-only demand, add a qualified Tempo/MPP partner adapter that binds the exact challenge and returns the same AE result, receipt, refund, status, and reconciliation contract without making AE a wallet or protocol owner.

Until all seven pass, the accurate description is: **AE has a strong Locus-shaped transaction kernel, substantial remaining operating-parity work, and an unproved market loop.**

## Ranked AE strengths revealed by the Locus standard

These are source strengths to preserve while closing parity; they are not substitutes for live proof.

### 1. Canonical Operation and supply-authority boundary

AE's strongest foundation is the exact supplier-level Operation plus the rule that imported metadata, an endpoint listing, or a payment challenge is not callable truth. That is the right response to Locus's heterogeneous registered/Parse/MPP catalog and its own distinction between reviewed support contracts and provider pass-through. The [Pro comparison](../.planning/locus-docs/comparison/PRO-EXECUTION-AE-COMPARISON.md) classifies catalog tiers, live-price fail-closed behavior, reviewed contracts, and buyer-safe machine detail as strong.

### 2. Durable effect-aware invocation and recovery

AE does not reduce retry safety to an HTTP code or payment result. Invocation identity, effect generation, outcome uncertainty, reconciliation, refund finality, and stored replay form one application-level state machine. This meets the core of both current Locus status/ledger recovery and the explicit legacy no-dispatch retry rule. The [legacy comparison](../.planning/locus-docs/comparison/LEGACY-AE-COMPARISON.md) classifies durable approval/recovery, retry semantics, refund state, and payment-versus-fulfilment separation as strong.

### 3. Atomic delegated authority and economic exposure

AE already counts settled plus reserved spend, binds holds to durable grants and principals, and prevents replay from mutating economic state twice. This matches Locus's best financial-control invariant: policy admission, counter update, money hold, and reservation must be one decision. The [financial comparison](../.planning/locus-docs/comparison/FINANCIAL-CONTROLS-AE-COMPARISON.md) classifies funded principals, atomic hold, and posted-versus-live exposure as strong. Principal-wide monthly exposure across every credential and route remains a parity gap rather than a reason to discount the kernel.

### 4. Bounded cross-harness market surface

Both systems avoid exposing thousands of supplier endpoints as default MCP tools. AE's search, detail, compare, inspect, invoke, status, and reconcile actions remain bounded and share canonical schemas across machine surfaces. This is the correct operating shape for large supply; parity work belongs inside those actions, not in another surface.

### 5. Product-boundary discipline

AE can reach Locus parity where the products overlap without copying Locus's entire suite. A Locus router, human-task broker, Build deployment, wallet, or checkout service may be one supplier's bounded Operation. AE still does not own the calling agent's project, supplier's internal workflow, wallet custody, PaaS lifecycle, workspace administration, or merchant billing product.

## Ranked parity weaknesses

### 1. The market loop is unproved

This is the highest-order weakness even though it is not a missing API. Neither AE source nor Locus documentation proves AE's required behavior. The repository has a bounded allocation-evidence projector, but no production chain persists unfamiliar gap → search → allocation → call → useful continuation → repeat/switch. A captured payment or completed call is not that evidence.

**Parity exit:** the sixth program test passes in one narrow category and the evidence is queryable without counting idempotent replay as repeat demand.

### 2. Inspect-to-invoke decision continuity is incomplete

AE's internal current-commitment checks are strong, but the caller cannot prove that the exact plan it inspected is the call being released. Locus's executable approval and legacy expected-cost assertion set a clearer operating contract.

**Parity exit:** the first program test passes under adversarial changes to arguments, price, Operation revision, readiness, effects, provider authority, caller grant, and expiry.

### 3. Caller-specific viability and exact Operation least privilege are incomplete

Canonical readiness is necessary but not enough. A principal also needs balance, grant scope, required provider connection, and consequence authority. AE's public search leaves some of this to later admission, while execution grants can cover all admitted Operations.

**Parity exit:** viable search and exact Operation/capability scopes pass the second and third program tests, including a proof that no platform-owned route receives silent priority or authority.

### 4. Production credential and governance lifecycle trails Locus

AE now has owner-bound headless OAuth activation/recovery, issue, expiry, immediate revoke, policy generation, PKCE/device flow, rotating refresh-token families, exact authority preservation, and safe provider-secret rotation. Remaining parity is narrower: owner-selected overlap/cutover for arbitrary non-OAuth harness keys, short/proof-bound high-risk execution identity, and fresh human authorization across all funding/payout/authority enlargement.

**Parity exit:** leaked-old-key, concurrent-rotation, stale-MFA, token-family replay, and immediate-revocation tests all fail closed in production-like conditions.

### 5. Operating recovery is stronger than its delivery and support edges

AE's status/reconcile/refund kernel and fee-inclusive payer funding handoff are mature in source and focused tests. Gaps remain around streamed delivery truth, principal-wide committed exposure, supplier maturity, universal fail-closed mutation audit, fresh human authorization, and durable outbound event delivery for asynchronous paid Operations. These should be closed around the existing Invocation, receipt, and money identities rather than through a general workflow product.

**Parity exit:** the fourth and fifth program tests recover correctly from process loss, delayed payment notification, ambiguous provider release, refund failure, and—where supported—stream disconnect and outbound delivery failure.

### 6. Outcome evidence is too weak to improve allocation

AE exposes ratings, completed-call counts, and latency with sample thresholds, but a rating requires authentication rather than a verified use. Allocation, switching, recency, and useful continuation do not yet influence the market surface. Locus's Laso compatibility signal is narrow and imperfect, but it demonstrates the operating principle: observed outcome history should change the next purchase decision. [Laso](https://docs.paywithlocus.com/features/laso)

**Parity exit:** only verified invocation/use evidence affects reputation or rank; every displayed signal carries sample size, recency, and provenance; usefulness and switching can be distinguished from payment/completion.

## Genuine non-goals outside parity

The following do not become parity work unless `PRODUCT.md` deliberately changes:

- wallet custody, general transfers, on-ramp, recovery-key, or card products;
- Build/PaaS projects, deployments, databases, DNS, domains, or environment management;
- general merchant checkout, embedded billing widgets, auto-recharge, volume-bonus, invoicing, or end-user balance SaaS;
- team invitation, membership, and workspace-administration products beyond the minimum market-principal governance AE needs;
- hidden human-task matching, tendering, or general procurement workflow;
- platform-owned multi-provider planning/fan-out/fusion as AE's market spine; and
- project planning, memory, orchestration, or general agent-runtime ownership.

The reusable Locus standards inside those products—exact price, bounded authority, durable identity, payer-safe handoff, posted-versus-held money truth, payment proof, observed outcomes, and recovery—must still be applied wherever an AE Operation overlaps them.

## Product conclusion

Locus sets a high, practical operating bar: a cold agent can discover a currently authorized capability through a compact surface, inspect cost, spend through bounded credentials, invoke one provider-native contract, recover the result and money state after interruption, and reconcile an append-only economic history. Current Locus Pro strengthens this with agent-native onboarding, MCP OAuth, caller-filtered catalog truth, prepaid settlement, and enterprise controls; legacy Locus contributes especially crisp exact-cost and retry-safety semantics.

AE has already built the hardest parts of a truthful transaction kernel. Its parity task is to finish the caller-visible joins between those parts and prove them live. The ordered work is therefore exact commitment continuity, caller viability, least-privilege authority, production credential governance, delivery/recovery operations, and live economic proof—followed by the market behavior only AE's charter can validate.

Reaching Locus parity does **not** mean becoming Locus's billing, wallet, hosting, team, or orchestration suite. It means that every overlapping Operation-market surface is at least as executable, scoped, recoverable, auditable, and economically truthful as Locus, and that AE can prove the complete loop with real callers and suppliers.
