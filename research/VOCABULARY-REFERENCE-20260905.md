# Vocabulary reference research — 2026-09-05

Status: research recommendation; glossary and migration cutover remain unapproved.
Retrieved: 2026-09-05 (Australia/Perth). Scope: nine first-party pages, plus the Whop documentation index for navigation. No authenticated product testing.

## Finding

**Tool is a defensible familiar name for AE's callable supply unit, but the references do not settle that choice.** Locus and MCP support it directly; Nevermined uses service more broadly and also sells agents. Preserve the proposal's precise distinctions around people, permission, execution and money. Familiar vocabulary is compatible with those distinctions; copying another platform's objects verbatim is not.

This assessment uses the current, uncommitted [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md), [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md) and proposed [UBIQUITOUS_LANGUAGE.md](VOCABULARY-PROPOSAL-20260905.md) from the main checkout, not the older product files in the isolated research branch. These links target the eventual main-checkout copy; the proposal is absent from the research branch's baseline. PRODUCT is active authority; CONTEXT is the accepted glossary. Joel's “tool probably” is tentative. The coordinated whole-platform migration scope is agreed; exact terminology and cutover are not.

## Recommended changes to the proposal for Joel's review

1. **Keep Tool as the working choice, with a scope sentence.** It means the one versioned callable contribution, regardless of HTTP, MCP or x402 access. “MCP tool” remains a protocol term. Service can remain ordinary prose without creating a parent record. Update Service request/comparison consistently if the choice is accepted; their bounded need and comparison facts currently appear in CONTEXT but are absent from the proposal's term tables.
2. **Retain Source, Provider connection, Listing and Publication as distinct meanings pending source mapping.** The references support discovery, connection and publishing language, but none of the reviewed pages proves AE's exact lifecycle decomposition. A glossary definition alone does not require a new table or a one-to-one rename.
3. **Fill whole-platform language gaps without inventing capability.** The proposal is strongest on purchases and money. Add a small surface checklist for team access (team member, role, invitation), integrations (connection, event, webhook), Provider management, support and leaving. Define only terms actually needed by the scoped behaviour; do not turn every navigation label into a domain object. Qualify deactivate/disconnect/remove/close by the affected identity, connection or account.
4. **Preserve commercial attribution.** Customer, User, Account and Agent remain different. Provider, Seller and Payment recipient remain different. Charge, Provider payable and Payout must not inherit Whop's payment/charge equivalence. Receipt, Statement and Tax invoice remain separate, supported by the AE charter; these reference pages do not establish Australian document requirements.
5. **Clarify status and recovery wording.** Result should allow returned error information without asserting usable delivery. Purchase status is the customer's view of the linked purchase, with delivered, failed, adjusted, refunded and unresolved meanings retained. Keep Attempt attached to the existing Call and preserve safe Next action. “Closed” must always identify what closed: support issue, dispute, connection or purchase. No universal successful/complete flag follows from the reference vocabulary.
6. **Keep glossary approval separate from migration mechanics.** After Joel chooses the terms, consolidate accepted definitions in CONTEXT and make the proposal historical. Map UI, docs, source, APIs, events and stored records together, preserving signed, external and historical names where required. The research supports coordinated terminology, not blanket text replacement or a selected cutover strategy.

## Confidence and limits

High confidence in the documented naming distinctions; moderate confidence in Tool as the best AE choice, because reference granularity varies and the commercial definition belongs to AE. All starting URLs were available. Live retrieval is a documentation observation, not runtime verification; the MCP evidence deliberately uses the requested dated revision, not a claim that it is the latest specification.

Coverage remains unavailable in this bounded sample for complete account deletion/export, customer support case lifecycle, detailed Provider withdrawal, invoice/statement issuance and jurisdiction-specific obligations. No claim of absence in the products is made. Those areas need targeted primary evidence when their glossary or implementation decisions arise. No code, schema, deployment or legal determination was performed. Only this research receipt is committed; the older isolated checkout is not implementation evidence for the current main working tree.
