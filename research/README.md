## Historical and local-only material

The [archive index](../docs/archive/README.md) locates superseded plans, reviews
and prior queues. Earlier comparison corpora, reference clones, planning spikes
and papers removed before this consolidation are retained in Git history where
tracked, or in the owner's recovery bundles where local-only. They are not
available as files in a clean checkout; this index does not link to machine-local
paths or claim missing papers are present.

Historical citations labelled local or unavailable provide context only. Pinned
dependency references require the matching installed package. Revalidate dated
vendor, legal, pricing and regulatory research before using it for a new decision.
